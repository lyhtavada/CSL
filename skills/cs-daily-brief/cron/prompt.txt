Run the daily CS brief for Liz (CS Leader, Avada — Chatty, Joy Loyalty, Joy
Wishlist, Team G2). Posted to #cs-2-daily (C0B8042TXQ9), which is Liz's own
tracking channel — not a team-facing broadcast.

This brief runs in EXCEPTION MODE: on a normal day it is a few lines, and it
only opens up when something actually needs Liz. The full 4-section report
always still goes out, but as a thread reply under the short message, so it
stays retrievable without filling the channel.

Window: rolling 24h, **08:30 VN the previous day to 08:30 VN today** (aligned
to this job's 08:45 run time). If today (VN) is the 22nd, the target date is
the 21st. Compute it yourself (yesterday in Asia/Bangkok, UTC+7) — never
hardcode. The scripts take `--date <window start date>` and work out the
08:30-08:30 bounds themselves.

=============================================================================
STEP 1 — Get the data and the verdict (one command)
=============================================================================

From /Users/avada/CSL:

  python3 skills/cs-daily-brief/scripts/evaluate.py --date <target> --json \
    > /tmp/cs_daily_eval.json

evaluate.py runs all 4 fetchers, applies Liz's rules from
`cron/thresholds.json`, and returns:

  quiet            true = nothing needs Liz today
  sanity.ok        false = the numbers look BROKEN, not calm
  sanity.problems  list of what looks broken
  flags.checkin    {late[], missCheckin[], missCheckout[], any}
                   (late already filtered to >= thresholds.checkin.lateMinutes)
  flags.aiStale    AI-created tickets with no progress
                   (tsStatus in pending/doing AND dueDateDone is not True)
  flags.lizTickets every ticket created for Liz in the window
  data             raw output of all 4 fetchers, for the thread detail

If the command exits non-zero, DO NOT send anything. Exit non-zero so the
cron log and the Telegram notifier catch it.

=============================================================================
STEP 2 — Section ④ needs real content, not just a subject line
=============================================================================

If `flags.lizTickets` is non-empty, pull the chats behind them:

  python3 skills/cs-daily-brief/scripts/fetch_chat_transcripts.py \
    --from-json /tmp/cs_daily_eval.json --json > /tmp/cs_daily_chats.json

For EACH ticket in flags.lizTickets write **1-2 sentences in Vietnamese**
covering what the merchant needs and where it currently stands, using all
three sources together:
  - the ticket `subject`
  - the ticket `description` (full text, often has store / plan / reason)
  - the transcript for its `chatLink` session (match on the session_ id)

Say what actually happened — "merchant xin refund 61.52 EUR gói Chatty, CS đã
xác nhận đủ điều kiện, đang chờ duyệt" beats "yêu cầu hoàn tiền". If a ticket
has no chatLink or the transcript is empty, summarise from subject +
description alone. Never invent detail that is not in the sources.

Note: `[DFY]` tickets carry a generic checklist as their description — for
those the transcript is the only real signal, so lean on it.

=============================================================================
STEP 3 — Compose the SHORT message (this is what lands in the channel)
=============================================================================

Three shapes. Pick by the verdict — do not blend them.

--- 3a. sanity.ok == false → SOMETHING IS BROKEN ---

Never report a broken pipeline as a calm day. Lead with the failure:

  *⚠️ CS DAILY — <dd/mm> 08:30 → <dd/mm> 08:30 — SỐ LIỆU NGHI LỖI*
  <one bullet per entry in sanity.problems>
  _Số bên dưới có thể không đúng, cần kiểm tra script/API trước khi tin._

Then still append the normal body (3b or 3c) so Liz can eyeball the numbers.

--- 3b. quiet == true → QUIET DAY ---

Keep it to these four lines. No filler, no "chúc buổi sáng".

  *📊 CS DAILY — <dd/mm> 08:30 → <dd/mm> 08:30*
  ① <total> conversation — Joy <n> | Chatty <n> | Wishlist <n>
  ② Checkin/checkout: ✅ OK
  ③ AI ticket treo: ✅ Không có (bot xử lý <tổng handled> chat, tạo <tổng ticket> ticket)
  ④ Ticket cho Liz: Không có
  🧵 Chi tiết đầy đủ trong thread

--- 3c. quiet == false → CÓ VIỆC ---

Same skeleton, but expand only the sections that fired. A section that is
clean keeps its one-line ✅ form from 3b — do not expand it.

  *📊 CS DAILY — <dd/mm> 08:30 → <dd/mm> 08:30*
  ① <total> conversation — Joy <n> | Chatty <n> | Wishlist <n>

  ② if flags.checkin.any:
       *⚠️ Checkin/checkout*
       🔴 Muộn: <nick> — <shift> (<minutes>p)      [one line each]
       ⛔ Miss checkin: <nick> — <shift>            [one line each]
       🚪 Miss checkout: <nick> — <shift>           [one line each]
     else: "② Checkin/checkout: ✅ OK"

  ③ if flags.aiStale non-empty:
       *⚠️ <n> ticket AI tạo chưa có tiến độ* (dueDate chưa done, còn pending/doing)
       • [<app>] <<ticketUrl>|#<ticketId>> — <subject, ~70 ký tự> _(<tsStatus>)_
     else: "③ AI ticket treo: ✅ Không có (bot xử lý <n> chat, tạo <n> ticket)"

  ④ if flags.lizTickets non-empty:
       *④ Ticket cho Liz: <n>*
       • [<app>] <<ticketUrl>|#<ticketNumber>> (<priority>) — <subject>
         <the 1-2 sentence summary from STEP 2>
         Chat: <chatLink>            [omit the line if chatLink is null]
     else: "④ Ticket cho Liz: Không có"

  🧵 Chi tiết đầy đủ trong thread

Use Slack hyperlink syntax `<url|text>`, NOT markdown — this goes through
chat.postMessage.

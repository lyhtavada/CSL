## 1. Getting Started & Setup

### Q1: How do I set up and train the AI agent for the first time?
**Frequency:** ~114 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Setting up the AI agent takes four steps:

1. **Add training data** — go to **AI agent → Training data**. Your products, collections, discounts and Shopify policy pages (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, FAQ, Contact us, About us) sync automatically. Turn on the sources you want the AI to learn from.
2. **Add your own knowledge** — under **Training data → Custom knowledge** you can add Q&A pairs, website URLs, or files (.JSON, .TXT, .PDF, .CSV, max 2MB each).
3. **Set instructions** — go to **AI agent → Instructions** to set the assistant's name, role, tone, boundaries, and what it should never discuss.
4. **Test, then activate** — use **Test AI** (top right) to check answers before going live, then enable the AI agent.

Finally, make sure the chatbox itself is live: **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, enable the **Live chat** block, and enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

---

### Q2: Can your team set it up for me? (Done-for-you setup)
**Frequency:** ~90 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — our team will configure and train your AI agent, chatbox and FAQs for you, free of charge. To get started we'll ask for:

- The assistant's **name** and **greeting message**
- **Availability** — always on, or only during your working hours
- **Notification preference** — email, web push, or both, and which address
- **Tone of voice** and any topics the AI should never handle
- **What happens when the AI can't answer** — transfer to your inbox, collect the customer's details and follow up by email, or show your contact information
- Whether the AI may look up **order status**

Setup usually completes within about 24 hours, and we'll send you a short walkthrough video when it's done.

### Q3: How do I get the Chatbox / Live Chat widget live on my store?
**Frequency:** ~30 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **Chatbox → General → Turn on**
2. Enable the **Live chat** block under **Blocks**
3. Enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**
4. Customize appearance and colours under **Chatbox → Appearance**
5. Reload your storefront to confirm

If it still doesn't show after that, it's almost always the App Embed toggle being off, or browser cache — check in incognito.

### Q4: How do I set up my FAQs (page vs. Q&A content)?
**Frequency:** ~30 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Two separate things, and merchants often want both:

- **FAQ page** — **FAQs → FAQs page** → toggle on **Display FAQs page**, copy the URL (default `/pages/avada-faqs`) and add it to your navigation menu.
- **Q&A content** — **FAQs → Add new → Add FAQ**, or use **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge → Sync Store Page** to auto-generate ~15 starter Q&As from your policy pages.

If you already have your own FAQ page you're happy with, you can skip Chatty's page and just feed your existing page's URL into Custom knowledge so the AI learns from it.

## 2. AI Agent — Training & Data

### Q5: Where does the AI get its answers from?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Most of it is automatic — Chatty auto-syncs your Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, and store policy pages (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, FAQ, Contact us, About us). Product data refreshes daily; force a refresh via **Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**.
Anything else goes under **Training data → Custom knowledge** as Q&A pairs, scraped URLs, or files (.JSON/.TXT/.PDF/.CSV, max 2MB).

---

### Q6: My discount code isn't being suggested by the AI — why?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Chatty only syncs **static discounts created directly in Shopify Admin**. Discounts created by third-party apps (e.g. Alpha Sale, Bold) or dynamic/auto-generated one-per-customer codes are **not synced** — this is by design, not a bug. Workaround: add the code manually as a Q&A under **Training data → Custom knowledge**, or embed it directly in your message text for Proactive Chat campaigns (no need to select from the synced list).
Also note: discounts limited to a specific customer segment are **inactive by default** in Chatty and must be turned on manually under **Training data → Discounts**.

---

### Q7: My products aren't syncing, or sync is stuck.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Only **published, in-stock** products sync, once daily at 12:00 AM PST. Check:
1. Is the product published and in stock in Shopify?
2. Have you hit your plan's product limit? (Free 200, Basic 500, Pro 8,000, Plus Unlimited)
3. Trigger a manual sync at **Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**.

If sync is stuck "In Progress" with no movement for an extended period, don't edit products while we investigate — send us a screenshot with the timestamp and how long it's been stuck.

---

### Q8: I uploaded a training file/blog URL but the AI isn't using it.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Go to **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge → Add data** — Questions (Q&A), URLs (scraped and indexed), or Files (.JSON/.TXT/.PDF/.CSV, max 2MB). Two things to check: entering just your domain only trains the homepage — add the **individual page URL** for anything specific (e.g. a blog post). And blog URLs don't auto-update when you edit the post; re-add the URL after changes. If a file/URL keeps failing, CSV is the most reliable format for structured Q&A; images and PDFs with tabular content aren't parsed reliably.

## 3. AI Agent — Accuracy & Behaviour

### Q9: The AI gave wrong or outdated information — how do I fix it?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Use **Review sources** — the link under any AI answer showing exactly which training data it used. Fix that specific source, check for conflicting entries elsewhere (a second FAQ or Custom knowledge entry saying something different is the most common cause), then **Reset conversation** and retest — an in-progress chat keeps using the knowledge it loaded at the start.

---

### Q10: How do I move the chat button or fix it overlapping another element?
**Frequency:** ~20 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Go to **Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — drag the dot, or pick a corner anchor and set horizontal/vertical offset (0–45%). No code needed for most cases.

**Known caveat:** Position sometimes only moves the **desktop** button, not the mobile icon. If mobile still overlaps a sticky add-to-cart bar or another app's button after adjusting Position, send us a screenshot/recording, the device, and the page URL — our team applies a targeted mobile CSS fix.

---

### Q11: I changed the welcome message but the old one still shows.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
There are three separate welcome messages:
1. **Chatbox → Chat page → Welcome Message** — used only when the AI is **off**.
2. **AI agent → Settings → AI identity → Welcome message** — used when the AI is **on**, overrides #1.
3. **Settings → Translations → [language]** — each published language keeps its own copy, overriding both #1 and #2 for that language.

Edit the one that matches your setup — if the AI is on, edit #2; if you have translations published, also check #3. A separate **Proactive chat** popup message is edited in the Proactive chat section, not here.

---

### Q12: The AI recommends out-of-stock, unrelated, or wrong products.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The AI works at product level, not live inventory level, and can't filter by exact stock or SKU status. Fixes that help: disable products that should never be recommended under **Training data → Products**, set up **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Product recommendations** to steer specific queries, and put sizing/spec details in the product description so they're part of the synced data.

### Q14: My custom instruction ("don't do X") isn't being followed even though I added it.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Check for a conflict between your **Custom Scenario** and a **built-in Assistant skill** running on the same topic (e.g. the built-in After-sales support skill vs. your own "Return/Refund" scenario) — when both exist, the built-in skill can win out over your custom instruction. Use **Review sources** to see which one actually answered, then either edit the conflicting scenario or turn off the overlapping built-in skill so only one governs that topic.

Send us the product URLs involved and a screen recording — our engineering team is investigating and there's no confirmed fix yet.

## 5. FAQs Page & Blocks

### Q21: "The FAQs page URL has already been taken" / my FAQ page won't activate.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
This happens when a Shopify page already exists at that path — usually a leftover from a previous install or a deleted-and-recreated page. Check **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Pages** for a page using the same handle, remove or rename it, then re-enable the FAQ page in Chatty. If it persists, send us your store URL and we'll change the URL from our side.

### Q22: Only some of my FAQs are showing on the FAQ page/block.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Check that the FAQs you expect are set to **Published** and, if you're using a FAQ block with "Featured" filtering, that they're marked **Featured**. Imported FAQs land as drafts and need publishing before they're visible.

In general, always review Q&A content generated via **Sync Store Page** or **Generate with AI** before publishing — treat auto-generated drafts the same as anything else you'd proofread before it goes live to customers.

### Q25: I can't log in to the web or mobile app.

## 7. Channels

### Q26: I can't connect a new WhatsApp number — everything looks correct but it still won't connect.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Beyond the standard requirements (Meta Business Manager Admin role, a number not already active on the personal WhatsApp app or another API provider), Meta also caps the **number of phone numbers per Business Account**. If you've confirmed everything else and it still won't connect, check **WhatsApp Manager** in Meta Business Suite for an existing unused number slot to free up, or request a higher number-capacity limit directly from Meta.

---

### Q27: My WhatsApp number is stuck on "Pending" or shows the wrong number connected.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Pending typically means Meta verification is still in progress (24–48 hours). Confirm: Meta Business Verification is complete, the number isn't registered with another WhatsApp API provider, and **two-factor authentication** is enabled in WhatsApp Business settings — this last one is the most common cause of a number stuck in Pending. If Embedded Signup offered a different/newly-created number instead of yours, double-check the correct Business Portfolio is selected during signup; if it still can't be chosen, send us the WABA ID, phone number, Business Manager ID, and a screenshot.

---

### Q28: How do I get the AI replying on Instagram and Messenger?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Connect the channel first (**Settings → Channels**), then separately enable AI on that channel at **AI agent → Settings → AI Channels** — this step requires **Pro or Plus**. The AI only responds to new incoming messages after being enabled; it can't retroactively pick up older conversations.

---

### Q29: My email channel won't verify / emails aren't reaching Chatty.
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Set up under **Settings → Channels → Email**, selecting the correct provider (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, Others). **Outlook** is a known Cloudflare-compatibility issue for automatic forwarding — workarounds are an intermediate Gmail forwarding step, switching hosting to Google Workspace/Zoho, or using web push/mobile app notifications instead. If your organization's IT manages your email, auto-forwarding to Chatty may need IT approval.

## 8. Translation

### Q30: How do I get the chatbox and FAQs showing in my language?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Add the language in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages**, then **Settings → Translation → Add language** in Chatty. Review the auto-translated draft, **Save**, then click the settings icon → **Publish → Confirm** — saving alone doesn't push it live. Language allowances by plan: Free 1, Basic 2, Pro 9, Plus unlimited; auto-translate isn't available on every plan.

---

### Q31: The AI replies in the wrong language.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Add an instruction under **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours**: "Always respond in the same language as the customer." If it still slips into English for some replies, the trigger is usually **untranslated Conversation Starters** rather than the AI itself — translate them under **Settings → Translations → [language]**.

## 9. Notifications & Mobile

### Q32: How do I get notified when a customer messages me?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → Notifications** — enable **Email** triggers and click **Subscribe** under **Web push**, then **Send test** to confirm. Each browser/device needs its own subscription. Notification settings are **per member account**, not global — if the whole team should stop getting a certain alert, every member needs to turn it off in their own settings.

---

### Q33: I'm not getting push notifications on my phone.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check Do Not Disturb/Focus mode first — this is the most common cause. Then confirm notification permission for Chatty in your phone's system settings, and that you're subscribed under **Settings → Notifications → Web push**. If the installed app misbehaves, delete and reinstall it.

## 11. Edge Cases & Compliance

### Q37: Accessibility (ADA/WCAG) issues in the chat widget — ESC key, ARIA labels, focus order.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new — **currently unresolved**

**Standard answer:**
These are genuine gaps our engineering team is reviewing (keyboard dismissal, missing ARIA labels on interactive elements, focus management, heading hierarchy). We don't have a published accessibility statement or fix timeline yet — if you're working against a compliance deadline, let us know so we can flag the urgency internally, and send a follow-up as soon as we have concrete progress to share.

---

### Q38: Does the `avada-chatty` cookie need consent, and what about the cart-related cookies from Proactive Chat?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new — **currently unresolved**

**Standard answer:**
The core `avada-chatty/session` cookie is essential to running the chat and is generally exempt from consent requirements under GDPR/ePrivacy. Separately, some Proactive Chat cart features (e.g. Cart booster, Remove items from cart) can set additional cart-related cookies that may need consent depending on your jurisdiction. If that's a concern, disabling the specific cart-related Proactive Chat campaigns is the current workaround while we build a proper consent gate — let us know if you still see those cookies being set after disabling, so we can investigate further.

---

### Q39: Shopify Markets — product/checkout links point to my primary domain instead of the active market domain.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new (enterprise/Shopify Plus) — **currently unresolved**

**Standard answer:**
This is a confirmed, already-tracked bug affecting multi-market Shopify Plus stores, where AI-generated links don't respect the visitor's active market domain. Our engineering team has this logged and a fix in progress — send us your store URL and example affected links if you're seeing this, so we can add your case to the tracked issue and notify you when it ships.

---

### Q40: Can I access Chatty admin from mainland China?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Chatty is hosted on Google Cloud, which is blocked in mainland China, so the admin app won't load there without a VPN. Your storefront widget and customers are unaffected — this only impacts store-admin access.

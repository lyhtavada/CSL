> Compiled from 708 Crisp chat sessions of the `app_chatty,app_faqs` segment over 7 days. Due to the volume, sessions were mined via 8 parallel session batches; 5 of 8 batches (438 sessions, ~62% of the week) were read in full detail for exact question/answer extraction, and all 708 sessions were scanned by keyword to sanity-check topic volume/ranking. ## 1. Getting Started & Setup

### Q1: How do I set up and train the AI agent for the first time?
**Frequency:** ~84 sessions (detailed sample) — largest topic by far
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **Add training data** — **AI agent → Training data → Manage**. Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, and your Shopify policy pages (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, FAQ, Contact us, About us) sync automatically; toggle on what you want the AI to use.
2. **Add your own knowledge** — **Training data → Custom knowledge** for Q&A pairs, scraped URLs, or files.
3. **Set instructions** — **AI agent → Instructions → Manage** to set tone, role, boundaries, and behaviours.
4. **Set identity** — **AI agent → Settings** for name, avatar, and welcome message.
5. **Test, then activate** — **Test AI → Test now** before going live (testing never counts against your AI conversation quota or sends real notifications).

Finally, make sure the chatbox itself is live: **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, enable the **Live chat** block, and enable Chatty under **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

---

### Q2: Can your team set it up for me? (Done-for-you setup)
**Frequency:** ~15+ sessions explicitly requested/completed (offered proactively in nearly every Q1 conversation)
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — free of charge. The team asks for: assistant name/greeting, working hours (24/7 or custom), notification email/channel, brand tone and off-limit topics, what happens when AI can't answer (transfer to inbox / collect info & follow up by email / show contact methods), and whether AI may look up order status. Setup usually completes in **12–24 hours**, with a Loom walkthrough video when done.

---

### Q3: How do I get Live Chat / the Chatbox live on my store?
**Frequency:** ~13 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **Chatbox → General → Turn on**
2. Enable the **Live chat** block
3. Enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**
4. Configure the **Pre-chat form** (guest email/name/phone, or anonymous chat) under **Chatbox → Chat page**
5. Reload the storefront to confirm

If it still doesn't show: App Embed toggle is almost always the cause, followed by browser cache — check in incognito.

---

### Q4: Can I migrate/replicate my Chatty setup to a new Shopify store or domain?
**Frequency:** 2 sessions (new pattern this week)
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No automatic migration exists. Conversation history, contacts, and AI usage data are **bound to the specific store**, even under the same account email — a new store install starts blank. Only your **AI Instructions, Custom Scenarios, and FAQ/Q&A content** can be manually copied over (there is no export/import button for this — copy the text yourself, or ask the team to replicate it as a one-off setup service, similar to a DFY request, ~24h turnaround). Chatbox appearance and channel connections must be reconfigured from scratch on the new store. Note: any loyalty/wishlist data (e.g. Joy) is a separate app and is not affected by or transferable through Chatty.

## 2. AI Agent — Training & Data

### Q5: My products aren't syncing, or the "learned products" count looks wrong.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Only **published, active, in-stock** products sync, once daily (auto) or on demand via **Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**. If the count looks low: check the **Status filter isn't hiding inactive products** and toggle any that should be included to Active — inactive/draft products sync but are excluded from what the AI actually uses. If you've hit your plan's product cap (Free 200, Basic 500, Pro 8,000, Plus 20,000 — see pricing table in Section 9), large catalogs above the cap need manual selection of which products to prioritize, or an upgrade.

---

### Q6: The AI gave wrong info — how do I correct or add training data?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Click **Review Sources** under any AI answer (in Test AI or a live conversation) to see exactly which training data it used, then fix that entry directly. Click **Add Data** to add a missing Q&A pair. Two common root causes to check beyond the obvious "wrong FAQ text": (1) a **Scenario** under Training data still containing outdated/conflicting instructions that override the FAQ, and (2) the **product page description itself** still holding old info (the AI reads it directly) — update the page, then re-sync. After any fix, use **Reset conversation** on the test/live chat — an in-progress chat keeps using the knowledge it loaded at the start.

---

### Q7: My discount code isn't being offered by the AI, or the AI shouldn't offer discounts at all.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Chatty only syncs **static discounts created directly in Shopify Admin** — third-party-app or dynamic/one-per-customer codes aren't synced; add those manually as a Q&A. Segment-restricted discounts are **inactive by default** in the Discount list and must be manually activated — but turning on the whole Discounts source can also expose unrelated influencer-only codes, so activate only the specific codes you want live rather than the whole source. To stop the AI from proactively offering/confirming discount codes, add a boundary instruction under **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → Behaviours**, e.g. "Never proactively offer or confirm discount codes — only respond if the customer explicitly asks."

## 3. AI Agent — Accuracy & Behaviour

### Q8: The AI recommended, added, or checked out the wrong product on its own.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new pattern (related to prior weeks' "wrong product" reports, but specifically the auto-add-to-cart behavior)

**Standard answer:**
By design, the AI can infer purchase intent from a message (e.g. "I want to buy the X") and add that item to cart automatically — this is **not currently configurable to trigger only on an explicit "add to cart" request**; the team has tried restricting it and reverted, so don't promise a fix. If it added the **wrong** product: usually either (a) the intended product wasn't an active/purchasable catalog item, so the AI substituted the closest match — activate the real product — or (b) a genuine bug in matching, which the team has fixed on a case-by-case basis when reported with a screen recording. If the merchant wants to reduce false positives generally, add an instruction under **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours**: "Only add items to cart when the customer explicitly says 'add to cart' / 'I want to buy' / 'place order' — a price question mentioning 'buy' is not a purchase request."

---

### Q9: How do I turn off the AI entirely and have my team handle all chats?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Go to **AI agent** (left menu) → **Deactivate** (top right). All conversations then go straight to the team Inbox with no AI involvement and no AI traces shown to customers. Make sure **Chatbox → General → Live chat** block is still on so the widget itself keeps working.

---

### Q10: How do I customize how the AI responds (tone, length, specific policies, keyword-triggered behavior)?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
General tone/behaviour: **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → General instructions → Behaviours** (e.g. "keep responses to 2–3 sentences," "always respond in the customer's language," "never discuss X").
For a specific situation triggered by keywords (e.g. a cancellation flow, a regional shipping exception, a specific policy): **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → Assistant skills → Custom scenarios → + Add scenario** — name it, set trigger keywords (include synonyms), and write instructions (max 1000 characters).

---

### Q11: How do I control when/where the AI hands a conversation to a human?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Configure at **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Customer support skills → Human handover**. Auto triggers (each toggleable except "customer asks to talk to a person," which is always on): AI cannot answer (2 consecutive low-confidence replies), customer repeats the question, negative sentiment. You can also add custom **Intent rules** to route specific topics (e.g. cancellations, legal requests) straight to a human without an AI reply first. Destination options: transfer to a human in the Chatty inbox (recommended), collect info & follow up by email, or show contact methods — each with its own offline-behavior setting.

## 4. Chatbox & Widget

### Q12: The chat button overlaps another element or needs repositioning.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — drag the dot or pick a corner anchor and set horizontal/vertical offset (0–45%). No code needed for most cases.

**Known caveat (confirmed again this week):** Position sometimes only moves the **desktop** button, not the mobile icon. For mobile-only overlap with a sticky add-to-cart bar or another app's button, send a screenshot/recording, the device, and the page URL — the team applies a targeted CSS fix (not self-serve). For offsets beyond 45% or pixel-precise placement, same escalation path.

---

### Q13: The chat widget doesn't show up at all.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. Confirm **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds** has Chatty toggled on and saved.
2. Confirm **Chatbox → General → Blocks → Live chat** is on.
3. Reload the storefront (check incognito to rule out cache).

If both are confirmed on and it's still not showing, this needs theme-level investigation — collect the merchant's collaborator code (Shopify Admin → Settings → Users → Security) and escalate. Root causes seen: a theme bug blocking chatbox render, "Chat Focus mode" being on (jumps straight to chat window, looks like the widget is missing), and malformed theme.liquid HTML conflicting with another script — all fixable but need direct theme access to diagnose.

---

### Q14: How do I customize the AI's name, avatar, welcome message, or colours?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
- **AI name** — **AI agent → Settings → Name**. If it still shows the old name, also check **Instructions → General instructions → Role** and any published **Translations** (each language keeps its own copy).
- **Welcome message** — **AI agent → Settings → AI identity → Welcome message** (supports a `{{customer_name}}` variable). Note: if the store has a published translation for the customer's language, the **Settings → Translations → [language] → AI Agent** version overrides this and must be edited there too.
- **Avatar** — **AI agent → Settings → Avatar**. Presets are free on every plan; a custom uploaded image/logo requires a paid plan.
- **Colours / button style** — **Chatbox → Appearance**.

## 5. FAQ & Knowledge Base Management

### Q15: I added/edited an FAQ but it's not showing on the storefront.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Two conditions both need to be true: the FAQ's **Status is Published**, and it's marked **Featured Question** to appear on the first page of the chatbox (non-featured FAQs still exist but require the customer to browse further). Separately, the **FAQs Block** (for showing FAQs on a storefront page like a product page) needs both a page-display condition set in the block AND the block added via the theme editor — this two-step requirement is confirmed as-is, not a bug, though it's been logged as UX feedback for a possible future simplification.

---

### Q16: I edited an FAQ but the chatbox's quick-question buttons still show the old version.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**FAQs** (Training data → FAQs → Manage) and **Conversation Starters** (Chatbox → Chat page) are two separate lists that do **not** sync automatically — updating a FAQ answer does not update a matching Conversation Starter, and there's no reverse sync either. If you use Conversation Starters as customer-facing quick replies, update the wording in both places manually.

## 6. Live Chat, Inbox & Team

### Q17: I can't log in, or my activation link/password isn't working.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Chatty uses a **separate password from Shopify**. Open Chatty from Shopify Admin and use the **Web app activation** prompt (Dashboard, or **Settings → Team**) to set a password, then sign in at **app.chatty.net** or the mobile app. Use **Forgot password** with your store owner email if you can't find the prompt; check spam for the reset link. If your Chatty login email doesn't match your current Shopify store email (e.g. you changed it), the two don't auto-sync — contact support to update the Chatty-side login email manually.

---

### Q18: How do I add/manage team members and their access?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → Team → Invite member** — enter name and email (email can't be changed later once invited) and send the invite. Seats by plan: Free 1 (admin only), Basic 5 total, Pro 10 total, Plus unlimited. All invited members are agents by default and see the full inbox equally — there's currently no role-based/restricted access to limit what a specific member can see (logged as a product feature request, no ETA). Only the Owner/Admin can change plan, billing, or global AI settings. Chatty currently supports **only 1 Admin per workspace** — admin rights can be transferred but not shared among multiple accounts simultaneously.

## 7. Channels

### Q19: My WhatsApp number won't connect, is stuck Pending, or the wrong WABA shows up.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Beyond the standard requirements (Meta Business Manager **Admin** role — Employee role isn't enough — and a number not already active on the personal WhatsApp app or another API provider), also check: **Meta Business Verification** is complete, **2FA** is enabled in WhatsApp Business settings, and the correct **Business Portfolio** was selected during Embedded Signup (a mismatch is the most common cause of the wrong/old WABA appearing). If every item checks out and it's still stuck, the cause may be **Meta's own cap on phone numbers per Business Account** — this is a Meta platform limit, not something Chatty can bypass; the merchant needs to free up a slot or request a higher cap directly from Meta. If still unresolved, send the WABA ID, phone number, Business Manager ID, and a screenshot.

---

### Q20: My email channel won't verify, or Outlook/Hotmail forwarding doesn't work.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Set up under **Settings → Channels → Email**. **Outlook/Hotmail** has a known Cloudflare-compatibility issue with automatic forwarding — this can't be fixed on Chatty's side. Workarounds: an intermediate Gmail/Google Workspace/Zoho forwarding hop, or relying on email/web-push/mobile-app notifications instead. If your organization's IT manages email, auto-forwarding may need IT approval for the `xxxx.forward@chatty.email` address. If an address has been stuck "Unverified" for a long time, removing and re-adding it often clears it. Note the forwarding domain changed from `@chattyemail.com` to `@chatty.email` — always copy the current address from **View Instructions** rather than reusing an old saved one.

---

### Q21: How do I get the AI to reply automatically on WhatsApp, Messenger, or Instagram?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Connecting a channel and enabling AI on it are two separate steps. Connect under **Settings → Channels**, then go to **AI agent → Settings → AI Channels** and toggle the specific channel on. This requires **Pro or Plus** — on Free/Basic, AI auto-reply only works on the website chat widget (connecting the channel itself and replying manually is available on every plan, including Free). The AI only replies to **new** messages after being enabled — it cannot respond to messages received before the connection or before AI was turned on for that channel.

## 8. Notifications

### Q22: I'm not receiving notifications for new messages.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → Notifications** — if Web push shows "Not subscribe," click **Subscribe**, allow the browser prompt, then confirm the relevant triggers (Web/Email) are on. Notification settings are **per member account, not global** — an admin turning everything off only silences their own account; every teammate must do the same individually. Each browser/device needs its own subscription. On **macOS + Chrome**, push notification **sound** specifically is a known Chrome/macOS limitation (visual banner works, sound doesn't) — Safari (Add to Dock) resolves it. On iOS, Safari doesn't support browser push at all — the **Chatty mobile app** ("Chatty AI Sale Assistant" on the App Store/Google Play) is the only reliable option there.

---

### Q23: I'm getting too many, or duplicate, email notifications.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No trigger fires on every single AI reply within a conversation — check which specific trigger is actually enabled under **Settings → Notifications → Email Notifications**. The most common cause of "one email per message" complaints is actually **Conversations assigned to AI assistant**, which fires **once per conversation** (when AI first takes it over), not per reply — recount before assuming a bug. If duplicates persist, check whether the **same address** is set as both the connected email **Channel** (which syncs the raw customer email) and the **Notification** recipient (Chatty's own alert) — those are two separate mechanisms that can both hit the same inbox.

## 9. Translation

### Q24: How do I get the chatbox/FAQs/AI replies showing in my language instead of English?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Add the language in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages** first, then **Settings → Translation → Add language** in Chatty. Click **Auto-translate** (or edit manually), **Save**, then the settings icon → **Publish → Confirm** — saving alone doesn't push it live. For the AI's own replies, add an instruction under **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours**: "Always respond in the same language as the customer" (the AI auto-detects and replies per-customer regardless of the chatbox UI language by default).

**Two separate limits, often confused:**

| Plan | Languages the chatbox can publish | Of those, auto-translated by Chatty |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | 0 — manual only |
| Basic | 3 | 2 |
| Pro | 10 | 9 |
| Plus | Unlimited | Unlimited |

A store can always add a language and write it by hand; the second column is how many Chatty fills in automatically.

### Q26: What exactly counts as 1 AI conversation, and what happens when I hit my limit?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
A conversation counts only if **all three** are true: the AI gives at least one valid reply generated from training data (a welcome message or a preset quick-reply click does **not** count), the customer engages (sends a message, or replies to a proactive outreach), and the conversation ends. The same customer chatting again **within 24 hours** still counts as the same conversation, not a new one — this is tracked per customer, per store. Usage resets on your **billing-cycle date**, not the calendar month — check **Subscription → View details** for your exact reset date and current usage; email alerts fire at 80% and 100%.

**When you hit the limit:** Free pauses AI auto-replies until the next cycle (no charge; human agents can still handle chats manually). Basic/Pro/Plus bill extra conversations at **$0.40 each** — set a spending cap under **Subscription → View details → Change limit** to control this.

### Q27: I need more than my plan's limit for products/custom answers/URLs & files — can it be extended?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Sometimes, as a one-tier, one-off accommodation (e.g. a Free store can be bumped to Basic-level limits). AI **conversation** limits and AI **scenario** limits are handled separately and need product-team approval — training-data limits (products, custom answers, URLs & files) can usually be extended directly by support. Contact us with your store URL, current plan, and which specific limit you've hit.

## 11. Order Tracking & Integrations

### Q28: My order-tracking widget isn't working, or I want to use a custom tracking link/17TRACK.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Enable the **Order tracking** block under **Chatbox → General**, then configure the method at **Settings → Integrations → Order tracking → Manage**: Default carrier tracking, a **Custom tracking link** (works on any plan, including Free — just redirects to your own tracking page URL), or **17TRACK** (free, embeds a tracking page in-chat; requires the merchant's own 17track.net account and Security Key, not the Shopify "17TRACK" app). The **AI real-time order-tracking lookup** (customer asks "where's my order" in chat and the AI pulls live status) requires **Basic plan or higher**. If tracking shows generic status when a custom link is set, confirm the order actually has a tracking number attached yet.

---

### Q29: Does Chatty have a public API, and what's included?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — API key generation and webhooks are available on **Pro and Plus only** (Free/Basic don't have API access). Sender identification in the API: check `isAutomated:true` first (AI), then `isAdmin:true` with `isAutomated:false` (human agent), otherwise it's the customer. The same API key works across both the GraphQL and REST endpoints. Role-based/restricted API keys (to limit what a given integration or contractor can see) are **not currently supported** — logged as a feature request.

## 1. AI Setup & Training

### Q1: Can your team set up / train my AI agent for me (done-for-you)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~90+ sessions
**Standard answer:** This is the dominant intent of the week — most fresh installs get the proactive nudge "Your AI is live but hasn't been trained yet." Merchants can self-serve via **AI agent → Training data → Manage** (add Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, FAQs, Custom knowledge) → **AI agent → Instructions → Manage** (behaviour/tone) → **Test AI → Test now** before going live. Alternatively the team sets it up free of charge. To collect the DFY brief, agents ask: (1) assistant name + greeting, (2) where chat messages/history should be sent (email), (3) always-on vs only-when-offline + active hours, (4) tone, (5) any topics to exclude, (6) how human handover should route (transfer to inbox / collect email / show contact), (7) whether the AI may look up order status. The team then syncs Products/Collections/FAQs/Discounts/Pages, generates ~15 suggested Q&As, adds instructions, enables Re-engage After Resolution + Follow-up Messages, and sends a Loom walkthrough for review.

### Q2: I have to type everything manually to train the AI — is there a faster way? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:** No need to type it all. Add FAQs/Q&A pairs, paste **URLs**, or upload files (PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON) as data sources under **AI agent → Training data** and Chatty trains from those. Existing Shopify Inbox Q&A pairs can be reused. If the merchant prefers, the team imports it all during the free DFY setup.

### Q3: How do I add a custom Q&A / sale campaign / discount answer or a scripted flow? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Standard answer:** Add Q&A pairs in **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge / FAQs**. For scripted behavior (e.g. a price-negotiation script that always offers "35% off on Auto-Ship, free shipping over $100, loyalty points"), use **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → Assistant skills → Custom scenarios** with trigger keywords. Scenario limits are plan-gated: Free/Basic 5, Pro 15, Plus unlimited. Extensions beyond the plan need PM approval — collect the request, don't promise.

### Q4: How do I test the AI before it goes live / verify it does a good job? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:** Use **AI agent → Test AI → Test now** to preview responses without affecting live chat. After launch, review past AI conversations in the **Inbox**; on any AI reply click **Review Sources** to see which training source it pulled from. The proactive weekly recap ("your chat handled X conversations, AI resolved Y% on its own") also surfaces performance — agents offer to fine-tune anything that sounded off.

## 2. AI Wrong / Missing Answers

### Q5: The AI told a customer a wrong fact (e.g. wrong store address). How do I fix it? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions
**Standard answer:** Collect the conversation ID (or customer name/email), the exact wrong answer, and the correct value. Guide the merchant to open the reply → **Review Sources**: no source shown = hallucination (the training data lacked that fact); source shown = the source itself is wrong/outdated. Fix: add the correct info as a Q&A in **AI agent → Training data → FAQs/Custom knowledge** and make sure that block is **Enabled** (a disabled Q&A is invisible to the AI — a real cause this week). The team can also add a temporary Q&A immediately and re-test. Escalate to CS with the collected details.

### Q6: The AI says "I don't have that information" even though I added/synced the data. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:** Most common cause: the **training-source toggle for that topic is OFF**. Syncing and enabling are two separate steps. Go to **AI agent → Training data → [matching tab]** (Discounts / Products / Collections / Markets / FAQs / Custom knowledge), confirm the "Enable your AI agent to answer questions about [topic]" toggle is ON, and check the "X items learned" badge is > 0 and Last updated isn't N/A. Click **Sync now**, then re-test in **Test AI**. If toggle ON + count > 0 and it still fails, check **Review Sources** and escalate.

### Q7: The AI over-promised (refund/return) or made up a discount that doesn't exist. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:** Collect conversation ID, what the AI promised, and whether that discount ever existed (Chatty auto-syncs real promotions, so it may be pulling a genuine-but-outdated one). Check **Review Sources**. As an immediate guardrail, add a **Custom scenario** with keywords "refund/return/discount/promo/% off" and an instruction like "Do not confirm or offer any discounts; direct customers to [support contact]." If a removed promo still shows, re-sync under **Training data → Discounts/Products**. Escalate to CS.

### Q8: The AI's FAQ / "Track Order" / "Chat with us" links are broken or point to Chatty instead of my store. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:** Broken product links: confirm the primary domain in **Shopify → Settings → Domains**, then **re-sync products** under **AI agent → Training data → Products**. If product links still fall back to the `*.myshopify.com` URL, that's a known issue on our side — collect the conversation ID + example link and escalate. For FAQ/quick-reply deep-links redirecting to Chatty's own page instead of the merchant's target (seen this week), collect the desired destination URL(s) and escalate to the technical team to correct the link mapping; log as product feedback.

## 3. AI Sync / Products

### Q9: Does the AI know my products so it can recommend them? How do I enable that? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:** Yes — enable **Products** under **AI agent → Training data → Products** (toggle each product/collection you want used). For tailored recommendations (best-sellers, new arrivals, offers) configure **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Product recommendations**. Product limits are plan-gated: Free 200, Basic 500, Pro 8,000, Plus unlimited. Stores with 10,000+ products need manual backend config beyond the 5,000-product AI-recommendation limit — book a demo call.

### Q10: Deleted / unpublished products still appear, or the catalog looks stale. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** Force a resync: **AI agent → Training data → Products → Sync products**, wait for it to finish, then verify in **Test AI**. If a deleted product still appears, check whether it was manually added to a FAQ or Scenario and remove it there. If it persists after sync + cleanup, escalate with the product name/URL, a Test AI screenshot, and the sync timestamp (possible caching issue).

## 4. Human Handover & Live Chat

### Q11: How do I set it so the AI hands off to a human when it can't answer / how do I see human requests? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Standard answer:** Configure at **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → Assistant skills → Customer support skills → Human handover**. Under "When should AI hand off," the **AI cannot answer** auto-trigger hands off after 2 consecutive low-confidence replies ("Customer asks to talk to a person" is always on). Under "Where does the customer go," choose **Transfer to human in Chatty inbox** (agent joins the same chat), **Collect info & follow up by email** (AI gathers details + emails your team, with a response-time promise), or **Show contact methods**. Transferred chats land in your **Chatty Inbox** as new/transferred conversations, plus a notification if enabled.

### Q12: I want AI to only collect the customer's email and have us follow up (no live agent). 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:** In **Human handover → "Where does the customer go?"**, select **Collect info & follow up by email**. Confirm the support email in AI settings and set a **Response time promise** shown to the customer (e.g. "We'll reply within 24h"). No live agents needed — the AI emails a summary to your team.

### Q13: I want to use Chatty as human live chat only (no AI). / How do I enable live chat? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:** To run human-only, **Deactivate** the AI agent (AI agent → Deactivate, top right) — all chats then go straight to the inbox with no AI traces. To enable live chat: **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, then **Blocks → Live chat** on, **Edit** the pre-chat form (email required; name/phone optional; "Chat as anonymous" possible), **Save**, then preview and click **Chat now**. Set availability in **Settings → General → Chat availability**.

### Q14: A human joined but the AI keeps replying / AI won't resume after takeover. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** Once a human takes over, AI is intentionally paused for that contact — to re-enable it, open the conversation in **Inbox → Assign to AI**. If the AI keeps posting *over* a joined agent, that's a recognized defect: make sure the agent formally **Joins/assigns** the chat (not just types); if it still talks over the agent, collect the conversation ID + which agent joined + whether it was assigned and escalate to dev.

### Q15: Where do I set my chat open/close times (active hours)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions
**Standard answer:** **Settings → General → Chat availability → Manage** — choose 24/7 or custom hours per day, plus breaks and holidays. For a holiday offline message, **Settings → Online Hours → Holidays**; note there is no per-holiday custom message (one general offline message applies) — log a feature request if the merchant needs distinct messages.

## 5. Notifications

### Q16: Notifications aren't on / where do I turn them on, and web-push vs email? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~40+ sessions
**Standard answer:** The onboarding nudge "notifications aren't on yet" drove heavy volume. Go to **Settings → Notifications**: under **Web push**, if it shows "Not subscribe," click **Subscribe** then **Manage** and allow browser notifications; toggle Web and/or Email per trigger. During DFY, agents ask which channel to use (web push, email, or both) and, for email, which address. Recommend not enabling email + desktop + mobile all at once for the same trigger to avoid duplicate alerts.

### Q17: I stopped getting desktop sound/banner alerts (or emails stopped). 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:** Confirm **Settings → Notifications → Web push** is Subscribed and the trigger toggles are on; the browser must allow notifications for `app.chatty.net` and the OS must not be in Do Not Disturb/Silent. If still nothing, clear cache/cookies, restart, or update the `firebase-messaging-sw.js` service worker (DevTools → Application → Service Workers). For email: check spam, verify the notification address, and note corporate mail may block automated emails. Meanwhile use email or the Chatty mobile app as a fallback; escalate with browser+version, OS, and a permissions screenshot if unresolved.

## 6. Widget / Chatbox Appearance

### Q18: How do I set up / turn on the chatbox so it shows on my store? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~20 sessions
**Standard answer:** **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, enable the blocks you want (**Live chat**, etc.), then enable **App Embed** in Shopify: **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App Embeds** → toggle Chatty ON and Save. Reload the storefront to preview. If it still doesn't appear after both are on, follow the collaborator-access flow and escalate to TS with the store URL.

### Q19: Can I move the widget (left/right, higher/lower) or resize the bubble? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:** Use the built-in control at **Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — drag the dot or pick a corner **Anchor** and adjust **Horizontal/Vertical offset** (0–45%). Button size is Small/Medium/Large in the same tab. For percentage resizing or moving beyond the 45% range, the team can add Custom CSS via **Chatbox → Advanced → Custom CSS**.

### Q20: Can the WhatsApp launcher icon be the same size / aligned with the chat bubble? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** The Chatty chat bubble is sized/positioned under **Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button** (size + Position offsets). Matching it to a separate WhatsApp icon that sits beside it is a fine-tune the team can do — this week agents manually resized the widget smaller and repositioned it to align with the merchant's WhatsApp icon. Share a screenshot so support can match the sizes and alignment.

### Q21: Can I stop the chatbox from auto-opening / popping up on every page load? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** Turn off **Chatbox → General → Chat focus mode** (only active when Live chat is on). If it still opens on load, check **Proactive Chat** for a campaign set to trigger immediately and disable it. Note: by design the widget loads *after* the page finishes loading (to protect page speed) — that's expected, not the auto-open behavior. If the merchant can reproduce an unwanted auto-open, ask for a screen recording and escalate.

### Q22: How do I change the assistant name, greeting/welcome message, logo/avatar? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:** Chatbox greeting: **Chatbox → Chat page → Welcome Message**. AI greeting/name/avatar: **AI agent → Settings → AI identity → Welcome message / name** (supports `{{customer_name}}`). Header logo/heading: **Chatbox → General → Header**. Avatar can be Store branding or a Team member profile under **Chatbox → Chat page → Chat avatar**.

## 7. Inbox / Bugs

### Q23: "Account not activated. Please open Chatty app from Shopify admin to activate." 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** The web app just needs one-time activation. Open Chatty from **Shopify Admin → Apps → Chatty**, go to **Settings → Team** (or the "Web app activation" banner on the Dashboard), click **Activate**, and create a password using your Shopify default email. Then log in at **app.chatty.net**.

### Q24: Microphone/voice input says "no permission" — how do I enable it? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** This is a browser permission, not a Chatty setting. **Chrome/Edge:** click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar → set **Microphone** to Allow → refresh (Edge also: ⋯ → Cookies and site permissions → Microphone → allow the site). **Safari:** Safari → Settings for This Website → Microphone → Allow → refresh. If it's still blocked, have the merchant record a short clip of the prompt so support can check.

## 8. Channels (Email / WhatsApp / IG / FB)

### Q25: What do I need to connect WhatsApp, and can the AI reply on WhatsApp? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Standard answer:** Requirements: a **Meta Business Manager** with **Business Admin** role; a valid WhatsApp number **not registered on the personal WhatsApp app** (verify via SMS or call); valid business info; and correct permissions granted to "AVADA group company limited" during OAuth. A number tied to another API provider (Twilio, 360dialog) or to the personal/Business app must be migrated first via Meta Business Manager. Once connected, enable AI for the WhatsApp channel in **AI agent → Settings** — but WhatsApp AI auto-reply requires **Pro or Plus**; on Basic, AI auto-reply works only on the website widget. Chatty can only reply within existing WhatsApp threads (no outbound-first messages).

### Q26: My WhatsApp number shows "Pending" — why? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:** Pending means Meta verification is still processing — usually 24–48 hours. Confirm the WhatsApp Business account is verified in Meta Business Manager, the number isn't on another API provider, 2FA is enabled, and the connecting user is Business Admin. If still Pending beyond 48h, escalate with the last 4 digits, Meta Business Manager ID, and a screenshot.

### Q27: Will my WhatsApp profile photo show, and can I change it in Chatty? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** Customers still see your WhatsApp profile photo on your side. When you view the WhatsApp thread inside the Chatty inbox it shows the Chatty avatar, but the customer always sees your WhatsApp avatar. WhatsApp photos are controlled entirely by WhatsApp/Meta — change them in **Meta Business Manager / WhatsApp Manager**, not in Chatty.

### Q28: My email channel won't verify / emails aren't forwarding to Chatty. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** Verify the forwarding address is exactly the current one from **Settings → Channels → [email] → View Instructions** (the domain changed from `@chattyemail.com` to `@chatty.email` — old addresses no longer verify) and check spam for the verification email. Outlook/Hotmail has a known Cloudflare incompatibility — route through Gmail/Workspace/Zoho instead. Connect the **real mailbox, not an alias**. Corporate/IT-managed mail may block auto-forwarding — the merchant's IT must allow it. Escalate with provider + forwarding address if it persists.

### Q29: How do I change or remove the auto/greeting messages on Instagram & Facebook, and why are IG/FB replies delayed or missing? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** Only messages sent **after** Chatty is connected sync; older ones stay in Meta. Ensure the connecting user has "Manage and access Page messages" and all OAuth permissions were granted. For AI on Instagram, enable the per-channel toggle in **AI agent → Settings**. Chatty supports IG text + image only (no story replies, reels, voice, stickers). Persistent delays or replies made directly in Meta not syncing back are known limitations — flag with the page name/ID to investigate.

Page: **FAQs → FAQs page** → toggle **Display FAQs page** (default URL `/pages/avada-faqs`), customize Header/Contact us/Custom CSS, and ensure **App Embed** is on. If the page looks blank in the Shopify editor, that's by design — the content renders via the Chatty app; check the live `/pages/avada-faqs` URL.

For WhatsApp specifically, a normal contact page won't open the WhatsApp widget — a proper WhatsApp deep-link is needed. Logged as product feedback while the fix is applied.

## 10. Translation

### Q32: How do I make the AI answer in the customer's language / add a language (e.g. Spanish, Arabic)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:** AI language is a behaviour, not a UI string — add under **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → General instructions → Behaviours**: *"Always respond in the same language as the customer."* Re-test in **Test AI**. To add a chatbox language: first add it in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages**, then **Settings → Translation → Add language**. On the Free plan only one language is available — after adding it, use the settings icon → **Change default** to switch. Paid plans can auto-translate; Free must translate manually.

### Q33: I translated my FAQ but the storefront still shows the old language. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** Translation is two steps — after editing, click **Save**, then the **settings icon → Publish → Confirm**. New FAQ content isn't auto-translated (a banner flags "Outdated") — re-translate then Save + Publish. If a third-party translation app (Translate & Adapt, T Lab) is overriding Chatty's FAQ meta content, remove the overridden rows or escalate to TS.

## 11. Order Tracking

### Q34: Can the AI check order status, and how do I enable order tracking? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:** Enable **Chatbox → General → Blocks → Order tracking**, then **Settings → Integrations → Order tracking → Manage** and pick a method: **Default** (redirects to the carrier page — DHL/FedEx/UPS), **Custom** (your own tracking URL), or **17TRACK** (embeds on-site). The AI can also look up status in chat once enabled — customers give an order number + email/phone or a tracking number. For unsupported carriers only basic status (Confirmed / On its way / Delivered) shows; to hide a foreign carrier or force your own page, use Custom tracking or escalate for a scenario tweak.

## 13. Pricing / Billing

### Q36: What does "500 AI conversations" mean — 500 messages or 500 people? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:** It means 500 complete **conversations**, not individual messages. One conversation can contain many back-and-forth messages with the same customer session and still counts as one until it ends (resolved/expired). Monthly AI conversation caps by plan: Free 50 (resets monthly, no extra), Basic 100, Pro 500, Plus 1,000 — paid plans allow extra conversations at **$0.40 each**.

### Q37: How much does Chatty cost / which plan do I need? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:** **Free $0** (50 AI conv/mo, 200 products, 100 custom answers, 20 URLs/files, 1 team member, 90-day history, 1 email channel). **Basic $19.99/mo** ($16.99 annual) (100 AI conv + $0.40/extra, 500 products, 1,000 custom answers, 50 URLs/files, 5 members, 12-month history, unlimited email channels, full chatbox customization). **Pro $68.99/mo** ($58.99 annual) (500 AI conv + $0.40/extra, 8,000 products, unlimited custom answers, 500 URLs/files, 10 members, unlimited history, full AI channels + analytics). **Plus $199/mo** ($169.99 annual) (1,000 AI conv + $0.40/extra, unlimited products/custom answers/members, dedicated AI consultant). **Enterprise**: custom. All paid plans include a 7-day free trial; 30-day money-back guarantee with a Shopify charge screenshot. WhatsApp AI auto-reply and full analytics need Pro+.

### Q38: The AI says it's out of conversations even though I have quota / how do I control overage? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** Check usage at **Chatty → Subscription → View details** (resets on the billing-cycle date, not calendar month; alerts at 80% and 100%). Free pauses AI when 50/mo is hit until the next cycle — conversations can still be handled manually in Inbox. Paid overage is $0.40/conversation; cap it via **Subscription → View details → Change limit**. If quota clearly isn't exhausted but AI still stops, verify the AI toggle is on and training data is active, then escalate.

### Q39: How do I cancel / downgrade / get a refund? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** Downgrade before the next billing cycle at **Chatty → Subscription → View plans → Free plan** (data/settings are kept). For a refund, collect a Shopify charge screenshot (app name, cycle, amount) — refunds fall under the 30-day money-back guarantee and require CS Leader approval; CS is not authorized to approve refunds directly. Refunds take 7–10 business days.

## 14. Mobile App

### Q40: Is there a Chatty mobile app / can I use this on my phone? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:** Chatty is a **PWA**, not a native App Store/Google Play app. Install from **Inbox → Download Chatty app** or **Settings → Notifications → Chatty mobile app → Download app**. iPhone: use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: browser menu → Install app. Enable notifications in device settings after installing. (Don't tell merchants to update via the App Store — there's no native app.)

## 15. Edge Cases

### Q41: A shopper is trying to buy products through the support widget. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** These sessions are shoppers who reached the Chatty *support* widget rather than the merchant's storefront chat. Agents politely clarify the chat is for the Chatty app itself and redirect them to the store — no action needed on the merchant side. Noise, not a support issue.

### Q42: Marketing / newsletter emails arriving as support tickets. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~5+ sessions
**Standard answer:** Several inbound "sessions" this week were merchant marketing/newsletter blasts (sale announcements, unsubscribe footers) landing in the support inbox via a connected/forwarding email. Agents recognize these as non-support and reply that it looks like a marketing email, inviting a real request if needed. No configuration change required; if volume is high, the merchant can review which forwarding rules point at their Chatty email channel.

### Q43: Can Chatty control my Shopify theme footer / Instagram footer icon link? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** The Shopify theme editor is not managed by Chatty, so footer links (e.g. a footer Instagram icon pointing to the wrong handle) must be fixed in the **theme editor → Footer → Instagram icon**. Chatty can't change theme content on the merchant's behalf. For in-widget links (FAQ quick-replies, contact links) the team can help; for theme elements, direct the merchant to the theme editor.

### Q44: How do I remove the Shopify Inbox popup that competes with Chatty? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:** That popup is Shopify Inbox, not Chatty. Go to **Shopify Admin → Settings → Apps and sales channels**, find Shopify Inbox, and disable/remove it there. Its existing Q&A pairs can be reused as Chatty AI training data.

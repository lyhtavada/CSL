## 1. Getting Started & Setup

### Q1: How do I set up and train the AI agent for the first time?
**Frequency:** ~128 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Setting up the AI agent takes four steps:

1. **Add training data** — go to **AI agent → Training data**. Your products, collections, discounts and Shopify policy pages (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, FAQ, Contact us, About us) sync automatically. Turn on the sources you want the AI to learn from.
2. **Add your own knowledge** — under **Training data → Custom knowledge** you can add Q&A pairs, website URLs, or files (.JSON, .TXT, .PDF, .CSV, max 2MB each).
3. **Set instructions** — go to **AI agent → Instructions** to set the assistant's name, role, tone, boundaries, and what it should never discuss.
4. **Test, then activate** — use **Test AI** (top right) to check answers before going live, then enable the AI agent.

Finally, make sure the chatbox itself is live: **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, enable the **Live chat** block, and enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

---

### Q2: Can your team set it up for me? (Done-for-you setup)
**Frequency:** ~57 sessions (across AI agent / FAQs / Chatbox / Live chat DFY requests)
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — our team will configure and train your AI agent, chatbox and FAQs for you, free of charge. To get started we'll ask for:

- The assistant's **name** and **greeting message**
- **Availability** — always on, or only during your working hours
- **Notification preference** — email, web push, or both, and which address
- **Tone of voice** and any topics the AI should never handle
- **What happens when the AI can't answer** — transfer to your inbox, collect the customer's details and follow up by email, or show your contact information
- Whether the AI may look up **order status**

Setup usually completes within about 24 hours, and we'll send you a short walkthrough video when it's done.

### Q3: How do I get the Chatbox / Live Chat widget live on my store?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **Chatbox → General → Turn on**
2. Enable the **Live chat** block under **Blocks**
3. Enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**
4. Customize appearance and colours under **Chatbox → Appearance**
5. Reload your storefront to confirm

If it still doesn't show after that, it's almost always the App Embed toggle being off, or browser cache — check in incognito.

### Q4: How do I set up my FAQs (page vs. Q&A content), including importing an existing FAQ list?
**Frequency:** ~28 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Two separate things, and merchants often want both:

- **FAQ page** — **FAQs → FAQs page** → toggle on **Display FAQs page**, copy the URL (default `/pages/avada-faqs`) and add it to your navigation menu.
- **Q&A content** — **FAQs → Add new → Add FAQ**, or use **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge → Sync Store Page** to auto-generate starter Q&As from your policy pages.

If you already have your own FAQ page (in any language), you can point Custom knowledge at that page's URL instead of re-typing everything, and the AI will learn from it directly. For a large existing list, pasting a CSV or a structured document under Custom knowledge → Files is more reliable than pasting free-form text into chat.

## 2. AI Agent — Training & Data

### Q5: Where does the AI get its answers from, and how do I add more products/metafields to it?
**Frequency:** ~14 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Most of it is automatic — Chatty auto-syncs your Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, and store policy pages. Product data refreshes daily; force a refresh via **Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**. Anything else goes under **Training data → Custom knowledge** as Q&A pairs, scraped URLs, or files.

For product **metafields** (e.g. an "inspired-by-brand" or size-chart field you want the AI to search against), there's a selectable metafield limit per plan. If you hit the "metafield selection limit" error, contact us with your store URL and which fields you need — we can review a one-off increase case by case (see Q36).

---

### Q6: My discount code isn't being suggested by the AI, or the AI is giving out discount codes I don't want it to.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Chatty only syncs **static discounts created directly in Shopify Admin**. Discounts created by third-party apps or dynamic/auto-generated one-per-customer codes are **not synced** — this is by design, not a bug. Workaround: add the code manually as a Q&A under **Training data → Custom knowledge**.

If instead you want the AI to **stop** offering/validating discount codes on its own (e.g. always defer discount questions to your team), add an explicit boundary under **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours**, e.g. "Never confirm, apply, or validate discount codes — always say a team member will help with this."

---

### Q7: My products aren't syncing, or the AI says a product is out of stock/unavailable when it isn't (or vice versa).
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Only **published, in-stock** products sync, once daily. Check:
1. Is the product published and in stock in Shopify?
2. Have you hit your plan's product limit? (Free 200, Basic 500, Pro 8,000, Plus Unlimited)
3. Trigger a manual sync at **Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**.

The AI works at the product level, not live per-variant inventory, so it can occasionally call a product "out of stock" when only one variant is, or the reverse. If you see a specific wrong case, use **Review sources** on that answer and send it to us — this is currently one of the more common accuracy complaints and is being tracked by product.

## 3. AI Agent — Accuracy & Behaviour

### Q8: The AI gave wrong, outdated, or made-up information — how do I fix it?
**Frequency:** ~8 explicit reports + 23 sessions with inline `[AI Feedback]` correction tags
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Use **Review sources** — the link under any AI answer showing exactly which training data it used. Fix that specific source, check for conflicting entries elsewhere (a second FAQ or Custom knowledge entry saying something different is the most common cause), then **Reset conversation** and retest — an in-progress chat keeps using the knowledge it loaded at the start.

If the AI stated a fact (a spec, a policy, a price) that doesn't exist anywhere in your training data at all — rather than just an outdated version of a real fact — please send us the exact conversation link; this is treated as a higher-priority accuracy report and logged for the product team.

---

### Q9: The AI recommends the wrong product, mentions products/categories I don't sell, or carries over context from a different product page.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Fixes that help: disable products that should never be recommended under **Training data → Products**, set up **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Product recommendations** to steer specific queries, and put sizing/spec details in the product description so they're part of the synced data. If the AI references categories or products you've never sold, or shows a very different product count than what's actually synced (e.g. mentioning far more products than you have), that usually points to stale or orphaned training data — send us your store URL and we'll check what's actually indexed.

The **AI Product Assistant embedded on product pages** carrying over the previous product's info instead of the one currently being viewed is a known, still-open issue — send product URLs + a screen recording if you hit this.

## 4. Chatbox & Widget

### Q10: The chat button/icon doesn't show up, overlaps something else, or needs repositioning/resizing.
**Frequency:** ~46 sessions (display, position, sizing, mobile-specific issues combined)
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Go to **Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — drag the dot, or pick a corner anchor and set horizontal/vertical offset (0–45%). No code needed for most cases. Icon size can also be adjusted here.

**Known caveat:** Position sometimes only moves the **desktop** button, not the mobile icon, and some mobile-specific overlap with a sticky add-to-cart bar or another app's button needs a manual CSS fix — send a screenshot/recording, the device, and the page URL and our team will apply a targeted fix.

If the widget doesn't appear at all: confirm **Chatbox → General** is on, the **Live chat** block is enabled, and Chatty's App Embed is turned on in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds** — then check in incognito to rule out cache.

### Q11: How do I hide the widget on certain pages, or only show it on specific pages?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings → Display pages** — choose all pages, only specific pages, or hide on specific pages. Enter the page path only (e.g. `/pages/contact-us`), not the full URL.

### Q12: How do I change the assistant's name, avatar, colours, or the random customer nicknames it generates?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
- **Name** — **AI agent → Settings → Name**. If the AI still introduces itself with the old name, also check **Instructions → General instructions → Role**, and any published **Translations** (each language keeps its own copy of intro text).
- **Avatar** — **AI agent → Settings → Avatar**. Presets are free on every plan; uploading your own image/logo requires a paid plan and a file under the app's size limit (JPG/PNG, max 200KB).
- **Colours** — **Chatbox → Appearance**.
- **Anonymous customer nicknames** (e.g. auto-generated names like "Warm Song") — this comes from an anonymous-visitor labeling setting; contact us with your store URL if you want it turned off entirely rather than just changing the current name, as there isn't yet a single self-serve toggle for disabling the feature outright — we'll apply it from our side and log the request as a feature preference.

## 5. Live Chat & Inbox

### Q13: A conversation is stuck in my inbox and won't resolve/disappear ("ghost" conversation), or unread counts/assignment look wrong.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check the **Resolved** tab first and clear any inbox filters — most "phantom" conversations are sitting there, not gone. If a specific conversation truly won't resolve or remains visible after resolving, send us your store URL and the conversation ID/link and we'll remove or fix it from our side.

Separately, some merchants this week reported that conversations are no longer **auto-assigning** to the first agent who replies (previously automatic), and that some conversations only appear in the "All" view after being manually clicked/resolved. Both are confirmed bugs our team is actively investigating — if you see either, send your store URL and we'll add your case to the tracked issue.

---

### Q14: I can't log in to the web or mobile app / messages don't load in the app.
**Frequency:** ~14 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The Chatty app uses a **separate password** from Shopify. Open Chatty from Shopify admin and use the **Web app activation** prompt (Dashboard, or **Settings → Team**) to set a password, then sign in at **app.chatty.net** or the Chatty mobile app. If you can't find the prompt, use **Forgot password** with your store owner email and check spam for the reset link.

If you're logged in but conversations never finish loading (stuck spinner) on mobile, try force-closing and reopening the app, and confirm you're on the latest app version — this has been reported intermittently and our team is tracking device/OS-specific cases; send your device model and OS version if reinstalling doesn't help.

## 6. Channels

### Q15: I can't connect a new WhatsApp number, it's stuck on "Pending," or the wrong number/WABA shows up.
**Frequency:** ~16 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Beyond the standard requirements (Meta Business Manager Admin role, a number not already active on the personal WhatsApp app or another API provider), also check: **Meta Business Verification** is complete, the number isn't registered with another provider, and **two-factor authentication** is enabled in WhatsApp Business settings — this is the most common cause of a number stuck in Pending. If Embedded Signup shows a different/older Business Account (WABA) than expected, confirm the correct Business Portfolio is selected during signup. If it still won't resolve, send us the WABA ID, phone number, Business Manager ID, and a screenshot and we'll investigate directly.

---

### Q16: My email channel won't verify, or notification/customer-reply emails aren't reaching Chatty (or reaching my customers correctly).
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Set up under **Settings → Channels → Email**, selecting the correct provider (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, Others). **Outlook** has a known Cloudflare-compatibility issue for automatic forwarding — workarounds are an intermediate Gmail forwarding step, switching hosting to Google Workspace/Zoho, or relying on web push/mobile app notifications instead. If your organization's IT manages your email, auto-forwarding to Chatty may need IT approval, and shared mailboxes sometimes need the forwarding rule set up differently than a personal inbox — if the standard steps don't verify, send us the exact forwarding address shown and we'll check on our side.

## 7. Notifications

### Q17: How do I get notified (email/web push) when a customer messages me, and why am I not receiving them?
**Frequency:** ~25 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → Notifications** — enable **Email** triggers and click **Subscribe** under **Web push**, then **Send test** to confirm. Each browser/device needs its own subscription, and notification settings are **per member account**, not global.

If test emails arrive but real customer-message notifications don't: check your working-hours/business-hours settings aren't limiting which days notifications fire, check spam, and confirm the notification email address is correct. If it worked before and stopped without any settings change on your side, send us your store URL and the approximate time it stopped — this has been an intermittent bug for some merchants and our team can check server-side delivery logs.

## 8. Translation

### Q18: How do I get the chatbox/FAQs/AI replies showing in my language instead of English?
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Add the language in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages**, then **Settings → Translation → Add language** in Chatty. Review the auto-translated draft, **Save**, then click the settings icon → **Publish → Confirm** — saving alone doesn't push it live. Language allowances by plan: Free 1, Basic 2, Pro 9, Plus unlimited.

For the AI's own replies (not just static UI text), also add an instruction under **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours**: "Always respond in the same language as the customer." If some elements still show in the default language after publishing, they're usually untranslated **Conversation Starters** or FAQ entries rather than a bug — translate those individually under **Settings → Translations**.

### Q20: I'm being charged even though I'm not using the app / was charged twice / want a refund.
**Frequency:** ~17 sessions (billing/refund/subscription-related combined)
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Billing runs through your Shopify subscription, so charges continue until the app is uninstalled or the plan is explicitly downgraded/cancelled — simply not using the app doesn't pause billing. If you believe you were charged in error (e.g. charged on a store where the app was fully disabled, or charged twice), send us your store URL/domain and the account email, and our team will check your billing history and process a refund if the charge was indeed a mistake — this is handled case by case, not automatically.

Sometimes, as a one-off accommodation. Contact us with your store URL, current plan, and which specific limit you've hit (products, metafields, custom knowledge files, languages, etc.). AI conversation limits are handled separately from training-data limits and usually mean an upgrade is the right long-term fix rather than a manual extension — tell us your use case and we'll follow up with the best option.

## 10. Order Tracking & Post-purchase

### Q22: The order-tracking link redirects to a generic tracking site instead of my own tracking page / custom carrier link.
**Frequency:** ~16 sessions (tracking-related, includes some general "where's my order" questions)
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Order tracking uses the tracking info Shopify has for the order; if you've added a **custom tracking link** under order tracking settings, confirm it's saved and that the order actually has a carrier/tracking number attached — orders without tracking numbers yet will still show the default status page. If your custom link is set correctly but customers are still redirected elsewhere (e.g. a 17Track-style default page), send us the order example and we'll check the routing on our side, since this has come up as an inconsistent behavior for a few merchants this window.

## 11. Team & Access

### Q23: How do I add/remove staff members, or change what a member-level role can access?
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → Team → Invite member** to add someone by email; remove or change their role from the same page. Member-level roles have access to the inbox but not full account settings/billing — only accounts with the Owner/Admin role can change plan, billing, or global AI settings. If you're moving ownership to a new person entirely (e.g. agency handover), let us know and we can help transfer the primary admin account cleanly rather than just adding a second member.

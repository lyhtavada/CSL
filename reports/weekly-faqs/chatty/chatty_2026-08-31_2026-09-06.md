## 1. Getting Started / AI Agent Setup

### Q1: How do I set up the AI agent from scratch?
**Frequency:** ~130 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **AI agent → Training data → Manage** — add training data (Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, FAQs, Custom knowledge). Products/Collections/Discounts/FAQs sync automatically from the store once a day (00:00 UTC, or force with **Sync products**); store pages (Shipping/Return/Privacy/Terms/FAQ/Contact/About) sync automatically whenever edited.
2. **AI agent → Instructions → Manage** — set tone/response behavior (Role field, up to ~250 characters; Behaviours field, up to ~1,000 characters). A **Generate with AI** button can draft a starting point based on business type.
3. **AI agent → Settings** — set AI name, avatar, welcome message, availability.
4. **Test AI → Test now** — test before going live, then **AI agent → Turn on** to activate.

**Important:** the four store data sources (Products, Collections, Discounts, FAQs) sync automatically, but the AI is **not allowed to use any of them until the corresponding toggle is turned on** under AI agent → Training data — syncing and "permission to use" are two separate steps. This is the single most common root cause behind "the AI doesn't know my products" or "it makes things up."

Chatty also offers a free "done-for-you" (DFY) setup: the bot runs a guided intake (what the assistant should take off your plate, current support coverage, 24/7 vs. only-when-away, name/greeting, notification channel + email, brand/tone, boundaries, human-handover routing, order-lookup preference) and hands off to the CS team, who complete training and send a Loom/Jam walkthrough video for review — usually within 12–24 hours. This setup does not consume any AI conversation quota.

### Q2: Does the AI automatically crawl or scan my whole website for training?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, clarified

**Standard answer:**
No. Chatty auto-syncs only these specific Shopify store pages with no manual action needed: Shipping policy, Return policy, Privacy policy, Terms of service, FAQ, Contact us, and About us. **Blog posts and any other page are NOT auto-synced** — add each blog post or page individually as a URL under **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge → URLs**. Entering just the domain (not a specific page path) only trains the AI on the homepage. There is no whole-site crawl.

### Q3: What file types and size limits apply to Custom Knowledge uploads?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, clarified

**Standard answer:**
Supported formats: **PDF, CSV, TXT, JSON** — **max 2MB per file**. CSV is best for structured/tabular data (e.g. a product-spec matrix); PDF is better for unstructured content like policy text, but images and PDFs whose content is in tables/scans are not reliably parsed — re-enter that data as text or CSV instead. New entries default to **Off/Inactive** status and must be manually activated before the AI will use them. A topic showing "Failed" after CSV import is usually because it was left "Published but not Active" — set it Active and re-upload.

## 2. AI / Copilot Training

### Q4: Why did my AI give a wrong or fabricated answer (invented policy, wrong price, wrong contact info)?
**Frequency:** ~25 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
When the AI has no matching FAQ/training-data entry, it can guess rather than say "I don't know." Fix: use **"Review Sources"** under the AI's reply (visible in Inbox, not on the storefront chat) to see exactly which FAQ/data source it pulled from, then correct or add a Q&A at **AI agent → Training data → Manage → FAQs** (or **Custom knowledge**) with the accurate fact stated explicitly. Re-test via **Test AI → Test now**.

**Important gotcha confirmed this week:** if the same outdated fact exists in **both** a Training Data entry **and** a published FAQ, removing it from only one place is not enough — the AI can still retrieve it from the other. Check and correct both.

Two extra failure patterns confirmed previously and still recurring:
- **Multi-topic question crowds out an answer:** the AI's retrieval only pulls its top-3 most relevant training entries per message.
- **AI claims an action was taken that wasn't** (e.g. "I've forwarded this to our team") when Human Handover is actually set to "Show contact methods," not a live transfer.

### Q5: My products/discounts/collections aren't syncing into AI training data, or the AI says "no info" on catalog questions.
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
First confirm the relevant source toggle is switched **on** under **AI agent → Training data** — Products, Collections, Discounts, and FAQs all sync automatically in the background, but the AI cannot use any of them until the toggle for that source is turned on. Only **published, in-stock** products sync; a product can be Active in Shopify but still be **Disabled** in Chatty's own per-product toggle (**Training data → Products**), which blocks it independently of Shopify's status. Product data refreshes once daily (00:00 UTC) — use **Sync products** to force an immediate refresh. Subscription products are not supported for AI training.

### Q6: Can the AI answer questions using detailed product-page content (specs, size charts, metafields) that isn't in the basic product description?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new detail

**Standard answer:**
No — the AI only reads Shopify's core synced product fields (title, description, price, variants, inventory) plus anything you separately add. Detailed spec data stored in **metafields** is not read by default; it must be manually synced via **Shopify Admin → Products → More actions → Manage metafields → Synchronize now**, and the Products source must also be turned on under AI agent → Training data. For information that lives only as an image (a size chart graphic, a diagram) — the AI cannot extract text from images, so that content must be re-entered as a Q&A or Custom Knowledge text entry instead.

### Q7: Why does the "Commonly Asked" / suggested-question buttons in AI Mode keep changing, or not match what I configured?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Two separate things are often confused:
1. **Featured FAQs** only control the **Legacy chatbox** home screen — they have no effect on AI Mode.
2. **AI Mode's "Commonly Asked"** buttons are configured at **Chatbox → Chatbox mode → AI Mode → General → Commonly Asked** (this is the same underlying list as Legacy's **Conversation starters** — editing one updates the other).

**Confirmed this week:** the **Test AI** panel (used for internal testing) pulls **random** suggested questions from your FAQ/training data purely for testing purposes — it does **not** reflect your actual configured Commonly Asked questions, even when the backend setting is verified correct. To see the real, customer-facing behavior, check the **live storefront widget** directly, not the Test AI panel.

### Q8: Why is my AI responding in the wrong language, or not consistently in the customer's language?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The AI already replies in whatever language the customer writes in, by default. If it's inconsistent: (1) add "Always respond in the same language the customer uses" under **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → General instructions → Behaviours** — this is required in addition to, not instead of, setting a default language under Settings → Translation; setting only the Translation default is not enough on its own; (2) a confirmed, since-fixed bug caused the AI to fall back to English specifically when a customer sent an **image with no accompanying text** — this has been fixed to retain the most recent conversation language instead, though some edge cases (an off-topic image with truly no context) can still be imperfect; (3) static/default content (Conversation Starters, Human Handover message, FAQs shown as buttons) is translated separately at **Settings → Translation**, since only live AI replies auto-adapt.

### Q9: Can the AI recommend products based on a specific attribute or occasion (guided selling)?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — **AI agent → Skills → Shopping → Smart recommendations**. Two built-in system recommendations are always available: **Best sellers** and **New arrivals**, each with an "Activate smart syncing" toggle to auto-populate from sales/recency, or manual product selection. You can also create **Custom recommendations** (e.g. "Sale promotion," "Special occasions," or your own) with search terms/triggers linked to specific products or collections. Avoid modifier words like "great" or "best" as triggers — these collide with Shopify's native Best-Selling logic.

## 3. Chatbox / Widget Setup & Display

### Q10: How do I reposition the chat bubble, and can it be different on mobile vs desktop?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Chatbox → Chatbox mode → Legacy chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — pick an anchor corner or drag the position dot, then fine-tune with the Horizontal/Vertical offset sliders (0–45%). This control only exists in **Legacy chatbox** mode, not AI Mode. There is **no separate desktop-vs-mobile position setting** — the same Position/offset applies to both; a genuinely different mobile-only vs desktop-only position requires custom CSS from the support team (available on request). Precise pixel offsets and button size beyond the built-in Small/Medium/Large presets also require custom CSS.

### Q11: Chatbox not appearing at all on my storefront.
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Checklist, in order: (1) **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds** → toggle Chatty **on**, Save — this must be re-checked after *any* theme switch, since a new theme starts with the embed off again; (2) **Chatbox → General → Display chatbox on your store** toggle on; (3) if on Legacy chatbox mode, **Legacy chatbox → General → Blocks → Live chat** toggle on; (4) check **Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings → Display pages** for a "Hide on specific pages" rule that may be excluding the page in question; (5) check **Chatbox → General → Chat Focus mode** is not enabled — when on, it hides the launcher bubble entirely, not just the home screen (a real, non-obvious cause of "the chat is just gone"); (6) for headless Shopify setups, the App Embed toggle alone doesn't work — the Chatty script must be added manually under **Website Integration**. If desktop works but mobile doesn't, it's usually a cache issue — try incognito/another device before escalating.

### Q12: Can I hide the chatbox on specific pages, deep-link into it, or add a contact button near another element?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
To hide/show on specific URLs: **Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings → Display pages**, add the URL or a pattern like `/products/*`. To deep-link: append `#chatty-chat` (Live Chat), `#chatty-home`, `#chatty-tracking`, or `#chatty-help` to any store URL — deep links can only open a specific chatbox tab, they cannot pre-fill a message into the input box. To add a **Contact button** near another storefront element (e.g. "Add to bag"): first enable at least one contact method under **Chatbox → General → Blocks → Contact**, then add the Contact button block via the theme editor; a plain hyperlink alternative is the deep-link URL above added as a theme text/HTML block.

### Q13: Product recommendation card format — can I change it to lead with the image, or customize its button?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The AI's in-chat product recommendation card (image + text + a fixed **"View Product"** button) is a **fixed system format** — it cannot be reordered to show the image alone first, and its button is not customizable. This is different from a **Proactive Chat campaign's** product-recommendation message, where the CTA button (e.g. "Add to Cart" or "Learn More") **is** configurable under that campaign's Message content → Chat message. Always confirm which one the merchant means before answering.

## 4. Notifications

### Q14: I'm not getting notified when a new chat/message comes in (web push or mobile app).
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check **Settings → Notifications**: enable **Web push** (click Subscribe, then allow notifications in the browser for **app.chatty.net specifically** — a very common mix-up is allowing notifications for the store's own domain or the Shopify admin domain instead) and/or **Email**, and toggle on the triggers needed. For the **mobile app**, push notifications are registered **per device** — if only one of several logged-in devices is "subscribed," only that device will receive alerts; check Settings → Notifications on each device individually. There is currently **no SMS notification option** — only Email, Web push, and the mobile app.

### Q15: How do I set holiday/away hours, and what should I check if the holiday message doesn't fully revert afterward?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new (root cause)

**Standard answer:**
Set up under **Settings → Working hours → Holiday** → toggle on and add the date range. A bug that had the AI ignore holiday status entirely (quoting normal hours and still offering human transfer) was fixed on 24/08 — a fresh report of that specific symptom after that date should be treated as new.

**Confirmed this week — a different, related trap:** if a holiday closure message was added manually as a **Custom Knowledge / training data entry** (rather than only via the Holiday toggle) to give the AI extra context during the closure, turning the Holiday toggle back off does **not** automatically remove that entry — the AI can keep citing the old holiday message from its knowledge base even though the toggle shows off. If a merchant reports the AI still mentioning a holiday that's already ended, check **AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge** for a leftover manually-added entry and remove/update it directly, in addition to confirming the Holiday toggle itself.

## 5. Proactive Chat

### Q16: Can I add a clickable CTA button or an auto-copy option to the discount code shown in a Proactive Chat popup?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not for the **discount block** (used by the Cart booster and Subscribe newsletter templates) — it only displays the coupon code as plain text plus a free-text usage-instruction field; there is no built-in CTA button and no one-click "copy code" for that block. Workaround: put a direct link with the discount pre-applied inside the **Usage Instruction** text field, e.g. `Shop now → https://yourstore.com/collections/all?discount=CODE` — clicking it applies the code automatically at checkout, no button or manual copy needed. (This is different from the **Product recommendation** template, whose CTA button — e.g. "Add to Cart"/"Learn More" — is configurable.)

## 6. Team / Access

### Q17: How do I change my login email, activate my account, or log out of a device?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
First-time activation: open Chatty from **Shopify Admin → Apps**, go to **Settings → Team**, click the activation banner, set a password (login email defaults to the Shopify account email), then log in at app.chatty.net. Password resets: use **Forgot password**. Team members need an invite first (**Settings → Team**), then create their own password from the invite email. To log out a specific device/session: on the standalone web app (app.chatty.net — not the embedded Shopify admin view, which has no logout option), click the account avatar (top right) → **Manage Account → Login Devices** → find the session → Log out.

## 7. Integrations

### 7a. WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram

### Q18: How do I connect WhatsApp, and why does it say my account "doesn't support partner sharing" or won't link?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended

**Standard answer:**
Requires a Meta Business Manager account with **Admin role** on both the Facebook Page and the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) — a Manager/Employee role is not sufficient. **WhatsApp Coexistence is NOT supported** — a number still linked to the personal WhatsApp/WhatsApp Business mobile app must be fully migrated to a WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API) number first: back up chat history → delete the mobile app account entirely (Settings → Account → Delete my account) → create/verify the WABA in Meta Business Suite → OTP re-verify. **This migration removes the number's existing chat history** — this is a Meta-side restriction, not a Chatty limitation, and there's no workaround while a number is still pending migration. A pending Meta "Display Name" review will also block outbound messages until approved; click **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp → Sync status** once approved to pull the update.

### Q19: Can't send/receive images or files over WhatsApp/Messenger. AI auto-reply on WhatsApp/Messenger/Instagram not working?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Meta blocks any third-party app from sending files once **24 hours** have passed since the conversation started (platform policy). Connecting a channel (WhatsApp/Messenger/Instagram) is free on any plan, but **AI auto-reply on those channels requires Pro plan or above**, enabled at **AI agent → Settings → AI Channels**. If a channel was just connected (especially a newly-added WhatsApp number), the AI Channels toggle needs to be (re-)confirmed on for that connection — it doesn't always carry over automatically. AI availability (Always vs. Only when offline) is a single **global** setting applied to all channels — it cannot currently be set differently per channel (e.g. WhatsApp only).

### 7b. Email

### Q20: Email forwarding into the Chatty Inbox isn't working, or a submission/notification isn't being delivered.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended

**Standard answer:**
Chatty gives a unique forwarding address; add a forwarding rule in your provider pointing to it, send a test email, then reload Email settings to verify. Known causes by provider:
- **Outlook/Hotmail:** a known Cloudflare/Microsoft mail incompatibility — bounces typically come back from `mailer-daemon@amazonses.com`. Workarounds: use an intermediate Gmail mailbox that forwards to Chatty, switch to Google Workspace/Zoho Mail, or rely on email/mobile-app notifications instead of forwarding.
- **Microsoft 365 (confirmed this week):** M365's own **outbound anti-spam policy** can block automatic external forwarding entirely, returning the specific error `550 5.7.520 Access denied, Your organization does not allow external forwarding`. Fix (the merchant's M365 admin must do this): **security.microsoft.com → Email & collaboration → Policies & rules → Threat policies → Anti-spam → Outbound spam filter policy → Edit protection settings → set "Automatic forwarding rules" to "On – Forwarding is enabled"** (optionally also enable "Keep a copy of forwarded messages").
- **Domain's own SPF/DMARC blocking Chatty's sending server** (`amazonses.com`) — check the domain's SPF record includes `amazonses.com` (a domain must have exactly one SPF TXT record — add `include:amazonses.com` to the existing one, don't create a second) and that DMARC isn't set to strict quarantine without alignment. Allow 24–48h for DNS propagation.

### Q21: Does Chatty have a public API?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, on **Pro and Plus plans** — generate a key at **Settings → API**. Works for `GET /chat/conversations` (paginated, filterable) and `GET /chat/conversations/{id}/messages`. Webhooks are also available on Pro+ (`customer_message`, `ai_response` events pushed to your own server) — no separate "callback URL" field, this is configured via the API key setup. Doc: help.chatty.net/integrations/chatty-public-api.

### 7c. Order tracking & Other integrations

### Q22: How does order tracking work, and does live in-chat order lookup require a specific plan?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Default provider is 17TRACK (no setup needed). Configure at **Settings → Integrations → Order tracking**: default carrier tracking, a **Custom tracking link** (any plan, including Free — note it only supports one tracking URL at a time, so it can't be split per-carrier), or your own 17TRACK API key (sign up free at 17track.net/en/api — no Chatty plan upgrade needed for this specifically). The AI's **live** order-lookup (customer asks "where's my order" directly in chat) requires **Basic plan or higher**; the static Order Tracking widget works on any plan, including Free.

### Q23: What data does Chatty actually collect, and does it use Shopify's MCP servers?
**Frequency:** ~1 session
**Status:** 🆕 new (documented for reference)

**Standard answer:**
Chatty does **not** use Shopify MCP servers — it collects data via standard Shopify Admin APIs and Webhooks (OAuth-based). Data collected: Products/Collections (title, price, inventory, URLs, variants), Orders (status/details), Discounts, and customer info (name/email/phone) only when submitted via chat; from the storefront widget script: cart state, page context, device/browser info, and the Shopify Customer ID if the visitor is logged in; from live conversations: chat messages, pre-chat form data, and CSAT ratings; from third-party integrations, Klaviyo sync is push-only (Chatty sends data to Klaviyo, it does not pull from it).

## 8. Translation

### Q24: How many languages can my chatbox support, and how many are auto-translated?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Add the language in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages** first, then **Settings → Translation → Add language** in Chatty. Click **Auto-translate** (or edit manually), **Save**, then **Publish → Confirm**.

| Plan | Languages the chatbox can publish | Of those, auto-translated by Chatty |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | 0 — manual only |
| Basic | 3 | 2 |
| Pro | 10 | 9 |
| Plus | Unlimited | Unlimited |

The AI's live conversational replies auto-detect and adapt to the customer's language regardless of how many languages are published (see Q8) — this limit only applies to **static** chatbox text (Conversation Starters, Human Handover message, FAQ buttons).

### Q26: What counts as "1 AI conversation," and what happens when I hit my monthly limit?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
One AI conversation = one complete interaction from first message through completion (manual resolve, auto-resolve after inactivity, reset, 3-day session expiry, deletion, or block) — not counted per individual message. On the **Free** plan, once the 50/month limit is hit, the AI pauses and stops taking new chats until the next billing cycle (manual/human handling in Inbox still works; extra conversations cannot be purchased on Free). On **paid** plans, usage past the limit is billed at **$0.40/conversation** unless a spending limit is set under **Subscription → View details → Change limit** to pause AI instead of incurring overage. Check current usage at **Chatty → Subscription → Daily AI Conversation and Usage**.

### Q27: Can I get a discount, or increase my seat/product limit beyond the plan cap?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No self-serve discount tool exists — every request is routed to the team for manual review. Training-data limits (products, custom answers, URL/file count) can sometimes be extended one tier as a one-off accommodation directly by support; AI conversation and translation-quota limits need product-team approval. Contact support with the store URL, current plan, and the specific limit hit.

## 10. Edge Cases / Bugs / Feature Requests

### Q28: Can Chatty block spam/repeat-abuse messages, or block a customer permanently?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No native IP-level blocking, bot-detection, or CAPTCHA, and no permanent block by email address — blocking inside Chatty is session-based only (open the conversation → visitor profile → Block). Mitigations: require the pre-chat form to add friction, or turn on **Settings → Automation → Spam protection → Smart spam protection**.

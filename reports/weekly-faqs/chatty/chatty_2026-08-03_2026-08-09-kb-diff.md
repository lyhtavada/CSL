## Cần sửa (OUTDATED)

### 1. Plus-plan "Products for AI training" limit — wrong in 5 files

**Verified ground truth** (chatty.net/pricing raw HTML, 3 independent extractions, 2026-08-09):
> `Products for AI training | 200 | 500 | 8,000 | 20,000` (Free / Basic / Pro / Plus)
> Also confirmed via the plan-card bullet list: *"1,000 AI conversations/month, 20,000 products for AI training, Unlimited team members"* (Plus card).

**Stale line, file by file:**

- **`kb/faq/pricing.md`** — Plan Feature Comparison section: *"**Plus:** AI conversations 1,000/month + $0.40/extra, **products unlimited**, custom answers unlimited, URLs & files 700, team members unlimited..."* and the bullet list above it: *"**Plus** — ...**unlimited products**, unlimited custom answers, 700 URLs & files..."*
  → Correct line: `products 20,000` (not unlimited) in both the bullet list and the comparison paragraph.

- **`kb/case/ai-product-limit.md`** — Plan Limits Reference table: `| Products for AI training | 200 | 500 | 8,000 | Unlimited |`
  → Correct: `| 200 | 500 | 8,000 | 20,000 |`. This also affects the "Decide how much to extend" table's Pro row (`Pro → extend to Unlimited (= Plus)`), which should read `Pro → extend to 20,000 (= Plus)`.

- **`kb/faq/knowledge-base.md`** — Plans table: `| Products for AI training | 200 | 500 | 8,000 | Unlimited |`
  → Correct: `20,000`. **Two additional stale figures found in the same table** (not from the mined FAQs, caught while verifying — flagging since they're in the same row range and equally wrong):
  - Header shows `Plus ($199.99/mo)` — verified live price is **$199/mo**, not $199.99.
  - `Chat history | 90 days | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited` — Basic is **12 months**, not Unlimited (confirmed both via raw HTML table and the Basic plan card bullet: *"12-month chat history"*).

- **`kb/case/ai-product-sync.md`** — "AI Not Syncing All Products" section: *"the merchant has hit their plan's product limit (Free: 200, Basic: 500, Pro: 8,000, Plus: Unlimited)"*
  → Correct: `Plus: 20,000`.

- **`kb/case/ai-sync-issues.md`** — same sentence appears twice (once under "AI Not Syncing All Products", once implicitly referenced in step 1): *"Free: 200, Basic: 500, Pro: 8,000, Plus: Unlimited"*
  → Correct: `Plus: 20,000`.

**One-line fix (all 5 files):** replace every standalone occurrence of `Plus: Unlimited` / `Unlimited` / `unlimited products` in a **products-for-AI-training** context with **`20,000`**. `kb/faq/knowledge-base.md` additionally needs `$199.99/mo` → `$199/mo` and Basic chat history `Unlimited` → `12 months`.

## Cần bổ sung (GAP/PARTIAL)

### GAP — No store-migration / replication guidance
**File to add to:** `kb/faq/others.md` (general/miscellaneous FAQ file) or a new `kb/faq/store-migration.md`.
**What to add:** Confirm no automatic migration tool exists between Shopify stores/domains — conversation history, contacts, and usage data are bound to the specific store even under the same account email. AI Instructions, Custom Scenarios, and FAQ/Q&A content must be manually re-created; chatbox appearance and channel connections must be reconfigured from scratch. Note that the team can do this manually as a one-off service (~24h), similar to a DFY request. Two sessions asked this in the sampled week (chunk 7: `session_3df91356`, `session_9b190eab`) — low volume so far, but zero KB coverage means every occurrence currently requires a from-scratch explanation.

### PARTIAL — FAQ edits don't automatically update matching Conversation Starters
**File to add to:** `kb/faq/chatbox-settings.md`, under the existing "Setting Up Conversation Starters" section.
**What's missing:** The current section explains how to add a Conversation Starter and link it to a scenario, but doesn't mention that **Conversation Starters are a separate list from FAQs and do not sync automatically in either direction** — editing an FAQ's answer does not update a Conversation Starter that asks the same question, and importing a starter from an FAQ is a one-time manual action, not a live link. Two sessions this week (`session_3993f20d`, `session_d0ddbe0a`, chunk 0) hit this and support ended up manually copy-pasting FAQ answers into Conversation Starters as a workaround because the merchant assumed editing the FAQ would update the starter. Suggested addition: a short callout similar to the existing "Do not confuse these controls" note elsewhere in the same file.

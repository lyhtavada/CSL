## PARTIAL / GAP detail (patched this run)

### 1. GAP — Test AI panel shows random questions, not real Commonly Asked (Q7)
**File:** `kb/faq/chatbox-settings.md`
**Where:** appended after the existing "Setting Up Conversation Starters" section (which already documents that Commonly Asked and Conversation Starters are the same underlying list).
**Why it's a gap:** the KB already explains the AI-Mode-vs-Legacy split and that Commonly Asked = Conversation Starters, but nowhere states that the **Test AI panel itself shows random questions unrelated to that config** — this caused at least 2 independent confused-merchant threads this week ("I configured 3 questions, Test AI keeps showing different ones") that support had to manually reproduce and explain each time. Adding this saves a repeat investigation.
**Confidence:** high — corroborated independently in two different chunks with matching agent explanations ("Test AI pulls random suggested questions from FAQ/training data for testing purposes").

### 2. PARTIAL — Holiday message persists via leftover training-data entry (Q15)
**File:** `kb/faq/online-hours.md`
**Where:** appended right after the existing "Holiday Hours and Chats Sent Outside Working Hours (fixed 24/08)" section.
**Why it's a partial:** the KB documents the Holiday toggle and the 24/08 fix for holiday-hours display, but doesn't cover the distinct case (confirmed this week, root-caused by the support team) where a manually-added Custom Knowledge entry referencing the holiday keeps being cited by the AI even after the toggle is off. This was a multi-day, high-frustration case for one merchant (two stores, "PP"/"PG") — worth having documented so it's caught on the first pass next time.
**Confidence:** high — root cause explicitly confirmed and explained by the support team in the transcript, with the exact fix applied (removed the leftover entry).

### 3. GAP — Discount block (Cart booster/Subscribe newsletter) has no CTA button or auto-copy (Q16)

### 4. PARTIAL — Microsoft 365 outbound-forwarding block (Q20)
**File:** `kb/case/email-channel-issues.md`
**Where:** appended after the existing generic "Corporate Email — IT Admin Approval Required" section, before "Alias, Forwarding-Loop, and Stuck Unverified Cases".
**Why it's a partial:** the KB already has a generic "IT admin may block forwarding" case and separately covers the Outlook/Hotmail-Cloudflare incompatibility, but has no specific guidance for the very common **Microsoft 365** case, which has an exact, fixable admin setting and a distinctive bounce code (`550 5.7.520 Access denied, Your organization does not allow external forwarding`). This was a multi-day escalation this week before the specific M365 admin path was found. Also noted (informationally, not as a new fact needing verification): support mentioned Chatty itself previously didn't surface a clear warning for this case and has since improved that — kept as a note, not asserted as a hard "fixed" fact since it wasn't independently confirmed against a changelog.
**Confidence:** high on the M365 fix path itself (merchant confirmed working after applying it) — the "Chatty now shows a clearer error" claim is lower-confidence (single support-side mention, not verified against release notes), so it's phrased cautiously in the patch.

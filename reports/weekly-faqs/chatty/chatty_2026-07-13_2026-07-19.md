## 1. Getting Started & Setup

### Q1: How do I set up and train the AI agent for the first time?
**Frequency:** ~90 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Setting up the AI agent takes four steps:

1. **Add training data** — go to **AI agent → Training data**. Your products, collections, discounts and Shopify policy pages (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, FAQ, Contact us, About us) sync automatically. Turn on the sources you want the AI to learn from.
2. **Add your own knowledge** — under **Training data → Custom knowledge** you can add Q&A pairs, website URLs, or files (.JSON, .TXT, .PDF, .CSV, max 2MB each).
3. **Set instructions** — go to **AI agent → Instructions** to set the assistant's name, role, tone, boundaries, and what it should never discuss.
4. **Test, then activate** — use **Test AI** (top right) to check answers before going live, then enable the AI agent.

Finally, make sure the chatbox itself is live: **Chatbox → General → Turn on**, enable the **Live chat** block, and enable Chatty in **Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds**.

## 2. AI Agent — Training & Data

### Q6: Where does the AI get its answers from? Do I have to type everything in?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Most of it is automatic. Chatty auto-syncs from your Shopify store:

- **Products** — titles, descriptions, variants, pricing, availability, standard metafields
- **Collections, Discounts, Markets**
- **Store pages** — Shipping policy, Return policy, Privacy policy, Terms of service, FAQ, Contact us, About us

Product data refreshes daily; you can force a refresh via **AI agent → Training data → Products → Manage → Sync products**.

Anything not on those pages you add yourself under **Training data → Custom knowledge** — as Q&A pairs, URLs to scrape, or uploaded files. A quick way to seed it: **Custom knowledge → Sync Store Page → Add Q&A** scans your policy pages and auto-generates around 15 common Q&As for you to review and keep.

The AI does not need an exact keyword match — it understands rephrased questions and answers from everything it has learned.

### Q7: My products aren't showing in Training data / the AI says it has no product information.
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Work through these in order:

1. **Is the Products source enabled?** — **AI agent → Training data → Products**. The source has its own on/off toggle, and individual products have an Enabled/Disabled status.
2. **Check the tab filter** — items sit under Active/Inactive tabs; "No items found" is often just the wrong tab.
3. **Force a sync** — **Products → Manage → Sync products**.
4. **Reset the conversation** before retesting — an open chat keeps using the knowledge it loaded when it started, so it won't reflect a fresh sync.
5. Only **published, in-stock** products are used. Product subscriptions aren't supported.

If products still don't appear after a manual sync, send us your store URL — we've seen genuine sync failures this week and can resync from our side.

- **Questions** — Q&A pairs you write yourself

### Q10: What's the difference between FAQs, Custom knowledge, Instructions and Scenarios?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
They do four different jobs:

- **FAQs** (the **FAQs** menu) — customer-facing content shown on your FAQ page, in FAQ blocks, and inside the chat. When enabled as a training source, the AI also learns from them.
- **Custom knowledge** (**AI agent → Training data → Custom knowledge**) — background knowledge for the AI only. Never displayed as a list to customers.
- **Instructions** (**AI agent → Instructions**) — *how* the AI behaves: its name, role, tone, boundaries, what it must never say.
- **Custom scenarios** (**AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Custom scenarios**) — rules for specific situations, triggered by keywords, with their own instructions (up to 1,000 characters each).

Rule of thumb: facts go in Training data, behaviour goes in Instructions, situation-specific handling goes in Scenarios.

### Q11: Does the AI learn by itself from customer conversations?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No. The AI answers from the training data you've given it — it doesn't automatically absorb new facts from chats or from your replies to customers.

What it does collect is **Unresolved questions**: things customers asked that it couldn't answer. Review those regularly and turn the important ones into Q&A entries under **Training data → Custom knowledge**. That review loop — test, check what it got wrong, add the missing data — is how the knowledge base improves.

### Q12: How do my Shopify discount codes reach the AI?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Discounts created in Shopify sync daily into **AI agent → Training data → Discounts**. Discounts available to all customers are active by default; discounts limited to a specific customer segment must be enabled manually there.

Two limitations: dynamic discounts and discounts created by third-party apps don't sync. If a code is live in Shopify but never suggested by the AI, check whether it appears in the Discounts tab — if it's missing, send us the code and your store URL so we can investigate. You can always guarantee coverage by adding the code as a Q&A in Custom knowledge.

### Q13: The AI doesn't know product details that are stored in metafields.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Standard Shopify product metafields can be synced — go to **AI agent → Training data → Products → Manage → Manage metafields** and enable the ones you need. If you see a "metafields limit reached" message, contact us and we can raise it.

Custom or third-party app metafields, and metaobject references, aren't supported as training sources. The reliable workaround is to add that information as a Q&A or file under **Training data → Custom knowledge**, or to include it in the product description so it syncs with the product. We've logged broader metafield support as a feature request.

## 3. AI Agent — Accuracy & Behaviour

### Q14: The AI gave a customer wrong or outdated information. How do I fix it?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Use **Review sources** — the link under any AI answer that shows exactly which training data it used. That tells you what to correct rather than guessing.

Then:
1. Fix the source it actually used (an FAQ, a Custom knowledge entry, a synced page).
2. Check for **conflicting entries** saying different things — this is the most common cause. Two FAQs with different delivery times will produce inconsistent answers.
3. Add or tighten an instruction under **AI agent → Instructions** if it's a behaviour issue rather than a fact issue.
4. **Reset the conversation** and retest — an in-progress chat keeps using the knowledge it loaded at the start.

---

### Q15: The AI invented something — a store location, a phone number, a product category we don't sell.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The AI shouldn't invent answers, so treat this as a data gap rather than only a behaviour problem. Check **Review sources** on the answer: if no source mentions the invented fact, the AI generalised because the correct information was missing.

The fix is to **add the correct information**, not to add a "never say X" rule. Negative instructions are unreliable and can end up quoted back to customers. Add the real address, the real category list, or the real policy as a Q&A in Custom knowledge, then retest.

If it keeps happening on a specific question after the data is in place, send us the conversation and we'll investigate — we've fixed several cases like this at the platform level in the past week.

---

### Q16: The AI keeps giving the old answer even after I updated the FAQ.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This is almost always a second, forgotten copy of the answer. Check these three places:

1. **Conversation starters** — the pre-set questions in the chatbox carry their own answers. This was the actual root cause in most cases we saw this week; merchants edited the FAQ but the starter still held the old text.
2. **Settings → Translations** — each published language stores its own copy of the AI's messages and FAQ text. The translated copy overrides what you edited in the main settings.
3. Other FAQs or Custom knowledge entries covering the same topic.

Run **Review sources** on the wrong answer to see which one is being used, correct that specific entry, then reset the conversation and retest.

---

### Q17: The AI replies in the wrong language, or mixes languages.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Start with an instruction: **AI agent → Instructions → Behaviours** → add "Always respond in the same language as the customer." You can write the instruction itself in your own language.

If it still answers in English for certain questions, the trigger is usually untranslated content rather than the AI:

- **Conversation starters** still written in English — translate them under **Settings → Translations → [language] → Live chat → Conversation Starters**.
- FAQ titles or Custom knowledge entries in English.
- The default language under **Settings → Translation** pointing at the wrong language — you can change it via the settings icon next to a language → **Change default**.

Languages must exist in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages** before they appear in Chatty.

### Q18: The AI answers correctly in Test AI but gives a different, wrong answer on the live site.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
First, reset the conversation on the storefront. A live chat session holds the knowledge it loaded when it started, so a chat opened before your change will keep the old answer — this explains most of these reports.

If a freshly reset conversation still differs from Test AI, that's not expected. Send us the question, a screenshot of the live answer, and the **Review sources** output from it — we have open investigations on this and the detail helps us pin down the retrieval difference.

### Q19: The AI recommends irrelevant products, or ignores size/variant requirements.
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The AI works at **product level, not variant level**. It can't filter by live inventory, match a specific SKU by stock status, or apply numeric range logic (e.g. "clubs for a 59-inch player"). Product cards are also retrieved separately from the text answer, which is why a card can look off-topic next to a correct reply.

What helps in practice:

- Put sizing, dimensions and specs into the **product description** so they're part of the synced data.
- Use **product tags** and set up **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Product recommendations** to steer which products surface for which queries.
- Disable products that should never be recommended (e.g. practitioner-only items) under **Training data → Products**.
- Create a **custom scenario** for the specific decision rule you need.

Variant-level and live-inventory recommendation is logged as a feature request.

### Q20: The AI shared information I didn't want customers to see (a staff email, our home address).
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The AI only shares what's in its training data or settings, so trace the source first — **Review sources** on the offending answer will show it. In the cases we handled it came from either a merchant-added FAQ containing the address, or the **Support email** field under **AI agent → Instructions**.

To fix it: remove or edit the entry containing the private detail, replace it with the contact information you *do* want shared, and add a boundary instruction under **AI agent → Instructions** covering that category of information. Then test with a few phrasings to confirm. If you'd like, send us the details and we'll make the change and verify it for you.

### Q22: The chat gives customers random names ("Hello Grateful dream") — how do I turn that off?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
When a visitor hasn't given their details, Chatty assigns a random label so the conversation can be identified in your inbox. Two things you can do today:

1. **Switch to a fixed anonymous label** — the visitor shows as "Anonymous-xxx" instead of a random name. Contact us and we'll change the setting for you.
2. **Remove the `{{customer_name}}` variable** from your AI welcome message (**AI agent → Settings → AI identity → Welcome message**) so the random name is never spoken to the customer. The label then only appears in your inbox.

If you'd rather capture real names, enable the **pre-chat form** under **Chatbox → Chat page → Pre-chat Form** and set Name as required. Fully configurable anonymous naming is a logged feature request.

### Q23: The AI isn't replying at all.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check in this order:

1. **Is the AI agent activated?** — the toggle on the **AI agent** page.
2. **Is the Live chat block on?** — **Chatbox → General → Blocks**.
3. **Is a pre-chat form blocking the flow?** — if the customer hasn't completed a required form, the AI won't respond. Under **Chatbox → Chat page → Pre-chat Form**, choose "Show both" or enable anonymous chat so they can message immediately.
4. **Has a human already joined?** — the AI stops once an agent takes over (see below).
5. **Have you hit your monthly AI conversation limit?** — check **Subscription → View details**.

### Q24: The AI stopped responding in a conversation a human had joined.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
That's expected behaviour: once a human agent replies in a conversation, the AI steps back so you're not talking over each other. It doesn't resume automatically in that same thread.

To have the AI handle the conversation again, use **Reset conversation**. For new conversations nothing changes — the AI picks them up as normal.

### Q25: Can I turn the AI off completely and handle chats myself?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes. Go to the **AI agent** page and click **Deactivate** (top right). Every conversation then goes straight to your Inbox with no AI involvement and no trace of it in the chat.

The AI is also off by default on a new install, so if you've never activated it there's nothing to change. You can reactivate at any time.

### Q26: How do I control what happens when the AI can't answer?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Go to **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Customer support skills → Human handover**. There are two parts:

**When to hand off** — automatic triggers, plus **Intent rules** (**+ Add rule**) where you define topics that should always go straight to a human, such as cancellations, wholesale, or legal requests. Intent rules transfer immediately without an AI reply first.

**Where the customer goes** — transfer to your Chatty inbox, collect the customer's details for follow-up by email (with a **Response time promise** you set), or show your contact methods. If you choose transfer to inbox, you can also configure offline behaviour and whether the AI keeps replying while the customer waits.

You can change the destination at any time. To be alerted when a handover happens, enable email notifications under **Settings → Notifications**, and make sure the agent has "There is a conversation assigned to me" enabled in their personal notification settings.

### Q27: Can the AI look up order status?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, and it's available on every plan including Free. To enable it:

1. **Chatbox → General** → turn on the **Order tracking** block.
2. **Settings → Integrations → Order tracking → Manage** → choose your tracking method → **Save**.

Methods: **Default tracking** (redirects to the carrier's page — best for DHL/FedEx/UPS), **Custom tracking** (your own tracking URL — best for local carriers), or **17TRACK** (keeps customers on your site).

One thing merchants are often surprised by: for identity verification the AI needs the customer's **email or phone number** as well — it won't return order details from an order number alone.

### Q28: Can the AI read an image or document a customer sends?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The AI can match a product photo against your synced catalog and reply about that product. It cannot read or interpret uploaded documents — PDFs, reports, or forms sent by a customer in chat — and it shouldn't be relied on for anything requiring interpretation of a document. Route those to a human via a Human handover intent rule.

### Q29: I've run out of room in the Instructions field.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Each instruction section has a character cap. Two options:

- **Move facts out of Instructions.** Instructions should describe *behaviour* — tone, boundaries, routing. Product details, policies and reference information belong in **Training data → Custom knowledge**, which is far more generous and better suited to it. Situation-specific rules belong in **Custom scenarios**.
- **Ask us to raise the cap.** We can increase the instruction character allowance on request — several merchants had this done this week.

## 4. Chatbox & Widget

### Q30: I changed the welcome message but the old one still shows.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring (frequency sharply UP)

**Standard answer:**
There are three separate welcome messages, and the one that displays depends on your setup:

1. **Chatbox → Chat page → Welcome Message** — used when the AI agent is **off**.
2. **AI agent → Settings → AI identity → Welcome message** — used when the AI agent is **on**, and it overrides the chatbox one. Supports `{{customer_name}}`.
3. **Settings → Translations → [language] → AI Agent / Live chat** — each published language keeps its own copy, and this overrides both of the above for visitors in that language.

If you have the AI enabled, edit #2. If you have translations published, also update #3 — a stale translated copy is the most common reason an edit "doesn't take". Note that a separate **Proactive Chat** campaign message can also appear as a pop-up teaser; that one is edited in the **Proactive chat** section.

### Q31: The chat window opens by itself on every page load.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Three possible causes, in order of likelihood:

1. **Chat focus mode** — **Chatbox → General** → toggle it off. (It's only active when Live chat is enabled.)
2. **A Proactive Chat campaign** set to trigger on page load — check the **Proactive chat** section and deactivate or delay the campaign.
3. **An Embedded chatbox section placed in your theme** — if the chatbox was added as a page section rather than as the floating widget, it renders open. Remove the section in the theme editor.

If you want the opposite — the chatbox opening straight into the chat so customers can type immediately — turn Chat focus mode **on** and allow anonymous chat in the pre-chat form.

### Q32: How do I move the chat button, or stop it overlapping my Add-to-Cart button / cart drawer?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
For position: **Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — drag the marker or pick a preset corner and set offsets. No code needed.

Overlaps with a sticky Add-to-Cart bar, a cart drawer, or another app's launcher usually need a small CSS rule, since there's no built-in "hide when cart opens" toggle. Add it under **Chatbox → Advanced → Custom CSS**, or send us your store URL and a screenshot and we'll apply the snippet for you — we did this for several merchants this week, including moving the widget behind the cart layer on mobile.

You can also hide the widget entirely on specific pages: **Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings → Display pages → Hide on specific pages**. When entering a page, use the path only (`/pages/contact-us`), not the full URL — a full URL returns "Custom URL page is invalid".

### Q33: Can I show the widget only on certain pages or only on desktop?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — **Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings**:

- **Display pages** — all pages, only specific pages, or hide on specific pages (enter paths like `/pages/contact-us`).
- **Display devices** — desktop only, mobile only, or both.

This controls *where the widget appears*, not what the AI knows. Restricting the widget to one page doesn't limit the AI's knowledge of the rest of your store. Note that the chatbox can't be shown on Shopify's checkout pages unless you're on Shopify Plus.

### Q35: How do I turn off / edit the conversation starters and pre-chat form?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Conversation starters** — **Chatbox → Chat page → Conversation Starters**. Edit or disable them here. Keep them to about three; they're quick-tap buttons and are separate from AI answers, so they don't conflict with what the AI says. Remember each starter carries its own answer — if you change a policy, update the starter too.

**Pre-chat form** — **Chatbox → Chat page → Pre-chat Form**. You can:
- Require name/email/phone before chatting (best for lead capture)
- Choose **Show both** so customers can chat anonymously or leave details
- Show the form after a number of messages instead of before the first one
- Remove individual fields, or set each as required or optional

Requiring the form does reduce completed conversations — several "the AI isn't replying" reports this week were customers who never filled it in.

### Q38: Chatty's script loads on every page — can I stop that for PageSpeed?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**

Lazy-loading the script until a visitor interacts isn't currently configurable. We've logged this — along with the related cookie-consent request — with the product team.

### Q49: My analytics numbers don't look right.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
A few definitions explain most discrepancies:

- **Handled Conversations** counts only replies made **within 24 hours** of the customer's message. Replies to a backlog older than that aren't counted, so the figure can look lower than your actual work.
- **First Response Time** is a median across conversations that received a **human** reply; automated replies and stale backlog replies are excluded.
- **Total conversations showing 0** usually means the **Live chat** block is off (see Q4) — no conversations are being created.

If the numbers still don't reconcile after that, send us your store URL and the period in question. We've had genuine counting issues this week that our team corrected at the backend, including a CSAT rating that wasn't recording.

## 7. Channels

### Q50: My WhatsApp number is stuck on "Pending" or the wrong number connected.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring (frequency sharply UP, still unresolved for some)

**Standard answer:**
To connect WhatsApp you need a Facebook page, a WhatsApp Business account linked to that page, admin access to both, and **two-factor authentication enabled** in your WhatsApp Business settings. Missing 2FA is the most common cause of a number that never leaves Pending. Connect via **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp**.

If 2FA is on and the number is still Pending, or a number you don't own appears instead of yours, this needs our team. Send us your WhatsApp Business Account ID, the phone number, and a screenshot of the Meta connection screen.

Being transparent: we have open cases here that took multiple days, including one traced to a conflict with another provider already registered against the number on Meta's side. If you previously connected WhatsApp through another Shopify app, removing that integration in Meta first often clears it.

### Q51: How do I get the AI replying on Instagram and Messenger?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Two separate steps:

1. **Connect the channel** — **Settings → Channels** → connect your Facebook account. Instagram must be a business account linked to a Facebook Page. This is available on any plan and brings the conversations into your Chatty inbox.

### Q52: I can't reply to a Messenger or Instagram customer — the message fails.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Meta enforces a **24-hour messaging window**: if the customer's last message is more than 24 hours old, replies are blocked on their platform. This is Meta's policy and it can't be extended from Chatty's side.

The practical approach is to reply within the window where possible, and to capture an email address during the conversation so you have a second route for anything needing a longer turnaround.

### Q53: My email channel won't verify / emails aren't reaching Chatty.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Set this up under **Settings → Channels → Email**, and make sure the mail provider is set correctly (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, or Others) — picking the wrong one gives you the wrong setup guide, which was the actual blocker in a case this week.

Common causes:
- **Gmail** sends its own confirmation to the Chatty forwarding address as part of setting up forwarding — that's Gmail's step, not ours, and there's a retry limit if you attempt it repeatedly.
- **Outlook** often blocks automatic forwarding at the organisation level. Screenshot the error and ask your IT administrator to permit forwarding to Chatty, then reconnect.
- The notification address itself showing **Unverified** will silently stop notifications — verify it or switch to a different address.
- Avoid using the same mailbox for both forwarding into Chatty and receiving notifications; that creates a loop.

Also note: replying from your own mail client doesn't sync back to the chat — reply from the Chatty Inbox. When you do, the customer receives your reply by email automatically if they left an address.

### Q62: What counts as an "AI conversation", and how do I control overage?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
One full chat session counts as **one conversation**, no matter how many messages are exchanged in it. The allowance is **store-wide per month**, shared across every channel (website, Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp) — not per customer.

Two things that do consume from the allowance and often surprise merchants: **resetting a conversation** starts a new one, and the product-page AI assistant creates frictionless conversations that count whenever the AI gives a valid response. If low-quality volume is an issue, turning off suggested questions on product pages reduces it.

To check usage: **Chatty → Subscription → View details**. You'll get email alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit, and usage resets on your billing cycle date, not the calendar month.

To cap spending: **Subscription → View details → Change limit** → set your budget → **Save**. Once reached, the AI stops replying until the next cycle and your human agents continue as normal. Setting the cap 15–20% above expected usage handles busy periods.

On the Free plan there's no overage — the AI simply pauses until the quota resets next month, and you can keep handling conversations manually in the Inbox.

### Q70: Can I stop spam or bot traffic in the chat?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

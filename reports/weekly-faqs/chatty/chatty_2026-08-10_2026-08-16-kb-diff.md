## OUTDATED — Detail

### Q8 — AI Instructions character limit is raisable or not?

**Contradiction:** `kb/faq/ai-training-setup.md` (`## Custom Scenario Character Limit`) currently says:

> "This is different from the Instructions field cap (AI agent → Instructions → General instructions): that cap *can* be raised on request — contact support."

Mined Q8 (from this week's real chats, resolved by support directly) says the opposite:

> "Instructions has a ~1,000-character limit and support **cannot raise it** (risk of AI drifting/hallucinating with an overloaded prompt). For longer reference material, move it to Training data → Custom knowledge, which has a much higher limit."

**Fix:** flip the claim in `ai-training-setup.md` to "cannot be raised," and fix the same claim referenced from `kb/faq/chatbox-settings.md` (`## Changing the Welcome Message` footnote calls the Instructions cap "raisable, see faq_ai-training-setup.md") — per the "update every file that references a config" rule, both need the correction so they don't contradict each other again.

### Q21 — Can the "Reply-To" address be changed?

**Contradiction:** `kb/case/email-channel-issues.md` (`## Forwarding Email Showing in Reply-To`) currently instructs CS to *fix* the Reply-To by changing the Email sender field:

> "Check the Email sender field — make sure it's set to your actual store support email, not the Chatty forwarding address... to confirm the correct email appears in Reply-To."

Mined Q21 says Reply-To **cannot be changed at all, by design**:

> "'Reply-To' is fixed to Chatty's internal routing address by design, so customer replies route back into the Inbox — this cannot currently be changed."

**Fix:** rewrite the section to state Reply-To is fixed by design (needed so replies route back into the Chatty Inbox), and that the Email sender field only changes the **From** name/domain, not Reply-To. The old section conflated two different fields and would tell a merchant something achievable that isn't.

Merchants conflate the two.

- **Q15** → `kb/faq/ai-agent-settings.md` (`## AI Availability`): doesn't explicitly rule out time-based failover or give the "treat team as offline even in business hours" workaround. `kb/faq/chatbox-settings.md` (`## Away Mode`) doesn't note the toggle only lives in the standalone web app, not the Shopify-embedded admin.
- **Q16** → `kb/faq/team.md` (`## Staff Permissions`): confirms permissions are per-staff-member but doesn't list the specific granular areas now shipped (Chat availability, Reports, Channels & integrations, Billing & plan) or the None/View/Edit levels — this was previously a feature request per this week's mined header, so the KB should reflect it's live.
- **Q18** → `kb/case/whatsapp-messenger-issues.md`: connection requirements are covered, but the actual **migration sequence** off the personal WhatsApp/Business mobile app (back up chat history → delete the mobile account → create WABA → OTP re-verify → enable 2FA) isn't spelled out as steps.
- **Q19** → `kb/case/whatsapp-messenger-issues.md`: the Meta 24-hour reply window is documented, and image/file support is documented, but the two aren't connected to the specific complaint "can't send images/files" — and the transient backend-upload-failure cause (distinct from the Meta policy block) isn't mentioned.
- **Q20** → `kb/case/email-channel-issues.md` (`## Outlook/Hotmail Forwarding Not Working`): doesn't include the diagnostic bounce sender `mailer-daemon@amazonses.com`, which is a quick way to confirm this known issue from a bounce screenshot.
- **Q22** → `kb/faq/order-tracking.md`: no mention that the AI's conversational live order-lookup (vs. the static Order Tracking widget, which works on any plan) requires **Basic plan or higher**.
- **Q24** → `kb/faq/inbox.md` (`## Exporting Conversation History`): confirms bulk export is team-assisted, but doesn't list the now-documented exportable field set (conversation ID, country, language, channel, page, timestamps, sender type, order ID/amount/payment method) or what's explicitly excluded (UTM, device/browser/OS, payment/refund status).
- **Q30** → `kb/faq/general-settings.md` (`## Automation Settings` → Spam protection): mentions Smart spam protection but doesn't state there's no IP-level blocking/CAPTOCHA/bot-detection, that blocking is session-based only, or the pre-chat-form/spending-cap mitigations for repeat abuse.

## 1. Getting Started / AI Agent Setup

### Q1: How do I set up the AI agent from scratch?
**Frequency:** ~130 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. **AI agent → Training data → Manage** — add training data (Products, Collections, Discounts, Markets, FAQs, Custom knowledge). Products/Collections/Discounts sync automatically from the store once a day (00:00 UTC, or force with **Sync products**); store pages (Shipping/Return/FAQ/About) sync automatically whenever edited.
2. **AI agent → Instructions → Manage** — set tone/response behavior (custom instructions). A **Generate with AI** button can draft a starting point based on business type.
3. **AI agent → Settings** — set AI name, avatar, welcome message, availability.
4. **Test AI → Test now** — test before going live, then **AI agent → Turn on** to activate.

Chatty also offers a free "done-for-you" (DFY) setup: the bot runs a guided intake (what the assistant should take off your plate, current support coverage, 24/7 vs. only-when-away, name/greeting, notification channel + email, brand/tone, boundaries, human-handover routing, order-lookup preference) and hands off to the CS team, who complete training and send a Loom/Jam walkthrough video for review — usually within a few hours to ~12–24 hours.

### Q2: How do I enable the chat widget / live chat on my storefront?
**Frequency:** ~35 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
1. Enable the app embed: **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds** → toggle **Chatty** on → Save.
2. In Chatty: **Chatbox → General** → turn on "Display chatbox on your store".
3. Under **Blocks**, turn on **Live chat**.
4. **Chatbox → Chat page → Pre-chat form** — choose whether to require guest info (email required, name/phone optional) or allow **Chat as anonymous**.
5. Reload the storefront to confirm the "Chat now" button appears.

**Note:** Uninstalling the app does NOT automatically turn this off — Shopify doesn't remove app embeds on uninstall, so a merchant who removed Chatty but still sees the widget needs to manually toggle the App embed off in the theme.

### Q3: Can I add the Chatty widget to a non-Shopify website, or use the same Chatty account across two stores?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
For a non-Shopify page: go to **Settings → Integrations → Website integration**, copy the HTML/Liquid snippet, and paste it before `</body>`.

For a second Shopify store under the same login: install Chatty on the new store, then at web-app activation click **Activate** — it links to your existing account instead of creating a new one. Switch stores via the store switcher at app.chatty.net. Each store keeps its **own separate inbox, plan, and settings** — nothing merges, and two markets/domains on the *same* Shopify store still share one Chatty config (see Q6b).

## 2. AI / Copilot Training

### Q4: Why did my AI give a wrong or fabricated answer (invented policy, wrong price, wrong product recommendation)?
**Frequency:** ~20 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
When the AI has no matching FAQ/training-data entry, it can guess rather than say "I don't know." Fix: use **"Review Sources"** under the AI's reply to see exactly which FAQ/data source it pulled from, then correct or add a Q&A at **AI agent → Training data → Manage → FAQs** (or **Custom knowledge**) with the accurate fact stated explicitly. Re-test via **Test AI → Test now**.

Two extra failure patterns confirmed this week:
- **Multi-topic question crowds out an answer:** the AI's retrieval only pulls its top-3 most relevant training entries per message. If a customer bundles many questions at once (price + delivery + dimensions + weight), a lower-priority topic (e.g. delivery time) can get crowded out of the top-3 even though it answers correctly when asked alone.
- **AI claims an action was taken that wasn't** (e.g. "I've forwarded this to our team") when Human Handover is actually set to "Show contact methods," not a live transfer. Fix: add an explicit instruction not to claim unperformed actions, and pick the Human Handover option that matches what actually happens.

### Q5: The AI shared a discount/coupon code I don't want it to share, or recommended unrelated products — how do I stop it?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
- To stop unwanted product suggestions in general: **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → Assistant skills → Shopping skills → Product recommendations**, toggle off, or add a Behaviours instruction like "Do not recommend products unless explicitly asked."
- To stop it recommending unrelated products during a specific type of question (e.g. pricing-only): add a boundary instruction, e.g. "Do NOT proactively suggest unrelated products when responding to pricing inquiries; only recommend if the customer explicitly asks."
- To hide **non-public / gated discount codes** (e.g. codes only meant to be unlocked via a verification flow) while still allowing public codes: **AI agent → Training data → Discounts**, deactivate the specific codes that shouldn't be shared, keep the Discounts toggle ON only for the public ones. This matters — one merchant had a verification-gated discount leaked directly in chat and it was usable at checkout.

### Q6: My products / discounts aren't syncing into AI training data, or the AI says "no info" on catalog questions.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Confirm the relevant toggle is enabled under **AI agent → Training data → Manage** (Products / Discounts / Markets). Only **published, in-stock** products auto-sync, once daily — use **Sync products** to force an immediate refresh. Being Active in Shopify does **not** automatically make a product Active in Chatty; if a product shows Inactive after sync, it must be enabled manually in **Training data → Products**. After a plan upgrade, product statuses can occasionally reset to Inactive — there's no self-serve "bulk-enable all" (support can bulk-activate on request for large catalogs). For large catalogs, a CSV can also be uploaded via **Custom knowledge → Add data → Files** (max 2MB) as a supplement.

### Q6b: Two country domains (Markets) on the same Shopify store show identical FAQs/contact info — can I set different content per market?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not for FAQs, contact info, or widget settings — those are shared across the whole Shopify store/Chatty instance. The **Markets** feature only lets you set a different **AI Agent name and Store name** per market. Workaround: include both regional links/answers in one shared FAQ (e.g. "UK customers: [link] · US customers: [link]"). To get fully independent settings, you'd need two separate Shopify stores (each with its own myshopify.com URL and its own Chatty install).

### Q7: How do I get the AI to always reply in the customer's language (not English/store default)?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The AI already replies in whatever language the customer writes in, by default. If it's still answering in English, check: (1) add "Always respond in the same language the customer uses" under **AI agent → Instructions → Manage → General instructions → Behaviours"; (2) static/default content (Conversation Starters, Human Handover message, FAQs shown as buttons) is translated separately at **Settings → Translation**, since only live AI replies auto-adapt.

For the **static chatbox text specifically**, display language is determined by the **Shopify Markets domain** the visitor is on (e.g. a German-market domain shows German), not purely browser auto-detection — this can differ from what merchants expect.

### Q8: Can the AI Instructions character limit be raised?
**Frequency:** ~1 session
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Instructions has a ~1,000-character limit and support cannot raise it. For longer reference material, move it to **Training data → Custom knowledge**, which has a much higher limit. If Custom Scenarios content is also limited, split content across multiple **Custom Scenarios** instead of overloading one Behaviours field — this keeps each instruction clearer and the AI more accurate.

### Q9: What counts as "1 AI conversation" against my monthly plan limit?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new (canonical definition)

**Standard answer:**
A chat counts as 1 AI conversation only when **all three** are true:
1. The AI gives at least one valid response using trained data (welcome messages, suggested questions, and system/error notices don't count).
2. The customer actually engages (opens the chatbox and sends a message, or replies to a proactive outreach).
3. The conversation reaches completion (manual resolve, auto-resolve after inactivity, customer reset, 3-day session expiry, deletion, or block).

A **3-day inactivity window** determines session boundaries — if a customer returns within 3 days of their last message, it's still the same conversation and doesn't use extra quota. Spam-flagged messages are labeled in the Inbox but not auto-blocked, so they can still count if the AI responds and the customer engages. Test/internal conversations run by the Chatty team do not count. Check current usage at **Chatty → Subscription → Daily AI Conversation and Usage**. Full doc: help.chatty.net/ai/overview/ai-conversations/.

### Q10: Can the AI recommend products based on a specific attribute (e.g. play style, taste, size)?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring / 🆕 new detail

**Standard answer:**
Yes — go to **AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Product recommendations → Manage**, create a custom recommendation, and set search terms/triggers (e.g. "defensive," "coffee machine") linked to the specific products/collections to suggest. **Avoid modifier words like "great" or "best"** as triggers — these collide with Shopify's native Best-Selling logic and can pull unrelated top-sellers instead of the right product type; use the plain product type as the trigger instead. The AI cannot browse the internet live — recommendations only draw from trained data (Products/Collections sync + anything added to Custom Knowledge, including URLs/files).

### Q11: Can the AI use a guided "quiz" flow to recommend products (guided selling)?
**Frequency:** ~1 session
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Yes, via the **Product Quiz** feature (**AI agent → Instructions → Assistant skills → Shopping skills → Product Quiz**) — requires **Pro or Plus plan**. It asks customers a series of questions and recommends products based on their answers. The quiz runs inside the chat conversation itself (not as a standalone page), but the whole chatbox (quiz included) can be embedded on any specific page via **Chatbox → Advanced → Embedded chatbox → Go to theme**. Doc: help.chatty.net/ai/train-ai/ai-skills/product-quiz/.

## 3. Chatbox / Widget Setup & Display

### Q12: How do I reposition the chat bubble (it overlaps another button, or needs to move to a different corner)?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Chatbox → Appearance → Chatbox button → Position** — pick an anchor corner or drag the position dot, then fine-tune with the Horizontal/Vertical offset sliders (0–45%). There is **no separate desktop-vs-mobile position setting** — the same Position/offset applies to both (one known caveat: on some stores the offset only visibly moves the desktop button, not mobile — worth testing after adjusting). Precise pixel offsets require **custom CSS**, which the team can apply on request.

### Q13: FAQ page font doesn't match my theme.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring — **correction to last week's answer**

**Standard answer:**
The font control for the FAQ page is actually at **Settings → General → Font**, not FAQs → Appearance → Font (which one merchant's screen didn't even show as an option). Set it to match your theme font, or ask support to adjust it directly if the option isn't visible on your account.

### Q14: FAQ page or chatbox content isn't showing, or a specific FAQ won't appear in the FAQ block picker.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Most common root cause: the Chatty **app embed is off** in the theme (**Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds** → toggle Chatty on, Save). Two distinct new causes confirmed this week for FAQs not appearing in the **FAQs block picker** even though they're Published:
- The FAQ is filed under a **Draft-status category** — Chatty hides FAQs from any category still in Draft, even if the FAQ itself is Published. Move it to a Published category.
- The FAQ block's category search only loads the **first ~20 categories**; a category further down the list won't show up until manually reordered into roughly the top 10.
A category renamed from the default "Uncategorized" (e.g. to "Delete") also **cannot be deleted** — Chatty blocks removing the default category regardless of its display name.

### Q15: Can I hide the chatbox on specific pages, deep-link into it, or hide "Contact us"?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
To hide on specific URLs: **Chatbox → Advanced → Display settings → Display pages → "Hide on specific pages"**, add the URL or a pattern like `/products/*`. To deep-link: append `#chatty-chat` (Live Chat), `#chatty-home`, `#chatty-tracking`, or `#chatty-help` to any store URL.

To hide "Contact us" — two different places depending on what's meant: **Chatbox → General → Blocks → disable Contact** (the widget-level contact block), or **FAQs → FAQs page → Contact us → disable "Display contact us"** (the FAQ-page section). Renaming the widget's bottom-nav labels (Home/Message/Track/Help) is not currently possible — those auto-generate from enabled Blocks.

### Q16: Does clearing browser cache reset a Proactive Chat popup so it shows again, and how do I control how often it appears?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, clarified

**Standard answer:**
Proactive Chat popups show **once per browser session** by default — clearing cache does **not** reset this (a merchant testing after a cache clear will still see old content and wrongly assume an edit didn't save). On a genuinely new session (or return visit), it can trigger again — there's no native "once per visitor forever" cap. "Always display" in campaign settings refers to the campaign having no end date, not a frequency control. Each campaign has a **Devices** targeting option (Desktop/Mobile/Both) under Targeting, so behavior can differ by device.

## 4. Notifications

### Q17: I'm not getting notified when a new chat/message comes in, or I'm getting duplicate/repeated notifications.
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended

**Standard answer:**
Check **Settings → Notifications**: enable **Web push** (click **Subscribe**, then allow notifications in the browser) and/or **Email**, and toggle on the triggers you need.

Key interactions merchants get confused by:
- **Auto-assignment ON** means chats never sit "Unassigned," so the **"New unassigned conversation"** email trigger won't fire.
- **"Conversation assigned to me"** only fires on a manual assignment by someone else — never for self-assigned or auto-assigned chats.
- **"No reply for 15 minutes"** only fires for conversations that ARE assigned.
- **Web push** doesn't depend on assignment status at all and fires almost immediately — best fallback for real-time alerts regardless of setup, but requires the browser tab open (can be backgrounded, not fully closed).
- Email notifications wait **60 seconds** after a new message before sending, and are skipped if the conversation is opened within that window (someone's already on it) — this is intended, and differs from push. All 5 email trigger templates include the customer's latest message text.
- There's no built-in way to get a transcript only once when a live chat ends — pick 1–2 relevant triggers (e.g. "assigned to me," "unassigned") instead of all of them to reduce noise.

### Q18: Can AI take over automatically if a human doesn't reply within a certain time, even during business hours? / How do I set holiday/away hours?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No time-based failover exists — AI availability is schedule-based only: **"Always"** vs. **"Only when agent offline"** (Settings → General → Chat availability → Manage for business hours; AI agent → Settings → AI availability for the AI rule). For holiday/away periods, set the Holiday status and matching Behaviours instruction — a bug that had the AI ignore holiday status (quoting normal Mon–Fri hours and still offering human transfer) was fixed this week. Also worth knowing: **AI Re-engagement** and **Follow-up Messages** (AI agent → Settings) can prompt "anything else I can help with?" to keep customers engaged in a conversation.

### Q19: What are the "Welcome message," "First auto-reply," and "Proactive Chat greeting" — why do I see what looks like duplicate greetings?
**Frequency:** ~1 session
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
These are three separate features that can look like the same thing stacking:
1. **AI Welcome Message** — set at Chatbox → Chat page → Welcome Message (or AI agent → Settings → AI identity), shown when the chatbox opens.
2. **First auto-reply (Online/Offline)** — Settings → General → Chat availability → Manage, an automated first response based on team availability.
3. **Proactive Chat "Welcome visitor" campaign** — a separate popup that can appear on page load, configured under Proactive chat.
If two of these share similar text, a customer can see what looks like a duplicate greeting — check all three locations if a "duplicate message" is reported.

## 5. Team / Access

### Q20: How do I change my login email, activate my account, or find my login credentials?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**

Password resets: use **Forgot password** on the login page — support cannot view/share the password directly. ## 6. Integrations

### 6a. WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram

### Q22: How do I connect WhatsApp, and why doesn't my existing number show up? Does Chatty support WhatsApp Coexistence?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended

**Standard answer:**
Requires a Meta Business Manager account with **Admin role** — a "Manager" or Employee role is **not sufficient** on either the Facebook Page or the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). **WhatsApp Coexistence is NOT supported** — you cannot keep using the number in the personal WhatsApp Business mobile app while also connecting it to Chatty. Full migration is required: back up chat history → delete the mobile app account entirely (Settings → Account → Delete my account) → create/verify the WABA in Meta Business Suite → OTP re-verify. Once migrated, the number only works through Chatty's Inbox. A pending "Display Name" review by Meta will also block outbound messages until approved — once approved, click **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp → Sync status** in Chatty to pull the update; this review step can't be expedited by Chatty.

### Q23: Can't send/receive images or files over WhatsApp/Messenger. Can the AI reply to Instagram comments (not just DMs)?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Meta blocks any third-party app from sending files once **24 hours** have passed since the conversation started (platform policy, not a Chatty bug); within the window, occasional backend upload failures can also occur (report if seen). Attachment support varies by channel (Messenger accepts images/PDF/Word only; spreadsheets aren't supported anywhere).

Chatty can only connect to **Instagram direct messages**, not comments on posts or story replies — comment-level support isn't available. AI replies on Instagram/Messenger/WhatsApp require: connect the channel under **Settings → Channels**, then enable **AI agent → Settings → AI Channels** (Pro plan and above). Note Messenger/Instagram DM cannot render clickable hyperlinks (a Meta platform limitation) — Chatty auto-converts formatted links to plain "Label (URL)" text.

### 6b. Email

### Q24: How do I set up email forwarding into the Chatty Inbox, and why is a submitted request or notification not getting delivered?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended

**Standard answer:**
Chatty gives a unique forwarding address; add a forwarding rule in your provider pointing to it, send a test email, then reload Email settings to verify. Only one email can be bound per store via forwarding.

If "Collect info & follow up by email" submissions aren't reaching the merchant's inbox, the cause can be the **merchant's own domain SPF/DMARC settings blocking Chatty's sending server** — check if the domain's SPF record includes `amazonses.com` and whether DMARC is set to strict quarantine without proper alignment. Fix: ask the domain's IT/DNS admin to add `include:amazonses.com` to the SPF record; allow 24–48h for DNS propagation. Doc: help.chatty.net/live-chat/channels/email/fix-email-delivery-issues-with-amazon-ses.

### Q25: Does Chatty have a public API? Can I pull conversation data into my own CRM?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, on **Pro and Plus plans** — generate a key at **Settings → API**. Works for `GET /chat/conversations` (paginated, filterable) and `GET /chat/conversations/{id}/messages`. No separate "beta" enrollment is required. Role-restricted/scoped API keys aren't supported yet (feature request).

### 6c. Order tracking & Other integrations

### Q26: How does order tracking work, and can I use a custom tracking link?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Default provider is 17TRACK (no setup needed). Configure at **Settings → Integrations → Order tracking**: default carrier tracking, a **Custom tracking link** (any plan, including Free), or your own 17TRACK account/Security Key. The AI's live order-lookup (customer asks "where's my order" in chat) requires **Basic plan or higher**; the static Order Tracking widget works on any plan.

### Q27: Klaviyo integration — does it add contacts to a marketing list / grant email consent?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The native Klaviyo sync (via the pre-chat form) pushes contact info (name, email, phone, location, customer type), tags, conversation counts, and last chat timestamps — it does **not** set marketing-consent/subscribed status or add contacts to a specific list. A "Subscribe Newsletter" Proactive Chat campaign submission does **not** guarantee Klaviyo email-marketing consent either (it saves to Chatty Contacts + Shopify's own email list, not directly into Klaviyo). If a compliant, consented newsletter signup is the goal, use a real Klaviyo Signup Form instead. Postscript SMS has no integration at all. Contacts already collected can be exported from **Chatty admin → Contacts → Export** (CSV by email; not filterable by campaign source).

## 7. FAQ Management & Data Export

### Q28: Can I bulk-import or bulk-export FAQs/conversations?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**FAQs**: bulk import/export is self-serve via **FAQs → More actions → Import/Export FAQs** (CSV only, not Word). To add FAQs one at a time: **FAQs → Add new → Add category** (first time), then **Add FAQ**. Product-specific FAQs (tied to AI training for one product) have **no bulk import** — add manually per product under **AI agent → Training data → Products tab**.
**Conversations**: single-conversation export is self-serve (⋮ menu → **Export transcript**). Bulk export by date range is **not self-serve** — send the team a date range + account email and they'll export and email a CSV, usually within a day.

### Q29: Order-attribution reporting shows Chatty-assisted revenue but my orders no longer carry the old "chatty-assisted"/"chatty-direct" tags — why?
**Frequency:** ~1 session
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Chatty's attribution mechanism changed from Shopify order **tags** to order **metafields** (`Chatty Assisted` / `Chatty Direct` boolean fields — doc: help.chatty.net/analytics/Chatty-Attribution-Metafields). To view/filter by them in Shopify admin: **Settings → Metafields and metaobjects → Orders**, enable filtering on both fields, then filter the **Orders** list by them. An order qualifies as Chatty Assisted if the customer chatted with Chatty within 7 days before purchase; Chatty Direct only if the purchase happened directly through Chatty. Note: some orders from before the metafield rollout may not retroactively reflect in the new field even though Analytics counted them historically — flag to support for review if numbers don't reconcile.

## 8. Translation

### Q30: How many languages can my chatbox support, and how many are auto-translated? Why do my translations keep reverting to English?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring, extended (bug)

**Standard answer:**
Add the language in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages** first, then **Settings → Translation → Add language** in Chatty. Click **Auto-translate** (or edit manually), **Save**, then **Publish → Confirm**.

| Plan | Languages the chatbox can publish | Of those, auto-translated by Chatty |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | 0 — manual only |
| Basic | 3 | 2 |
| Pro | 10 | 9 |
| Plus | Unlimited | Unlimited |

**Known recurring quirk:** editing/adding a FAQ in the default language can mark OTHER language translations as "stale," causing them to silently revert to showing English until re-translated and re-published — this can interact badly with a separate translation app (e.g. Langify) overwriting Chatty's FAQ translation data. If translations keep disappearing after edits, re-check and re-publish **Settings → Translation** after every FAQ change, and confirm no other translation app is writing to the same fields.

## 10. Edge Cases / Bugs / Feature Requests

### Q34: Can Chatty block spam or repeat-abuse messages, or block a specific customer/email permanently?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No native IP-level blocking, bot-detection, or CAPTCHA, and no permanent block by email address — blocking inside Chatty is session-based only (open the conversation → visitor profile → Block). Mitigations: require the pre-chat form to add friction, set a spending cap, or turn on **Settings → Automation → Spam protection → Smart spam protection** (auto-classifies and blocks spam/abusive messages). To reduce notification noise about a specific customer, have each teammate turn off unneeded triggers in their own Settings → Notifications.

### Q35: Can I exclude specific products from the AI (no recommend, no add-to-cart)? Can subscription products be added to cart via chat?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Yes — set the product's status to **Disabled** under **AI agent → Training data → Products** (distinct from just not training on it). Once disabled, the AI won't recommend, reference, or add it to cart. Subscription products currently **cannot** be added to cart directly through AI chat at all — the customer must select a subscription plan first, which the in-chat flow doesn't support; support can disable the "Add to Cart" button on AI recommendation cards for an account if this is confusing customers.

### Q36: Can I tag conversations by topic, and can tags be auto-assigned by keyword? Can I control what the Satisfaction Survey triggers on?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Status:** 🔁 recurring / 🆕 new detail

**Standard answer:**
Manual conversation tags are supported and filterable; automatic tag assignment by keyword is **not** available (feature request).

Satisfaction Survey trigger: **Settings → Automation → Satisfaction Survey → Manage → Trigger time** — options are keyword-based (default, e.g. a "thanks" match) or **"Conversation is resolved"** (only fires if conversations are actually being resolved — check whether Automatic Resolution is enabled, or resolves are being done manually).

## GAP Detail

### Q1 — Installing on a second store: what carries over
**File:** `kb/reference/getting-started.md`
**Missing sub-point:** The file's Setup section covers first-time setup on one store but never addresses installing Joy Wishlist on a second/additional store. One merchant asked directly whether installing on another store keeps "all the functions" (session 14) — agent correctly answered features are the same but settings/design must be configured fresh per store, since nothing carries over. Cheap, low-risk fact to document.
**Priority:** Low-medium (1 session, simple factual addition)

### Q2 — Heart icon on product image (not next to Add to Cart)
**File:** `kb/reference/wishlist-design.md`, § Wishlist Button
**Missing sub-point:** § Wishlist Button documents Button Style options and general positioning/CSS limits but doesn't state whether the icon can be placed directly over the product image itself (a distinct ask from repositioning near Add to Cart). One merchant asked for exactly this and was told it's not supported, logged as feedback (session 10).
**Priority:** Medium (a clean, confirmed "not supported" fact — cheap to add and prevents the bot from over-promising a custom-CSS fix that doesn't actually exist for this specific placement)

**File:** `kb/reference/integrations.md`, § Joy Loyalty

**Priority:** Medium-high (this reads as a genuine pre-sales/evaluation question likely to recur for merchants running Joy Loyalty + Joy Wishlist together — cheap and safe to document since it's a confirmed answer from the Joy Loyalty team itself)

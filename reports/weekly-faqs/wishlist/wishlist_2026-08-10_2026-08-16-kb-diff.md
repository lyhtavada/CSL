## GAP Detail

### Q6 — Icon missing/not repositionable in "You may also like" recommendation blocks
**File:** `kb/reference/wishlist-design.md`, § Wishlist Button
**Missing sub-point:** The current § Wishlist Button documents three scoped toggles (product details page / collection page / home page) and their global-only scoping. It doesn't address recommendation-style blocks ("You may also like," related-products carousels) which use a distinct card template not covered by any of the three toggles. Two chats this window hit this: a merchant wanted the icon added to their "You may also like" section (session 2) and another had the icon not appear there at all despite it working elsewhere (session 18). Both were resolved by the team applying custom setup on request, not a self-serve toggle.
**Priority:** Medium (2 sessions, ~9% of relevant volume, real merchant confusion about scope)

### Q7 — Cart drawer: native Shopify only vs. third-party cart apps
**File:** New sub-section needed, likely under `kb/reference/wishlist-design.md` (§ Wishlist Button or a new § Cart Drawer) or `kb/reference/integrations.md`.
**Missing sub-point:** No file in the current 18-file KB mentions the cart-drawer wishlist option at all — not `wishlist-design.md`, not `settings.md`, not `integrations.md`. Two chats surfaced this: one asking directly whether the cart-drawer integration only works with the native Shopify cart drawer or also third-party cart apps (session 4, answer: native only, third-party not guaranteed), and one where the merchant hadn't enabled a cart drawer at all — only a cart page — so the Wishlist option had nowhere to render (session 15).
**Priority:** Medium-high (2 sessions, and this is a completely undocumented feature area — worth a full sub-section, not just a note)

## GAP / PARTIAL detail — patched this run (14 edits across 12 files)

**Q7 (freq 4) — self-serve "Sign up account → Sync" button missing from the KB's escalation-only guidance.** `kb__case__points-earning.md`'s Signup section only tells CS to escalate for extending signup points to pre-existing customers. This week's chats show a genuine **self-serve path**: Settings → Pre-launch → Sign up account → Sync. **Patched** — added as Step 3a before the escalation fallback.

**Q17 (freq 2) — redeem block disappearing below the original redeemed cart value, undocumented.** A real, verifiable, working-as-designed behavior with no KB coverage at all — merchants report "the redeem block/discount vanished" when it's actually the cart dropping below the value it was generated against. **Patched** into `kb__reference__redeeming-programs.md`.

**Q18 (freq 1) — coupon manually extended in Shopify still shows "Expired" in Joy history.** Cosmetic desync, undocumented, quick add. **Patched** into the same file, near the "conditions cannot be edited" note.

**Q20 (freq 1) — points earned are calculated on the post-discount subtotal, not pre-discount.** A concrete, verifiable calculation detail missing from the Calculation notes. **Patched** into `kb__reference__earning-programs.md`.

**Q24 (freq 1) — Sign-up reward has no in-widget claim button.** Genuinely useful for CS to know up front rather than troubleshoot as a bug each time. **Patched** into `kb__reference__widget.md`.

**Q27 (freq 1, high operational risk) — VIP threshold/rule change recalculates ALL customers and can re-fire Tier Achieved emails; large stores take hours.** `kb__case__vip-tiers.md`'s "Mass Tier Reset" section explains the *what* (re-assessment, not data loss) but not the *emails-refire* or *hours-long-on-large-stores* operational risk, which is exactly what a merchant needs to hear **before** clicking Relaunch. **Patched.**

**Q30 (freq 1) — B2B Partner Tier + retail Exclusive-tier combined architecture undocumented.** Both pieces exist separately in the KB but nothing connects them for the "Trade Account gated to a B2B tier, but the gating mechanism is a DTC Exclusive tier" pattern. **Patched** into `kb__reference__membership-b2b.md`.

**Q33 (freq 1) — email logo blurry when reusing the Shopify shop logo.** Quick, concrete, verifiable fix. **Patched** into `kb__reference__notifications.md`.

**Q36 (freq 1, high value/risk-prevention) — Klaviyo billing spike from deleted-then-recreated contacts.** Zero KB coverage; this can cost a merchant real money and get misattributed to Joy as "your app is spamming our Klaviyo bill." **Patched** into `kb__reference__integrations-email.md`.

**Q37 (freq 1) — Shopify Flow "Add point by variable" 404, fixed by rounding a decimal presentment value.** Concrete, reusable technical fix. **Patched** into `kb__reference__integrations-shopify-flow.md`.

**Q38 (freq 1) — WhatsApp is consent-capture only, not native sending.** The existing SMS/WhatsApp consent section doesn't explicitly rule out native WhatsApp *sending*, which is exactly the question merchants ask next. **Patched** into `kb__reference__widget.md`.

**Q45 (freq 1) — store credit expiration is always 11:59 PM GMT+0.** Concrete, verifiable, prevents a recurring "why did it expire at a weird time" ticket. **Patched** into `kb__reference__points-advanced.md`.

**Q48 (freq 1) — duplicating a program for safe testing, plus the Loyalty Hub-stays-visible caveat.** Useful process tip with a real gotcha (unlaunched program can still show on the account page). **Patched** into `kb__reference__rule-engine.md`'s Sandbox Mode section.

**Q49 (freq 1) — export field list clarity + an automatic monthly report some stores receive.** Existing export docs are solid but didn't mention the monthly automated summary email. **Patched** into `kb__reference__analytics.md`.

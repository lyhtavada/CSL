## 1. Getting Started, Setup & Launch

### Q1: How do I set up Joy from scratch?
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Start at **Joy Admin → Reward programs**: create your **Place order** earning rule (points or store credit per $ spent), then a **Redeeming program** (discount, free gift or free shipping). Build the storefront presence: add the **Joy Loyalty** page template at **Online Store → Themes → Customize** (Essential+ for the dedicated page; free-plan merchants use the `#joy-home` widget deeplink instead), then enable the widget embed at **Theme Editor → App embeds → Joy Loyalty - Widget → Save**.

For a fuller build (VIP Tiers, Referral, Birthday rewards — all Essential+ or above depending on the piece), configure each program, then test with **Sandbox Mode** (see Q4) before going live. If you want a review before launch or a fully custom program built to your spec (e.g. tiered order-value rules), our team can walk through it with you or build it directly from a filled-in template sheet.

### Q2: Can your team set up or style my widget / loyalty page for free?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — this is a free design/setup service on any plan. Share a reference design or your brand colors/fonts and, if you'd like the team to configure it directly (not just advise), grant **collaborator access** via your 4-digit code (**Shopify Admin → Settings → Users → Security → Collaborator request code**). The team styles the widget and/or loyalty page and sends a preview before anything goes live on your storefront. Typical turnaround is next-day.

### Q3: What's included on each plan (Starter vs Essential vs Advanced vs Ultimate)?
**Frequency:** ~18 sessions 🔁 recurring (cuts across every category)

**Standard answer:**
Pricing is **base monthly fee + free order quota + per-100 overage**:

| Plan | Base | Free orders/mo | Overage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $0 | 250 | n/a |
| **Essential** | $29/mo | 500 | $15/100 |
| **Advanced** | $129/mo | 2,000 | $10/100 |
| **Ultimate** | $499/mo | 7,000 | $5/100 |

Feature gating that comes up most: **VIP Tiers, Rule Engine, Cart Drawer redemption, Milestones, Loyalty Hub (account page), B2B tiers, Wallet passes** — Advanced and Ultimate only. **Points expiration, dedicated loyalty page, birthday rewards, free gift/free shipping redemption, analytics, migration** — Essential and up. **Checkout-page redemption, API/webhooks, Hydrogen/headless** — Ultimate only (checkout also needs Shopify Plus). **Essential is limited to a single Place-order earning rule** — running more than one simultaneously (for stacking bonuses, e.g. a channel-specific rule alongside the base rate) requires upgrading to **Rule Engine** (Advanced+). Free trials: 14 days (Essential/Advanced), 30 days (Ultimate), one-time only per store — if the trial isn't needed, the **30-day refund policy** after upgrading is the fallback, not a trial extension. Compare and upgrade at **Settings → Subscription → All plans**.

### Q4: How do I test my program before launching (Sandbox Mode)?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Turn on **Sandbox mode** at **Reward programs → Sandbox Mode**, then click **Test list** and add your test email(s) — only those accounts earn points while sandbox is active. Sign in to the storefront with a test account and run through signing up, placing an order, and redeeming, then verify the points landed under **Joy Admin → Customers**. Two things that catch people out: the Joy embed widget must be active (an account earning nothing while logged in is usually a disabled embed, not a sandbox issue), and an email not on the test list earns nothing even though the customer is real.

### Q5: Can I migrate or import data from another loyalty app?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Full migration is available on **Essential, Advanced and Ultimate**, and can bring over customer name, email, phone, VIP tier name, birth date, member status and point balance. There are dedicated guides for Smile.io, Rivo, Yotpo Loyalty, LoyaltyLion, Stamped Loyalty, Bon Loyalty and Appstle; anything else (Marsello, Smartrr, Growave, custom platforms) uses the generic CSV route — the CSV only strictly requires a Point column, since identity (email/phone/ID) and name sync from Shopify directly.

Two paths: **Import** (self-serve, no enabling needed) at **Customers → Import** — covers point balance and birthday only, on any plan. **Migrate** (assisted, full data set) — the tool at **Settings → Migration** is locked until our team enables it; tell us which app you're moving from and what data you need. Coupon codes issued by the old platform are **not** imported as Joy coupons.

## 2. Points — Earning

### Q6: A customer placed an order but got no points — why?
**Frequency:** ~14 sessions 🔁 recurring (largest topic this week)

**Standard answer:**
Work through these in order:
1. **Order hasn't reached the reward status yet** — points fire on the trigger set in Settings → Order (Paid or Fulfilled); the underlying webhook can also lag Shopify's side by 40–90 minutes.
2. **Customer checked out as Guest, not a Member** — if your rule rewards members only, a guest order earns nothing.
3. **Program was still in Sandbox/Test mode** when the order was placed — only test-list emails earn during that window; switching to Live afterward doesn't retroactively credit orders placed while sandboxed.
4. **Payment method excluded** — a program can be configured to exclude orders paid via Store Credit or gift card.
5. **Program date range, product/collection conditions, sales-channel conditions, or order-level exclusions** (tag, source, payment gateway, B2B) don't match — including custom/non-standard sales channels (e.g. a bespoke B2B ordering app) that don't sync the same way POS/online-store orders do.

If none of these explain it, send us the order number and customer email and we'll check the activity log directly — and if the earning rule itself was misconfigured (e.g. a product-tag condition that no products actually have), we can run a retroactive order sync for a specified date range once it's fixed.

### Q7: Why does every customer already show as a "Member," and why didn't they get the sign-up bonus?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
These are two different things. **Becoming a Member** happens automatically: with Shopify's Customer Accounts (new/SSO), anyone who ever leaves an email anywhere on the store — checkout, a newsletter form, account creation — gets marked "Enabled" by Shopify, and Joy reads that as a Member. This is *not* the same as earning the **Sign-up reward**, which only fires when the customer actually **logs in** with the Joy embed widget active, joins via the **widget popup**, or is identified as a member at **POS**. Leaving an email alone never grants the reward, even though the customer already counts as a Member.

If you don't want customers auto-joining just from leaving an email, turn on **Settings → General → Customer Eligibility → Manually assigned customers only** (or use **Customer opt-in**, though that only affects new customers going forward, not already-synced ones). The other common cause of a missing sign-up bonus: the account was created **before** the program's start date — existing customers don't qualify by default; ask us for a manual pass if you want them included.

### Q8: Can I run a time-limited promotion (2× points) or stack multiple earning rules?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
On **Essential** (single earning rule only): manually raise the rate on your one Place-order rule for the promo period and revert it afterward — there's no scheduled auto-revert on this plan. On **Advanced/Ultimate**, upgrade to **Rule Engine** (Reward programs → Earning programs → More actions) and create a separate rule with the boosted rate plus a start/end date; it auto-reverts and can be scoped to a specific sales channel, collection, or customer segment.

Multiple rules **stack** by default unless their conditions prevent overlap — a base "1pt/$1 on everything" rule and a "2× on Summer Collection" rule both apply on a qualifying order. Two caveats worth knowing: a collection-scoped bonus is calculated on the whole order subtotal, not just the qualifying items in a mixed cart (no per-line-item restriction), and the widget's "Applies to" label can show blank next to a collection-restricted rule even though the rule works correctly (a cosmetic display gap, not a functional one).

### Q9: The Point Calculator shows a different number than what a customer actually earns, or my custom calculator text broke the live value.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The Point Calculator on the product page estimates from the **VAT/tax-inclusive displayed price**, since tax isn't known until checkout — actual points are calculated on the **order subtotal excluding tax**, matching normal earning-rule behavior. This can make the calculator's estimate look slightly higher than what's actually credited; it's expected, not a bug. If you want to avoid confusion, consider wording it as "You could earn *up to* X points" rather than a flat number.

When editing the calculator's text (**On-site content → Product page → Point calculator → Content & Translations**), keep the placeholder variable in your copy or the live point value stops updating — e.g. `"You could earn {{earning_point_raw}} {{loyalty_point}} with this purchase!"`. `{{earning_point_raw}}` is the live number, `{{loyalty_point}}` is your point-name label.

### Q10: Customer wrote a review but didn't get points.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check, in order: (1) the review app is **Connected** under Integrations, and a **Write a review** program exists; (2) the review is actually **Published** in the review app, not still pending; (3) the customer is a **Member**, not a guest. The most common cause: **Settings → General → Default status for Product reviews** (or Google Maps reviews) is set to **Pending** — points wait for manual approval in **Customers → Activities → Review activities**, not a bug. Switch it to **Approved** to auto-credit future reviews. Note Judge.me's own "not eligible for coupon" message is unrelated to Joy points — it's Judge.me's separate Coupons feature.

### Q11: Social/Instagram/follow points aren't being awarded.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Social follow/share programs are **trust-based** — there's no API to verify a real follow, so points are awarded when the customer clicks "Complete" in the widget. This is an industry-wide limitation, not Joy-specific. For **Instagram-specific** programs (comment, story reply/mention, live comment), the customer must first be a Member, then **link their Instagram username inside the Joy widget's "Ways to Earn" section** before the tracked action — posting or mentioning your brand without linking first won't be detected, since Joy has no way to auto-discover an unlinked account. Story-based actions require the Instagram mobile app (not web/desktop). Requires an Instagram Business/Creator account connected under Integrations.

### Q12: Can you retroactively award points for orders placed before the earning rule existed?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
There's no self-serve retroactive sync — our team runs it manually for a merchant-specified date range, applying the **current** rule settings (rate, product eligibility) to historical orders and skipping cancelled/refunded ones. It doesn't touch other programs (e.g. sign-up bonus) — those need their own separate sync request if you want existing customers included. If you also use VIP Tiers, let us know whether tier points should be recalculated at the same time; the tier program has its own start date that gates which historical points count toward tier progress, separate from the redeemable balance.

### Q13: A long-time customer is still on a low VIP tier — why?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
VIP tier progress is based on **total lifetime points/spend/orders earned toward the tier**, not the current **redeemable balance** — the two are separate metrics. A customer with 810 lifetime points and a 1,000-point tier threshold is still 190 points short even if they've already redeemed most of that balance down to a low number. Check the customer's lifetime tier points (not their spendable balance) against the tier threshold before assuming something's broken.

### Q14: I'm hitting a "transaction limit reached" message — can I get unlimited transactions?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
A **transaction** here means any point-related activity — a customer earning points from an order, or redeeming points for a reward — not a Shopify order or payment. Unlimited transactions is **not** a paid-plan feature and isn't tied to your plan; any merchant who asks can have it enabled. We can't turn it on directly in chat — share your store URL and we'll pass it to the team to enable, usually at no extra cost.

### Q15: How does point expiration timing actually work?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Set up at **Reward programs → Point Expiration**. Three modes: **Full inactivity** (Essential+) — the whole balance expires after N days with no earn/spend activity; any activity resets the clock for the *entire* balance, not just recent points. **Fixed date** (Essential+) — everything expires on one calendar date regardless of when earned. **Expiration by earning date / FIFO** (Ultimate only) — each batch expires N days after it was individually earned. If a customer's points expired sooner than expected, check which mode is active and confirm the clock is running from their actual last activity, not the store launch date. Points earned before a mode switch keep the old logic unless the team runs a sync to reapply the new one.

## 3. Redemption & Discount Codes

### Q16: How do customers redeem, and does the code apply automatically?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Widget or loyalty page: log in, open **Redeem points**, pick a reward, click **Redeem**, then either click **Apply** to push the code to cart or copy it to paste at checkout manually. **The code doesn't apply itself** — this remains the most common "my discount disappeared" report. Unused codes stay retrievable from **My coupons** in the widget.

### Q17: My Joy discount code won't combine with another discount at checkout.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
First check **Combinations** on the redeeming program (Product/Order/Shipping classes) — Joy coupons don't stack by default, and the setting only affects **newly issued** codes. Shopify's own rules apply on top regardless of your setting (e.g. Shipping+Shipping never combines; Product+Order and Order+Order need eligible-merchant status or Shopify Plus).

If two **Order**-class discounts are conflicting (a common case: a Joy coupon plus a second unrelated order-level code), one workaround beyond the Combinations toggle is changing the Joy coupon's underlying **discount class** — adding a product/collection condition to the redeeming program (e.g. restricting it to "all products") makes Joy generate a **Product**-class coupon instead of an Order-class one, which can combine differently with the conflicting discount. This is a configuration technique, not a bug fix — test the resulting combination behavior before relying on it. Note also: **Shopify's native store credit** doesn't have a Combinations setting at all (it's applied as an account balance, not a discount code), so it can't be blocked from stacking with a discount code through Joy — that would need to go through Shopify Support.

### Q18: Can I set a minimum order requirement or multiple tiered redemption levels?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — each redeeming program has its own **Minimum order requirement** field (Reward programs → Redeeming programs → [program] → Settings). For tiered redemption (e.g. "100pts = $3 off," "500pts = $18 off"), create multiple separate **Fixed Discount** redeem rules, each at its own point cost. To cap the discount value on a **Dynamic Discount** program instead, use the **Maximum discount % of cart total** field under that program's Reward value section — a true fixed dollar cap requires switching that same program to **Fixed Discount** mode (other settings like min order, combinations, and expiration carry over when you switch).

### Q19: Can used, one-time discount codes be auto-deleted or auto-expired from Shopify's discount list?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No — Joy doesn't currently auto-expire or auto-delete a one-time-use code from Shopify's discounts list once it hits its usage limit, and this isn't on the near-term roadmap. Two workarounds: filter Shopify Admin → Discounts by **"Eligibility is not specific customer"** (Joy-issued codes are always customer-specific, so this filter hides them), or search by your redeeming program's custom code prefix and bulk-delete manually. Separately, each redeeming program does have its own **expiration** setting that deactivates a coupon after a set duration — that's different from removing it from the Shopify discounts list.

### Q20: A reward I redeemed from the cart drawer got cancelled or won't apply.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This is a known limitation specific to the **Redeem block inside the Cart Drawer** — a reward redeemed from there can get revoked or fail to apply correctly. If you hit this, redeem the same reward from the **widget** or **loyalty page** instead, which don't have this issue. We're tracking this with the team; let us know if it's affecting checkouts regularly.

### Q21: A customer redeemed the same discount code twice.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Each redeeming program's **Redemption limit** card lets you set a per-customer cap — if that's left blank, nothing stops a customer from generating and using the same coupon type more than once. Set **Each customer's limit** on the program to prevent repeats going forward; for a code that's already been used more than intended, deactivate it directly in Shopify Admin → Discounts.

## 4. VIP Tiers & Milestones

### Q22: I edited a VIP tier (threshold, entry reward) but the change doesn't seem to apply.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Changes to a VIP tier's thresholds, calculation rule, or entry reward are **saved but stay inactive** until you **re-Launch** the tier program (Membership → VIP Tiers → Launch). If you're adding an entry reward to a tier that customers are already in, relaunching also shows a separate **"grant entry reward"** checkbox — leave it **unchecked** unless you actually want every existing member of that tier to receive the reward retroactively; checking it on a low/base tier in particular can issue a large unexpected batch of rewards. Re-launching also triggers a full re-assessment of every customer against the current rules, which is expected, not data loss.

### Q23: What VIP tier features need which plan, and can tiers apply per-order automatically?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
VIP Tiers themselves (up to 5+ tiers, per-tier perks, different earning rates per tier) require **Advanced or Ultimate**. **Tier Assessment** (rolling-window re-evaluation to keep/demote based on spend/orders over time) is Advanced/Ultimate plus needs our team to enable it per-shop. A per-tier discount can apply **automatically at checkout with no code** — configure it as a **Bonus Points/Discount privilege perk** under Tier privileges; it behaves like an automatic discount and can be combined with point redemption via the perk's own Combinations setting. **Redeem-at-checkout with zero friction** is Ultimate + Shopify Plus only. The **Loyalty Dashboard block** (points/tier/history on the account page) is Advanced+.

### Q24: What can store credit be used as a reward for, and can customers use only part of their balance?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Store credit can only be granted as a reward from **three** sources: a **Place Order** earning rule, a **Milestone** reward (Advanced+), or the **Referral** program. Because store credit is Shopify's native balance rather than a Joy coupon, Joy has no control over partial use — once a customer opts to apply their store credit at checkout, Shopify applies the **entire balance**, not a chosen portion. There's no per-order cap or partial-redeem option for store credit today.

### Q25: My tier isn't syncing to Klaviyo — is that a bug?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not necessarily. **Exclusive tiers** are manually assigned, not auto-progressing — if **zero customers** are currently assigned to that tier, there's simply nothing yet to sync to Klaviyo. Once at least one customer is manually assigned to the tier, their `joy_vip_tier` property starts syncing to their Klaviyo profile normally.

## 5. Referral

### Q26: When exactly does the referrer get rewarded?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Referrer shares their link → referee clicks it, lands on the referral page, enters their email → a **7-day cookie** stores the pending status → referee places an order **with the same email** → the referrer's reward issues once that order reaches your shop's earn-status trigger (Paid by default; shows "Awaiting fulfilment" if you require Fulfilled). The referee's own reward issues immediately at the claim step, not at order time.

### Q27: Can I extend the 7-day referral cookie window? What happens if it expires before the friend orders?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
The tracking cookie is **fixed at 7 days** with no merchant-facing setting to extend it — but the link itself never expires and generates a fresh 7-day cookie on every new click. Importantly, even if the cookie technically expires before the referred friend orders, the referrer can still be rewarded if the friend's order uses the **same email** they entered when claiming the referral — Joy matches by order email, not solely by the cookie.

### Q28: Is there any verification before a referral discount code is issued to the referee?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
By design, no — the referee's discount is issued as soon as they enter an email on the referral landing page, with no account or email-ownership verification at that step. This is intentional, to keep the referral flow low-friction. Fraud is controlled separately by **Anti-cheat** (blocks referrals sharing the same browser/email/IP, or a $0-subtotal order) rather than at the code-issuance step.

## 6. Widget & On-site Display

### Q29: The launcher button overlaps my cart / chat / navigation. Can I move it precisely?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring (still a top topic)

**Standard answer:**
Rough clearance is self-serve: **On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup → Settings → Launcher** → Alignment (bottom-left/bottom-right), size, border radius, plus **Advanced → Display → Page restriction** to hide it on conflicting pages.

**Exact placement** needs custom CSS from our team — send a screenshot of the target position, page URL, and device, plus your collaborator code. As a reference, a common mobile-only fix looks like:
```css
@media screen and (max-device-width: 480px) {
  #Avada-Joy_FloatingButton { position: fixed !important; bottom: 80px !important; left: 20px; z-index: 1200; }
}
```
Adjust the `bottom` value to move it up/down. If a conflicting floating button on the page isn't Joy's, we can't move it — several cases each week turn out to belong to another app.

### Q30: Should I be on the Classic or Unified widget?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Unified (v4)** is the current, actively-developed version; **Classic (v3)** is being phased out and is no longer offered to new setups. Check which you're on at **On-site content → Widget → Setup**: a full sidebar (Header/Body/Footer) = v4, horizontal tabs (Widget theme/Membership card/Display) = v3. Switch anytime via **Switch to Unified** — all data carries over. A handful of features (Milestone UI, wallet pass/QR, submit-receipt) still render in Classic style even on Unified stores.

### Q31: The widget isn't showing on the storefront at all.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check these, in order:
1. **App embed off** — Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → **Joy Loyalty - Widget** must be on.
2. **"Hide widget button" enabled** — On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup → Advanced → Display.
3. **"Display after login" is on** — guests never see it; test while logged in first.
4. **Customer Eligibility set to "Manually assigned customers only"** — Settings → General — hides the widget from everyone not manually added.

Note the widget also can't display on Shopify's **New Customer Account "Profile" page** specifically — that's a Shopify platform limitation, not a Joy bug; it shows normally on every other storefront page after login. If none of the four checks resolve it, send us the store URL.

### Q32: My widget/loyalty page text isn't translating correctly, or translations reverted.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check **Settings → Translations** for the default language, and check for a third-party translation app (Translate & Adapt) overriding Joy's fields. Two things that genuinely can't translate through Joy: reward product names (pulled from the Shopify product title itself) and some VIP tier description strings inside Customer Accounts. If custom translations you'd already entered suddenly reverted to English (commonly after a theme update), that's a real bug worth escalating with the specific fields affected — don't use "Revert to default," since that wipes *all* custom translations, not just the broken ones.

### Q33: Widget doesn't display on the Shopify New Customer Account "Profile" page.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This is expected — Joy's floating widget cannot render on Shopify's **New Customer Account Profile page** specifically, due to a technical limitation on Shopify's side, not a Joy bug. The widget displays normally on every other storefront page once the customer is logged in.

### Q34: Can a customer edit their own birthday after they've entered it?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No — once a customer submits their birthday through the widget or a registration form, they cannot change it themselves afterward. If it needs correcting, the merchant can update it from the customer's profile in **Joy Admin → Customers**.

## 7. Integrations & POS

### Q35: POS tile/redemption isn't working (error, stuck loading, out of stock).
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
First self-fix: update the Shopify POS app to the latest version, then remove and re-add the Joy tile from scratch. Confirm the relevant redeem/earn program has **Sales channels** set to include **POS** — programs aren't POS-enabled by default. If it persists, we'll need device model, OS version, and Shopify POS app version to reproduce it, plus (for redemption specifically) whether the "out of stock" message is coming from Shopify POS itself checking a free-gift product's inventory rather than from Joy.

### Q36: How does Joy sync with Klaviyo/Omnisend, and what variables do I use?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Both sync the same **profile properties**: points balance, VIP tier, member status, referral link, birthday — updated automatically as they change in Joy. Klaviyo template syntax: `{{ person|lookup:'Joy Loyalty Points'|default:'' }}` for profile data; use `event|lookup` instead of `person|lookup` for data carried by a triggering event (e.g. bonus points on an Earn/Tier-reach flow), since profile properties can briefly lag the event by a few seconds. If a tier-reach email shows the wrong/previous tier, that's this exact lag — switch that specific email to the **event** property, not the profile property. Omnisend has the same fields as Klaviyo plus a manual **Sync Again** button if a recent change hasn't reflected yet.

### Q37: The Shopify Pixel "Connect" button doesn't complete — it just reopens the Joy app.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This is a recognized, recurring issue. First fix: reopen the Joy app and re-accept/re-authorize the permission prompt — Shopify sometimes requires a freshly-scoped grant that the first click doesn't complete. Then check **Shopify Admin → Settings → Customer Events** (or Checkout Settings → Tracking and analytics → App pixels) for "Connected" status. If re-authorizing doesn't fix it, toggling the **Referral program** off and back on can also refresh the pixel connection, since the pixel is tied to referral tracking specifically. If a merchant only uses Joy for points/VIP tiers with no referral, sign-up, or reward-celebration popups, **no App Blocks are required** on the Thank You/Order Status page at all — the pixel only matters for those optional features.

## 8. Billing & Pricing

### Q39: My usage/order-quota fee seems too high — is it only counting Joy-assisted orders?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No — the usage fee is calculated on **every** order processed through your store, regardless of sales channel (POS, Amazon, TikTok, marketplace-synced) or whether that specific order used a Joy discount, because Joy syncs all orders in real time to track program activity across the board. If you want a specific channel excluded from the count (e.g. a marketplace channel you don't consider "real store traffic"), that's possible via a consistent order tag, but it removes those orders from the loyalty program entirely too (no points, no rewards) — confirm you're OK with that trade-off before we set it up.

### Q40: Can I extend my free trial?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No — the 14-day (Essential/Advanced) or 30-day (Ultimate) trial is strictly one-time per store and can't be paused or extended once billing has started, even mid-setup. If you upgrade and it turns out to be premature, the **30-day refund policy** after upgrading is the safety net, not a trial extension.

## 9. Notifications

### Q41: My new store's notifications are disabled and I can't turn them on.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Stores under 7 days old (installed after 2025-01-13) have Notifications disabled by default as an anti-phishing precaution — this is temporary and lifts automatically once the store passes 7 days, or our team can verify and enable it sooner on request. This is expected behavior, not a bug or a plan restriction.

### Q42: A notification variable like `{{birthday_rewards}}` shows raw/untranslatable text — can I edit it?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No — some system-generated notification variables (e.g. `{{birthday_rewards}}`) produce fixed text that can't be directly edited or translated in the template editor. Workaround: delete the variable from your email template and type your own custom wording instead. If you only need the reward's **value** (not the full auto-generated sentence), use the singular `{{birthday_reward}}` variable, which returns just the discount value so you can wrap it in your own custom text.

## 10. Customer Data & Reporting

### Q43: How do I export customer/points data, and why don't I see an Export button?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Customers → Activities** → set a date range → Export type (Earned/Redeemed) → enter your email for the file. Full customer list export lives under **Customers → Export**; if you don't see that button, it needs to be enabled by our team first — share your store URL and we'll turn it on. Exports are **CSV only** (no native Excel) — open in Google Sheets or convert manually if needed. There's no in-app sort by points value or combined "Customer name + Order ID + Points earned" report — for that specific combination, the team can generate it manually on request for a given period; the standard export doesn't include the VIP tier column by default either, but the team can add it.

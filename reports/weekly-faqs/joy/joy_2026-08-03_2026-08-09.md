## 1. Getting Started, Setup & Launch

### Q1: How do I set up Joy from scratch?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Start at **Joy Admin → Reward programs**: create your **Place order** earning rule (points or store credit per $ spent), then a **Redeeming program** (discount, free gift, or free shipping). Build the storefront presence: add the **Joy Loyalty** page via **Online Store → Themes → Customize → Add section → Joy Loyalty**, then enable the widget at **Theme Editor → App embeds → Joy Loyalty - Widget → Save**. Test everything with **Sandbox Mode** before going live (see Q8) — this is also where most first-time confusion starts (Q8).

Our team also offers a free design/setup service — grant collaborator access and we'll style the widget/loyalty page to match your brand and send a preview before anything goes live.

### Q2: Can I test the app on a dev store, or get a trial of a higher plan to evaluate a specific feature?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Dev stores can't process real app charges, so our team can set up trial access manually for a specific feature you want to evaluate (e.g. the Account page / Loyalty Hub on the Advanced plan). Share what you're testing and your store URL, and we'll enable it. Note: **the dedicated Loyalty page** (a standalone landing page, distinct from the Account page block) needs Essential or above.

### Q3: Can I migrate program setup (not just customer data) from one Joy store to another?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No — only **customer loyalty data** (points, tiers, activity) can be migrated between Joy accounts. Program configuration itself (earning rules, redeeming rules, VIP tiers, widget design) has to be rebuilt manually on the new store; there's no clone/copy-config tool today.

### Q4: Can Joy cover [long checklist of loyalty + affiliate/commission + discount-code requirements] without custom development?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
For detailed multi-part evaluations (independent earn/redeem rates, partial point redemption, automated point deduction on return, an ongoing per-partner affiliate/commission model, bulk custom-code import, native Wishlist, etc.), our AI/chat can confirm the items that map directly to documented features (e.g. Judge.me is a native direct review-points integration; newsletter-signup points trigger off Shopify's own marketing-consent status, no separate ESP required) but the rest needs a written answer from our product/specialist team — we'll pass along the full list and follow up in writing, or you can book a call with a specialist for a faster back-and-forth.

## 2. Points — Earning

### Q5: A customer placed an order but got no points — why?
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Work through these in order:
1. **Program is still in Sandbox/Test mode** — the single most common cause this week; only test-list emails earn while sandbox is on, and switching to Live afterward does **not** retroactively credit orders placed while sandboxed (our team can run a manual re-sync for a specified date range on request).
2. **Order hasn't reached the reward status yet** — points fire on the Paid or Fulfilled trigger (Settings → Order); the underlying webhook can also lag 40–90 minutes on Shopify's side.
3. **Customer checked out as Guest, not Member** — if the rule rewards Members only, a guest order earns nothing. Note: with Shopify's New Customer Accounts, leaving an email anywhere (checkout, newsletter) is enough to make Shopify treat the account as "enabled," so Joy also converts them to a Member automatically — but if it's the **old/Legacy** account system, the customer must actually create a password account and log in first.
4. **At POS specifically:** the staff account used on the device needs both "Use apps that work with Shopify POS" and "Manage POS UI extensions" enabled under that staff member's app permissions, or the POS UI won't pull the customer's Joy/loyalty data at all.
5. **Manually-created/draft orders**: points still trigger automatically as long as a real, non-guest customer is correctly attached to the order and it's marked Paid — same logic as an online order.

If none of these explain it, share the order number and customer email so our team can check the activity log directly.

### Q6: Can I limit points to full-price products only, but still count Amount Spent toward VIP tier from all orders including sale items?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes for the tier side: **tier calculation** and **points-earning** are two independent settings that don't have to match — you can keep the VIP tier's calculation method as **Amount Spent** (tracking total spend across every order) while separately restricting your Place Order **earning** rule so it only grants points on full-price items. There's currently no way to hide the underlying spend thresholds from customers in the widget (they show in your store's currency) — that would need custom CSS from our team, which isn't offered as a self-serve toggle.

### Q7: How long does a customer have to wait after a transaction to see their new points balance?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Points are usually calculated quickly once the order is marked **Paid**. Points fire from a Shopify webhook, which can occasionally be delayed on Shopify's side — sometimes 40–90 minutes during peak times. If your program uses the **Fulfilled** trigger instead, points don't show until fulfillment; turning on **Display awaiting points** lets customers see a pending entry right after ordering, before the points actually convert.

### Q8: My program shows Sandbox mode / analytics all show 0 even though I already "launched" — what's going on?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Sandbox mode only pauses **point earning** for real (non-test-list) customers — it does not stop enrollment, and it does not hide the widget or the program from view. If you're seeing customers, "Members" and referral traffic but zero points/analytics, first check **Reward Programs → Sandbox Mode**: is it still on? Only accounts on the **Test list** earn points while it's active. Turning it off makes the program go fully live for all customers immediately — orders placed while sandboxed are not retroactively credited unless you ask our team to run a manual re-sync for a specific date range.

### Q9: Can I set the reward value to any point amount (e.g. redeem exactly however many points a customer has), or only fixed thresholds?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
That depends on plan. **Fixed Discount** mode (set point cost per redemption, e.g. "100 points = $5 off") is available on every plan, including Starter (free). **Dynamic Discount** mode — flexible point thresholds where a customer can redeem any amount within a min/max range — is a **paid-plan feature**; it's not available on Starter. If you're on Starter and want multiple redemption levels, create several separate Fixed Discount programs instead (e.g. "5 points = $5 off," "10 points = $10 off").

### Q10: A customer's points balance shows negative — is that a bug?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not always. Manually adjusting a customer's points in **Customers → [name] → Adjust points** has a floor of 0 — you can't push a balance negative that way. But automatic **Anti-Cheat** deduction (triggered when a program has "auto-revoke points on refund/cancel" enabled) works differently: if the customer already **redeemed** the points they earned on an order (spent the balance down) before that order is later cancelled or refunded, deducting the earned points back can push the balance below 0 — this is expected, not a bug, and reflects the customer effectively owing back points they already used.

### Q11: How does point rounding and the analytics "Points redeemed" figure work?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Point-per-currency rounding rounds **down** by default (e.g. €24.43 spent → 24 points, not 24.43). Separately, the **"Points redeemed"** figure in Analytics counts every point a customer spent to **generate a coupon**, regardless of whether that coupon was ever applied to a paid order — so it can be much larger than the **"Revenue by Joy"** figure, which only counts revenue from orders where the discount code was actually applied at checkout. A large gap between the two is expected, not a data error — it just means some generated coupons were never used.

## 3. Redemption & Discount Codes

### Q12: Where and how do customers redeem points, and can it happen automatically at a point threshold?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Customers log into the widget or loyalty page → **Redeem points** → pick a reward → **Redeem**, then either **Apply** (pushes the code to cart) or copy/paste it at checkout. There is **no auto-conversion** — Joy never automatically turns points into a discount the moment a customer crosses a threshold; redemption is always an explicit customer action. The closest low-friction option is **Redeem in Cart Drawer** (Essential+), which surfaces "Redeem your points" directly in the mini-cart once a customer has enough points, without leaving the page to open the widget.

### Q13: If a "Buy X Get Y" reward has X's minimum quantity set to 0, can customers just add the free item Y to cart and check out?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No. Even with X's minimum requirement set to 0, the customer still has to actively **redeem the reward through the widget or loyalty page first** to receive the discount code, then apply that code at checkout — adding product Y to the cart on its own never auto-applies the discount. Also note: with multiple free-gift options configured under one program, the default selection mode is **"Free one product from selection"** — customers get to choose just 1 item, not all of them, unless you explicitly switch to "Free all products in selection" for a bundle.

### Q14: Can a Joy redeemed discount stack with my own automatic store-wide discount, and can customers use multiple discount codes on one order?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, as long as both discounts have **Combinations** enabled and comply with Shopify's own combination rules (which apply the same way regardless of Joy — see Shopify's discount-combination reference). Shopify allows a maximum of **5 product/order discount codes plus 1 shipping discount code** on the same order — this is Shopify's own platform limit, not something Joy's settings can raise.

### Q15: Can I lower my Shopify app spending limit, or does Joy control that?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The spending limit shown for app charges is a **Shopify billing setting**, not something Joy (or any app) controls from its own side — you'd need to contact Shopify Support directly to have it adjusted.

## 4. VIP Tiers & Milestones

### Q16: Why does a long-time customer's VIP tier points total not match their point balance (usually lower)?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
VIP tier points and redeemable point balance are two **separate metrics**. Points added via **manual admin adjustment or CSV import** don't automatically count toward VIP tier progress — only points from configured earning programs feed tier calculation by default. To make a specific manual adjustment count toward tier progress too, check the **"include in tier calculation"** option on the adjustment dialog. If tier points are unexpectedly **lower** than the balance despite no manual adjustments, check whether the VIP Tier program was activated **after** some of the customer's earning activity (e.g. from Birthday or another program) — points earned before the tier program was live may not have been backfilled into tier progress and can need a one-time manual recalculation from our team.

### Q17: A customer's VIP tier didn't update even though their earning activity should have qualified them — is there a self-serve fix?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
If it's a brief **processing delay** — especially for customers with a high volume of "Place Order" transactions queued up — you can refresh their status yourself anytime with the **"Recalculate VIP Tier"** button directly in the app dashboard, no need to escalate. If the mismatch is because points were added via **manual adjustment or a bulk import** and need to be retroactively counted toward tier progress across many customers at once (not a single processing glitch), that's a backend operation our team runs on request — share the original data/points file, the list of affected customers, and the date to recalculate from.

### Q18: Why can't I mark my base/first VIP tier as Exclusive (manual-only)?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Joy doesn't allow the **first/base tier** to be set as Exclusive, because all new members land in the base tier automatically by default — an Exclusive tier is manual-assignment-only, which would conflict with that automatic default enrollment. If you want a paid/invite-only tier (e.g. a subscription-gated "Premium" tier with 2× points), keep your original base tier as the general, non-exclusive tier (rename it if needed, e.g. "General Member"), then click **Add new tier** to create a separate Exclusive tier for the paid group and check "Exclusive tier (Manual only)" on that new one.

### Q19: How do I auto-promote a customer to a VIP tier when they buy a specific subscription product?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
This uses Joy's Membership feature (Advanced+, with Joy Subscription connected): 1) create and **Launch** your Exclusive VIP tier; 2) go to **Membership → Membership Plans**, select the subscription plan tied to your product, and set its **"Promote to VIP Tier"** field to that tier. Once configured, any customer who purchases the subscription plan is automatically promoted to the tier, and demoted back when the subscription lapses/cancels. The VIP tier must be launched before it's selectable in that setting.

### Q20: Can I set up separate VIP tiers for B2C and B2B customers?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — these are two fully separate systems. Regular **VIP Tiers** (Membership → VIP Tiers) apply to B2C customers and can be manually assigned per customer from their profile. **B2B Partner Tiers** (Membership → VIP Tiers → Partner tier tab, Advanced+ with Shopify Plus B2B) track **company-level lifetime spend** and apply automatically to every member of that business account — B2B tiers currently have **no manual per-customer assignment UI**; they're calculated entirely on the backend and can't be changed for an individual B2B contact.

### Q21: Can a merchant recover from an incorrect VIP tier calculation-method change (e.g. switching from Points to Amount Spent) without permanently downgrading customers?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — record every customer's current tier before switching (for reference), then switch and launch the new calculation method as normal. Any customer who ends up downgraded because they haven't hit the new threshold yet can be **re-imported back to their previous tier** afterward. When re-importing, decide whether entry rewards should re-trigger for those customers (disable that option if you don't want a duplicate reward issued). After the process, the Activity Log will show two entries per affected customer — one downgrade event and one re-import event — that's expected, not an error.

## 5. Referral

### Q22: What's the actual difference between the three referral email templates, and when does each one send?
**Frequency:** ~1 session (long thread) 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
These map to different moments in the flow, and the friend (referee) is the recipient of two of the three:
- **Referral invitation** → sent to the **friend**, the instant the referrer enters the friend's email and clicks Send (only relevant if **Automated email sharing** is enabled — with **Manual sharing**, customers copy/paste their own link, so this template never fires). Contains the link and reward details, **but no coupon yet**.
- **Coupons for referees** → sent to the **friend**, right after they open the referral link and submit their own email in the popup. This is the moment their actual coupon is generated and shown on-screen — the email is a backup copy so they can find the code later if they don't use it immediately. It is **not** tied to the friend placing an order.
- **Reward for referrers** → sent to the **referrer**, after the friend's order completes and reaches the shop's earn-status trigger (Paid by default).

The **"Claim Your Rewards"** button/link in these emails opens your storefront homepage with the referral popup auto-attached (the unique referral link is attached to the button automatically) — a plain hyperlink and a styled button in the same email produce the same result, so having both isn't required, just a design choice.

### Q23: For privacy/GDPR purposes, what does Joy store when a visitor lands on a referral link, and when?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
When a visitor opens a referral link, Joy caches an **`avada-joy-geo`** entry in `sessionStorage` (IP address, city, ISP) for the duration of that browser tab — used exclusively for referral **fraud prevention** (detecting when a referrer and referee share the same IP/device). The lookup is made from the visitor's browser directly to **`geoip.apps.getjoy.ai`**, an Avada-owned domain (no third party). The referrer's identity is stored separately in an **AES-encrypted cookie** (`avadaReferralCustomer`/`avadaReferralCustomerId`) used at `checkout_completed` to approve the reward. The referral tracking pixel's `[customer_privacy]` config declares `analytics=false`, `marketing=false`, `preferences=false` — meaning it does not currently wait for a cookie-consent decision before firing; there is no built-in setting today to delay this until after consent. None of this runs on normal storefront pages — only on referral-link landing pages, once per tab session.

### Q24: Can we require that a referrer must be an actual paying customer before they can refer people?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Not as a native toggle today — any Member can generate and share a referral link regardless of purchase history. This came up after a merchant discovered a customer who referred someone despite having made zero purchases; there's no built-in setting to gate referral eligibility behind "at least 1 completed order." Log this as product feedback if it's a concern for your program.

### Q25: Can I make referral rewards increase cumulatively (e.g. 1st referral = $10, 2nd = $20 total, 3rd = $30 total)?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No — the referral program only supports a **fixed reward per successful referral** (e.g. flat $10 for each one). There's no built-in cumulative/escalating reward structure. Combine a fixed per-referral reward with **"Referral limit per customer"** to still cap the total a member can earn (e.g. $10 × 5 referrals = $50 max).

## 6. Widget & On-site Display

### Q26: Should I switch from the Classic to the Unified widget?
**Frequency:** ~15+ sessions 🔁 recurring (still the most-repeated message in the dataset)

**Standard answer:**
Yes if you're still on **Classic** — it's being phased out and won't get new features. **Unified (v4)** is cleaner, fully responsive, and unlocks Store Credit display across the storefront plus a built-in Reviews tab. Switching carries over your existing data and translations — no re-entry needed. Our team can also set up and style the new widget to match your store's branding for free, with a preview before it goes live. Check which version you're on at **On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup**.

### Q27: My widget/loyalty page/Loyalty Hub text isn't translated correctly, or the same text shows differently in different places.
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
There are effectively **three separate translation locations**, and they don't automatically share content:
1. **Settings → Translations** — the main source; covers the widget, loyalty page, checkout blocks.
2. **Theme Editor → the widget/block's own inline "Content & Translations" panel** — narrower scope, for app blocks embedded directly in your theme (e.g. Hero Banner, Point Calculator).
3. **Settings → Translations → Storefront blocks** — a distinct sub-section specifically for **Loyalty Hub (account page)** content.

Because of this, the same conceptual field (e.g. a program's description, or "Show more/less") can show a different value in the widget vs. the loyalty page vs. the Loyalty Hub if you've only translated it in one location — you may need to update all three separately. Also check for a third-party translation app (Translate & Adapt) overriding Joy's own fields. A handful of minor auto-generated strings (e.g. singular/plural grammar variants, some VIP tier description strings inside Customer Accounts) don't have an editable field at all and need to be escalated as a gap.

### Q28: My widget shows on the storefront but customers say it disappears / isn't clickable / doesn't reposition correctly on some devices.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check the standard causes first (see prior weeks' Q29/Q31: app embed off, Hide widget button, Display after login, Manually-assigned-only eligibility). For **exact positioning issues on old/small-screen devices**, the built-in Alignment settings only give rough clearance — pixel-precise or screen-size-adaptive positioning needs custom CSS from our team; share the exact screen resolution, browser, and a screenshot. Popup close-button/positioning issues that only affect very old or unusually small screens are usually a genuine display gap, not a customer-side setting — our team can verify by testing your exact resolution.

### Q29: Can customers reach the loyalty program via a menu link instead of (or in addition to) the floating widget button?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — add a menu item using the deeplink **`#joy-home`** (e.g. `yourstore.com/#joy-home`) at **Online Store → Navigation**; the Joy app embed still needs to stay enabled in your theme for this to work. You can hide the floating launcher separately at **On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup → Advanced → Display** while keeping the deeplink menu item active.

## 7. Integrations & POS

### Q30: Does Joy sync with Intercom, or work with mobile app builders like JMANGO360?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
**Intercom**: yes, Joy syncs loyalty reward data (points, tier, etc.) to Intercom so support agents/AI can see it inside the conversation. **JMANGO360** (a native mobile app builder that wraps your Shopify storefront): not currently supported — this is different from the Ultimate-only **Shopney/Tapcart** integrations, which are supported. A JMANGO360 request has been logged as product feedback with no committed timeline.

### Q31: How do I migrate loyalty-style cashback data (e.g. from a POS system like Lightspeed) into Joy as store credit?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Customer records themselves sync automatically from Shopify once imported there — import your customer list to Shopify first, then Joy will pick them up. For the actual **balance/credit values** (e.g. a "Loyalty $" column functioning like cashback), that's not something that syncs automatically; export the balances from your prior platform and share the file with our team so we can import it as **Store Credit** via our Store Credit import feature. Rewarding store credit going forward for new purchases can now be configured directly in the **Place Order program** — Shopify Flow is **not required** for this anymore. To let customers spend store credit at checkout, enable it under **Shopify Admin → Settings → Customer accounts**.

### Q32: A staff member scanning a customer at Shopify POS can't see their Joy or third-party app info.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
This is almost always a **staff permissions** issue, not a Joy bug. Go to **Shopify Admin → Settings → Users and permissions**, select the specific staff account used on that POS device, and confirm **"Joy: Loyalty Program & Rewards"** is enabled under App permissions — then save and reopen the POS app.

## 8. Billing & Pricing

### Q33: What plan is right for a simple points program, and what does the POS tile actually require?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Starter (free)** is enough for a basic points program — earning points per $ spent and redeeming for a fixed discount both work on Starter. For **Shopify POS**, the free plan doesn't include it at all: **Essential** gives earn-only display at POS, and in-store **redemption** specifically requires **Advanced or Ultimate**.

### Q34: Can I extend or get access to a program a downgrade removed, right before cancelling my plan?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Downgrading (e.g. to the free Starter plan to effectively cancel a paid subscription) takes effect immediately — once downgraded, you won't be charged going forward, and higher-plan features simply become unavailable while your data stays intact. If you're mid-billing-cycle and want a refund for a charge already processed, share a screenshot of the invoice and our team will review it under the 30-day refund policy.

## 9. Notifications

### Q35: Can our team change the birthday reward's 30-day advance rule for our store?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes. The 30-day minimum gap (customer must save their birthday at least 30 days before the actual date, to prevent fraud) is a per-store setting our team can adjust on request — it's not exposed as a self-serve toggle in the admin. If your store already restricts customers from editing their own birthday after the first entry, the fraud risk is lower, so requesting a shorter window (or 0 days) is a reasonable ask; share your store URL and desired value.

### Q36: A fixed email variable like `{{send_email_before}}` shows the wrong language and can't be translated.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Some notification variables render as a **fixed English string** (e.g. "7 days") that can't currently be translated into another language through the template editor — this is a known app limitation. Workaround: if you send the reminder at a fixed, known interval (e.g. always exactly 10 days before expiry), replace the variable with your own translated text typed directly into the template instead of using the dynamic placeholder.

## 10. Customer Data & Reporting

### Q37: Can I export "Customer + current points balance" and "Points earned/redeemed activity" as separate reports?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, these are two different exports. **Customers → Activities** → set a date range, choose Export type = **Earned** or **Redeemed** → enter your email — includes customer name, order ID, and points for that activity type. For **current balances and tier**, use **Customers → Export** (includes email + current point balance by default) — if you only see an **Import** button and no Export, that needs to be enabled by our team first; share your store URL.

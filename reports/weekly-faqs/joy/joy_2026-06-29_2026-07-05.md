## 1. Getting Started, Setup & Activation

### Q1: How do I launch my program / activate it for tomorrow? Can I schedule the launch? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Two things make the program live: (1) turn **Sandbox mode off** at **Joy Admin → Reward programs**, and (2) enable the storefront widget at **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → Joy Loyalty - Widget → ON → Save**. Until both are done, customers can't join or earn. There is currently **no way to schedule a launch** — you switch the program to Live manually when you're ready. A solid starter setup: a sign-up bonus, 1 point per $1, and a 100-point = small-discount reward. Points only start accruing from the moment you go Live — past orders aren't rewarded unless you deliberately sync them (see Q22). If you'd like, share collaborator access and our team will review your configuration and flag anything before you go live.

### Q2: Can your team set up and design my program / loyalty page for me? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Yes — we offer a **free setup service**: share collaborator access and our team styles your Unified widget and/or builds a loyalty page to match your branding (colors, layout, images), then sends you a preview to review before anything goes live (typically 24–48 hours). Note two things merchants hit this week: (a) when a custom page is built on one theme and you switch themes, the formatting can break — tell us your live theme so we migrate it correctly; and (b) a heavily custom-coded loyalty page pulls some data through the theme, so **changes you make in the app may not auto-appear on that page** — flag any mismatch and we'll re-sync it (see Q17).

### Q3: How do I reset the app / program settings to "fresh install" for a rebrand — without losing customer points? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
There's no one-click "reset to default" for program settings — you edit each program individually. To start visually fresh without touching customers' balances: pick a new Preset Theme at **On-site content → Widget → Design**, use **Revert to default** in the widget's Content & Translations to reset text, and rebuild the loyalty page blocks. If you want to pause customers from earning during the rebrand, put programs in **Sandbox** and turn notifications off. Editing your **VIP tier milestones (not the start date)** keeps existing customers' points and just re-slots them into the new tiers; changing the **start date** resets tier-point accumulation from that date forward.

## 2. Testing & Going Live (Sandbox / Test mode)

### Q4: How do I test before launch — and why is my widget/points not behaving in Sandbox? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Use **Sandbox mode** (**Joy Admin → Reward programs → Sandbox mode → ON**) and add your test email(s) to the **Test list** — only whitelisted accounts earn points, so nothing goes public and no real customers are emailed. Two points that confused merchants this week: (1) **Sandbox does not hide the widget** — the floating widget still shows to all visitors; it only controls *who earns*. To hide it before launch, use **Advanced → Display → Hide widget from guests** or exclude it per page. (2) If a test account earns nothing, confirm its email is **exactly** on the Test list. You can also test on a **duplicated draft theme** with the embed enabled and preview it via Shopify without affecting your live store. Turn Sandbox **off** to go live.

### Q5: My customers aren't getting points — is it because I'm still in Sandbox? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
This was the root cause for several stores this week: the program was still in **Sandbox mode** (so only whitelisted emails earn) even though the merchant believed they were live. Confirm at **Reward programs** that Sandbox is **off** and the Place-order program is **Active**. Once you go live, we can **trigger points retroactively** for orders placed during the Sandbox period (backfill availability depends on your plan — the sync-past-orders option is Essential+). Best practice: always re-check the Sandbox state before telling customers the program is live.

## 3. Points — Earning

### Q6: A customer didn't receive points for their order — why? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:**
The usual causes: (1) the **earning program was inactive** or still in **Sandbox**; (2) the buyer was a **guest** (not a logged-in member) at checkout — points tie to a customer account; (3) the order hasn't reached your configured trigger status at **Joy Admin → Settings → Order → Reward by order status** (Paid vs Fulfilled — for **COD** orders use Fulfilled); or (4) a **Check-if** condition on the rule wasn't met. Share the order number + customer email and we'll investigate and add the points manually if valid. If you spot it's an inactive program, reactivate it and we can trigger points for everyone who missed out during the down period.

### Q7: My promo/multiplier (e.g. 5x points) dropped to 1x after the orders were fulfilled. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~1 session (high-impact escalation)
**Standard answer:**
Points fire when the order reaches your configured status. If you run a limited-time multiplier and award points **on Fulfilled**, an order placed during the promo but shipped **after it ended** can recalculate at the base rate. The intended fix is either awarding **on Paid** (Settings → Order → Reward by order status) or enabling **"Lock earning conditions at order creation"** (Settings → Order), which snapshots the rate at order time. This week a store had the lock enabled and orders still dropped to 1x — that was a genuine bug; our engineering team identified the cause and prepared a point-adjustment file to correct the affected orders. If you see a multiplier not honored on fulfilled orders, send the order numbers + emails and we'll escalate and remediate.

### Q8: Can different VIP tiers earn at different rates / a per-tier multiplier? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Yes, on **Advanced/Ultimate**, once your **VIP Tiers** program is **launched**. Set the per-tier rate in the **Place-order program → "VIP tier points only"** option (it only becomes selectable after the tier program is live). Note the **Bonus Points** perk under Tier privileges takes **whole** multipliers (2×, 3×) and its UI label can read like a flat point value — which confused several merchants; agents recommend controlling per-tier rates from the Place-order rule for clarity. If you previously set tier multipliers under Purchase reward (Rule engine), disable those to avoid double-counting.

### Q9: How do I bulk-add points or credit customers for past orders? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Two paths: (1) **Bulk adjust via CSV** at **Joy Admin → Customers → Import** — map each customer's email to the points to add (choose "Add" to top up, or "Reset" to overwrite). (2) **Credit historical orders** via **Settings → Sync points for past orders** — this is a self-serve sync that awards points across your store's existing Shopify orders from a chosen start date. Confirm which customers already have manual points so you don't double-credit. For a mass credit or a specific date range, our team can also trigger it for you.

### Q10: How do I exclude specific products, collections, or discounted orders from earning points? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Group the items into a **Shopify collection** (e.g. sale items), then add a **Check-if** product condition on the Place-order rule to **exclude** that collection; mirror it on the redeeming program if you also want to block redemption there. This applies to all order sources (online, POS, draft orders). Limitation flagged this week: for **manual/draft price-match orders** where you discount a product manually (so it's not in the "sale" collection), Joy can't reliably detect and exclude it — it evaluates fixed conditions, so there's no clean rule for ad-hoc manual discounts; logged as feedback.

### Q11: Daily-visit / streak / social / Google-review points aren't being awarded. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
- **Visit / streak / social / Instagram** are trust-based — the customer must be **logged in** when they take the action.
- **Streak bonus challenge** had a bug this week where it worked on day 1 but not day 2; the cause is that it runs on **server time (UTC+0)**, not your shop's timezone — logged with the tech team.
- **Google reviews** require **admin approval** in **Joy Admin → Activities** before points are granted (or enable instant approval); make sure the Google Places URL is valid.
- **Daily-visit points** remained unresolved for some customers on one store and stayed with the tech team.
Send the customer email + activity and we'll investigate any configured action that still isn't paying out.

### Q12: How does multi-currency earning work for international orders? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
By default your earning rate (e.g. 1 point per $1) applies as a **fallback across all currencies**, calculated on the amount in the customer's local currency; the storefront **displays** the equivalent automatically via Shopify's conversion (so a JPY shopper sees the JPY figure). If you want a **different rate per currency** (e.g. to keep value fair), that's the **Multi-Currency Earning Rates** feature on **Advanced** — toggle "Apply different rates by currency" and set each rate. Redemption amounts you set in your base currency also display converted to the customer's currency.

## 4. Points — Redemption & Discount Codes

### Q13: How do I let two discount codes stack / why do codes show "0 used" or not apply at checkout? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Every Shopify discount is one of **Order / Product / Shipping**, and Shopify only combines codes when **both sides opt in**. In each redeeming program, open **Combinations** and enable the classes you want (e.g. tick Order + Product so a Joy code can stack with your welcome/product discount). If a Joy code clashes with a third-party app's code (AOV, Discount Ninja, email-automation codes), enable the matching class **on both** the Joy program and the other app. A frequent report this week: codes showing **"0 used"** — that's just a usage counter (it goes up when a customer actually applies the code); if codes weren't applying at all, the cause was Combinations left unchecked. Send a customer email + code for anything still erroring on a valid cart.

### Q14: Can customers choose how many points to redeem (a slider), or redeem in a set amount? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Use the **Dynamic Discount** redeem type (**Redeeming programs → [program] → Redeem value → Dynamic**) — customers enter a custom number of points, and you can set **Minimum points**, **Maximum points per redemption**, and **Maximum discount % of cart**. For a "5% off" goal, Dynamic + "limit maximum discount to a % of cart" (set 5%) scales the discount to the cart. **Fixed Discount** sets one $ amount per redemption. Redeeming always **consumes** the points — there's no "redeem without losing points"; for a progress-tracker that rewards on a threshold without spending points, use **Milestones** (Advanced) instead.

### Q15: How do I refund / recover a customer's redeemed points? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
If the coupon is **unused**, go to **Joy Admin → Customers → [customer] → Revoke coupon** — revoking returns the points and disables the code. If the coupon was **already used**, it can't be revoked; you'd **manually add the points back** at **Customers → [customer] → Adjust points**. Note an already-used entry-reward/free-shipping code can't be re-issued as the same code — you'd manually grant an equivalent reward. To auto-return points on cancelled/refunded orders going forward, enable **"Refund points"** on the redeeming program.

### Q16: I set a coupon expiry but a customer still used it after the date. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Redeemed coupons lock in the rules that existed **when they were generated** — so a code created **before** you set/changed the expiration keeps its original (non-expiring) terms, which is how a customer applied it past your new expiry date. This caused a real loss for one store this week and was escalated to the tech team. To invalidate still-active old codes, **revoke** them (Customers → Revoke), or use Shopify Sidekick to list active codes by your Joy prefix. New expiry/combination settings only affect **newly redeemed** coupons.

### Q17: A tier-restricted reward is showing/redeemable for the wrong tier, or its collection info isn't visible. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
A redeeming program scoped to a specific tier should only be redeemable by that tier — if a lower-tier customer redeemed it, send the customer email and we'll investigate and correct the block. Several merchants also want a tier-gated reward to be **visible to everyone** (including logged-out visitors) but **greyed-out/blocked with a "tier not eligible" message** for non-qualifying customers, to motivate upgrades. On a standard setup the program is simply hidden from ineligible tiers; the "visible-but-blocked" behavior needs a custom block adjustment by our team (partially delivered this week, logged where still open). For redeem programs that reward a product from a collection, the collection name can be made a clickable link on the custom loyalty page on request.

## 5. Free Gift, Store Credit & Sale Conflicts

### Q18: My free-gift / redeem-for-product reward generates a discount code that conflicts with my sales — can I use gift cards instead? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
All Joy rewards (including **Free Gift**) generate **Shopify discount codes**, and a Free Gift is a **Product discount** (it zeroes a specific item), so it can clash with sale pricing or other product discounts unless Combinations are aligned on both sides. Joy does **not** issue native Shopify **gift cards** as rewards (logged as feedback). The closest alternative is **Store Credit** — a real cash-value balance applied automatically at checkout as a payment method (no code), which behaves more like a gift card and avoids the discount-code conflict. Enable it by selecting Store Credit as the reward type and clicking **Grant Access → Confirm** the first time. Note store credit shows only on the standard online checkout when signed in (not Shop Pay/guest).

### Q19: A customer redeemed the same free gift twice / more than the per-order limit. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
If a customer has enough points they can redeem a program multiple times unless you cap it. Set the **per-customer redemption limit** on each Free Gift / redeeming program (Redemption limit card) so the same reward can't be claimed repeatedly, and use a **minimum order requirement** to prevent stacking several free-gift codes on one small order. We can apply the per-customer limit across all your gift programs at once — just ask. For a case that already happened, send the customer email and we'll reconfigure it so it won't recur.

## 6. VIP Tiers

### Q20: How do I set up VIP tiers with an annual/rolling spend assessment? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Base tiers on **Amount spent** at **Joy Admin → Membership → VIP Tiers**, then use **Tier Assessment** (Assessment Cycle X / Order Review Timeframe Y / Spending Threshold Z, plus an offset) to review and retain/adjust tiers on a yearly cycle. Important plan note flagged repeatedly this week: **Tier Assessment is an Ultimate feature** (agents sometimes misstated it as Advanced) — VIP Tiers themselves are Advanced+, but the rolling assessment logic is Ultimate. To count spending from **before you installed Joy**, set the tier start date to your desired window and **sync past orders once** (you can only sync once, so set the start date correctly first).

### Q21: Existing customers didn't get the new/updated entry reward for their tier. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
A tier's **Entry reward** fires once when a customer first reaches the tier — so if you **edit or add** an entry reward later, existing members already in that tier won't automatically receive it. At (re)launch, tick **"Grant the entry reward for existing customers"** (Membership → VIP Tiers → Tier configuration / Customer management) and **relaunch** the tier. For a large base this triggering is heavy (one store had ~185k coupon triggers and it took many hours across tiers); if you need it done reliably at scale, share the details and our dev team runs it in the background. Also confirm the reward is under **Entry reward**, not Tier privileges.

### Q22: A customer's tier/points look wrong after a tier edit or manual adjustment. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Two causes surfaced this week: (1) when a tier is **deleted and recreated**, it gets a **new internal ID**, so earning rules that were linked to the old tier lose the connection and show a blank rate — re-enter the rate on the new tier (your customers' points aren't lost). (2) A **manual adjustment** can trigger a tier recalculation that unexpectedly moves a customer. Send the customer email + order and we'll recalc the tier and check whether other customers were affected. On **Points-earned** tiers, redeeming points doesn't lower tier progress (that number only goes up); use **Amount spent** if you want spend-based status that redemptions never reduce.

## 7. Referral

### Q23: How does the referral flow work — who gets the code, when is the referrer paid, and why don't links/emails work? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:**
The **referee** (invited friend) enters their email on the referral popup/link → gets their unique code/credit; the **referrer** is rewarded **only after the referee completes a qualifying first order with the same email** (a 7-day cookie tracks it). Notes from this week: referral links are **unique per customer** (no single static "refer a friend" URL — link a button via a **deep link** to open the referral section instead); testing on the same device/logged-in account can misbehave or trigger **anti-cheat** — test in incognito or another device; and **store-credit** referral rewards need **Grant Store Credit Access** plus Shopify store credit enabled (not applied on Shop Pay/guest checkout). Referral emails send via Joy's built-in email, Klaviyo, or Shopify Flow — check spam, and custom SMTP is paid-plan only.

### Q24: Why did our referral revenue drop, or why is the product-referral widget missing on the product page? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
- **Revenue drop:** we updated referral analytics so a referral order's revenue is counted **only once the order is completed** (not on click/pending), which can lower the reported figure versus the old logic — share the period and we'll review the numbers on your store.
- **Multi-market links:** for stores with Shopify Markets, referral links for foreign-market customers weren't routing correctly — a bug we escalated (needs Markets permission granted).
- **Product-referral block not rendering on the product page:** some themes block app blocks from rendering inside the product-information section — that's a theme limitation, so we place the block in an alternative section and suggest contacting the theme developer to allow it in the desired spot.

## 8. Notifications, Email & Privacy

### Q25: How do I edit the wording of the points-earned / tier emails (customers think they've unlocked a reward)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Edit all notification content at **Joy Admin → Settings → Notifications** — turn the **global toggle** on, then open the specific template. A recurring issue: the **"Earn points"** template is reused for actions like newsletter sign-up, and merchants had customized it to imply a reward was unlocked (e.g. "you reached 100 points") when the customer is only partway — reword that template so it reflects the actual earned amount using the variables (e.g. `{{earning_point_raw}} {{loyalty_point}}`). There's one Earn-points template (it adapts to the reward earned); you can't duplicate a separate one per action.

### Q26: Can I stop an email for just one customer, or are customers auto-enrolled/emailed (GDPR)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Joy's notification toggles work at the **program level**, not per individual customer — there's no built-in "exclude one person from one email"; the only route is a Shopify customer **segment** exclusion, which some merchants find heavier than they want (logged as feedback). On the privacy side: loyalty emails are treated as **transactional** (triggered by the customer's activity/subscription status in Shopify), and Joy's updated sign-up flow only enrolls a customer as a member when they **actually activate/join** — so you can stay compliant without upgrading. To limit who gets emails, keep notifications selective, exclude segments, and note the **Unsubscribe** link routes to Joy's unsubscribe page and also unsubscribes the contact in Shopify. Contacts that live only in a third-party tool and never sync to Shopify aren't emailed by Joy.

### Q27: Will my data stay if my Shopify store closes; and how do custom sender/SPF settings work? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
When a Shopify store is **fully closed/deleted**, Shopify sends a data-deletion request to all apps and Joy automatically removes the associated loyalty data (points, tiers, history) — so **export your customer data first** if you may need it (Customers → Export). For email deliverability: you only need to add an **SPF/custom sender domain** if you send emails **through Joy** (Settings → Email); if you route loyalty emails through Omnisend/Klaviyo instead, Joy only sends triggers and their tool handles sending, so no Joy SPF change is needed. Custom sender/SMTP is a paid-plan feature.

## 9. POS

### Q28: Does Joy work with Shopify POS, and why aren't POS features/redemptions working? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Point **display (earn-only)** works on **Essential+**; full **redemption at POS** (discounts, free products) is **Advanced+**. Install the **Joy: Loyalty Program POS Extension** (add a tile → Apps → Joy) and set each program's **Sales channels** to include POS. Common fixes this week: a POS discount code "not working" was resolved by **assigning the customer to the cart first**; a **free-product redemption crashed** with an extension load error on one device (we investigate via POS staff access — `request.pos@avadagroup.com`). Note **"Adjust points" is not available in the POS interface** — credit points from **Joy Admin → Customers → Adjust points** instead.

### Q29: POS-created customers stopped getting the sign-up bonus — it used to work. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
This is a real trigger change. Joy's **Sign-up reward** now fires only when a customer **actively joins** — logging into a Shopify account on the storefront while the widget is active, joining via the widget popup, or being identified as a member at POS — and **no longer** on every Shopify account creation. As a result, customers **created at POS** (staff adds them, no storefront login) may not receive the sign-up points. Workaround: build a **Shopify Flow** with the **"Customer created"** trigger → award the reward, then **turn off Joy's in-app Sign-up program** so customers don't get it twice. We can also add the missed points manually — share the emails.

## 10. Integrations

### Q30: A customer left a review (Judge.me / Air / Trustpilot) but didn't get points. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:**
First confirm the right review app is selected at **Reward programs → Write a review → [review app]** (a common slip: it was set to "Air Reviews" while the store uses Judge.me — switch the dropdown). Points are granted on **published** reviews, and Joy keeps its own approval status, so a review stuck pending in Joy won't pay out. If Joy tagged the profile (e.g. "Wrote Judge.me review") but no points landed, that's a trigger bug — try **disconnect/reconnect** the integration, and as a workaround **manually credit** the points (Customers → Adjust points). Several stores hit a **systemic** version of this and it was escalated to the tech team. Note: **photo-only review** conditions aren't supported (use a minimum character count instead), and Trusted Shops/Trustpilot only expose a **pull** API (needs Zapier/Make), not a native push into Shopify Flow.

### Q31: Does Joy sync with Klaviyo / Shopify Flow / Google, and can it auto-email at a points threshold? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Yes for **Klaviyo/Omnisend** (Joy Admin → Integrations) — Joy streams events and syncs profile fields (points, tier, member status, referral link, birthday). Joy sends triggers; the **Shopify → Klaviyo** sync is what adds a contact to Klaviyo lists (so a contact must exist in Shopify). For an automatic "you have enough points" email, use **Shopify Flow** with Joy's **"Customer has enough points for reward"** trigger → Klaviyo/Omnisend action (Shopify's native Messaging app also works but with limited variables). **Google Merchant Center** isn't supported (logged as a feature request); **Google Reviews** integrate via your Google Places URL with admin approval. If Klaviyo shows a "Customer Sync Temporarily Disabled" notice, that's escalated to the tech team.

### Q32: Can I show/redeem points in my third-party cart drawer (Discount Ninja, iCart, Swym, etc.)? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Joy's **Redeem-in-line / cart-drawer** block (Advanced+) binds to Shopify's **native** cart drawer. With a **third-party cart drawer app**, our team can add a snippet to attempt integration, but often the third-party app controls the drawer and blocks the discount from displaying — in which case the code still applies correctly **at checkout**, and you'd ask that app's support to allow Joy's block/snippet. Note a **page reload when a customer redeems in the cart is expected** (the theme needs to refresh to apply the code). You can also enable **"Hide block when the customer doesn't have enough points"** in the Redeem-in-line block's visibility settings.

### Q33: How does the Instagram / social-follow reward actually verify the action? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Basic **social/follow** programs are **trust-based** — Joy can't verify external actions like a Facebook/Instagram follow, so points are granted a few seconds after the customer opens the program and clicks "Take action" (it encourages engagement rather than confirming it). The **advanced Instagram programs** (comment, story reply/mention, live comment — Advanced/Ultimate) do require connecting an **Instagram Business/Creator account** (Integrations → Instagram) and the customer linking their IG username. Guidance on whether a plain "Follow" needs the IG API connection was inconsistent in chat this week — for reliability, treat Follow as trust-based (no API needed) unless you use the advanced comment/mention events.

## 11. Billing, Pricing, Plans & Unlimited Transactions

### Q34: How much does Joy cost, what's on each plan, and can I get a discount? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Starter $0 (250 orders/mo), Essential $29/mo (500 orders, $15/100 overage), Advanced $129/mo (2,000 orders, $10/100), Ultimate $499/mo (7,000 orders, $5/100) — merchants on pre-Feb 2026 pricing keep grandfathered rates (e.g. Advanced at $99). Feature gates: VIP Tiers/Milestones/POS/cart-drawer redemption/Rule engine start at **Advanced**; checkout-page redemption/API/Hydrogen and **Tier Assessment** are **Ultimate**; the Loyalty page and multi-language start at **Essential**. ## 12. Migration, Import & Export

### Q37: How do I migrate from another loyalty app, and what data comes over? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Joy migrates **current point balance, VIP tier, birthday, phone, and customer status** — but **not** points-earned history or lifetime spent amount (a recurring limitation merchants ask about). Path: our team enables the **Migration** tool on your store (it's locked until enabled), then you export a CSV from the old app (Smile, Yotpo, Okendo, Appstle, LoyaltyLion, etc. — generic CSV route works for any app) and upload it at **Settings → Migration**, mapping tiers. Joy matches customers by **email** already in Shopify. Two gotchas this week: (1) **decimal point balances** (e.g. Appstle 21.99) can import ×100 — we fix by rounding to whole numbers; (2) VIP-tier spend is tied to the tier, so a large hard-launch migration needs careful coordination (see the escalation note). Verify everything **before** switching live.

### Q38: How do I export my Joy customers / points — I don't see an Export button? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Export from **Joy Admin → Customers → More actions → Export**. If you **don't see the Export button**, it has to be enabled on the backend first — ask support and it appears after a refresh (this is the single most common "there's no export option" reason this week). You can choose which fields to include; a **Phone number** or **VIP tier** column can be added on request; and we can export a filtered slice (e.g. only Gold-tier customers). To export point-change history, use **Customers → Activities → Export** (note the order-number column isn't available in that export yet).

### Q39: How do I reduce/scale all imported point balances after changing my earning rate? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
If you moved from a higher earning rate (e.g. 3 pts/$1) to a lower one (1 pt/$1), you can rescale existing balances so the program stays fair: **export** your customers (Customers → Export), in a spreadsheet **recalculate** the new balance (e.g. divide each by 3), then **import** with the **"Reset existing customer's point balance"** option so the file value replaces the current balance. We can help with the spreadsheet formula or run the recalculation for you (specify round up/down). Do this carefully with a clean source file — mismatches between your old-app CSV and Joy balances (and any unexpected point expiration) should be investigated first so you don't lock in wrong numbers.

### Q40: I fixed some orders/currency — can you re-enable the Sync button so tiers recalculate? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
The **Sync** button is enabled on the backend when needed — ask support and we'll turn it on. Syncing recalculates **Amount Spent** (and tiers) from your Shopify orders, so you can run a test sync first to confirm the numbers, then adjust tier settings and **re-sync manually** for the changes to take effect (it won't recalculate automatically after a settings change). If you deleted/re-imported orders, share the affected order IDs so we sync cleanly without duplicates.

## 13. Customer Accounts, Members vs Guests, Sync & Opt-in

### Q41: What's the difference between a member and a guest, and why is everyone tagged "Member"? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions
**Standard answer:**
With Shopify's New Customer Accounts, every customer who has an account is auto-synced to Joy and treated as a **member** on the lowest (Bronze) tier by default — which is why merchants see "everyone tagged Member." To make membership meaningful (only people who genuinely join), enable **Opt-in enrollment** (Settings → General) — customers then must click **"Join the Program"** to become a member, and only they earn/see rewards. **Member tags** (joined) and **VIP tier tags** (Bronze/Silver/Gold) are separate: tier tags update automatically with the customer's tier and are the ones you generally keep; older tags like "Member"/"joy_tag_member" can be removed on request. Note the **Sign-up reward** only fires when the customer actually joins/logs in — not just from having a Shopify account.

### Q42: A customer isn't showing in Joy / can't sign in. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Customers **manually created in Shopify** (no online registration) may not auto-sync to Joy — add them via **Customers → Add customer** (search the existing Shopify record) or send us the customer ID and we'll trigger a sync. For login: with New Customer Accounts, sign-in uses Shopify's email + one-time-code flow (no password); a persistent **"Couldn't sign you in"** OTP error is Shopify-managed — share the store URL and we'll have the tech team check the storefront integration. Remember Joy's loyalty activity lives in **Joy Admin → Customers**, not on the Shopify customer profile, so an empty Shopify profile doesn't mean the points are missing.

### Q43: Can I share one loyalty account/points across two stores or expansion stores? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
No — Joy manages loyalty data **per Shopify store/admin**. Two stores with **different domains and Shopify admins** each need Joy installed separately and will keep **separate** points, tiers, and customers; there's no built-in shared account or cross-domain points pooling (a repeatedly requested feature this week, including Shopify Plus expansion-store setups). We've logged the interest, and our team can research custom options — share your setup and book a call for a plan recommendation.

### Q44: How do I bring loyalty into the account page / Loyalty Hub or a third-party account editor? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
The **Loyalty Hub** (Advanced+) surfaces points, rewards and tier on the Shopify customer account page — enable it at **On-site content → Account page**, then add the blocks in the Theme Editor. If you use a **third-party customer-account app** (e.g. C:Hub / C Customer Accounts Editor), the Hub may not embed natively — some merchants can add it themselves via that app's editor, otherwise we investigate whether our block can integrate. For excluding **B2B/wholesale** customers who still land on the rewards page after login, we can add a small theme redirect so they route to shopping/orders instead.

## 14. Widget & Loyalty Page Display

### Q45: The widget is in the wrong position, overlaps the menu/footer/cart, or doesn't show after sign-in. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions
**Standard answer:**
First confirm the embed is on (**Theme Editor → App embeds → Joy Loyalty - Widget → ON → Save**). For **position/overlap/z-index** (menu header, sticky add-to-cart, footer, mobile), our team applies a CSS adjustment — share the page/device and desired position (note: a popup sitting above a raised-z-index footer while the button stays below can be partly theme-limited). For **"not showing after sign-in,"** that's a known Shopify SSO timing issue — the quick workaround is clicking **"Join program"** right after login, and configuring Shopify to **redirect to the previous page** (not the account page) reduces it. If the widget looks **cropped**, it's usually browser **zoom** (set to 100%) — the desktop widget is built to always display fully, and the display-ratio setting is mobile-only.

### Q46: How do I customize the widget/loyalty page — colors, button icon, images, fonts, text, layout? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Edit the widget at **Joy Admin → On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup** — Design (colors, layout, launcher button style/icon, border radius), Content & Translations (all text labels), and per-block images under **Header** (logo/background) and **Membership card** (guest/member card banners). A preset theme only sets **sample** images — swap them per block via **Upload image**. For **Ways-to-earn/redeem icons**, edit each program (On-site content → Loyalty page → [program] → custom/AI icon). Build the loyalty page in **Online Store → Themes → Customize → Joy Loyalty template** (Essential+). For pixel-level changes (centering icons/text, spacing, hero banner, button hex, fonts), share collaborator/theme access and our team adds the CSS — separate **mobile vs desktop hero banners** are now supported. To set the widget font to **Inherit** (drop the extra Google Fonts request), use Design → Typography.

### Q47: My "Sign In / Sign Up" buttons or VIP-tier table on the loyalty page don't work / don't match my settings. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions
**Standard answer:**
For **Sign In/Sign Up buttons** that go nowhere, set the link to the **relative path `/account`** (not the full `account.yourdomain.com` URL, which errors) — with New Customer Accounts both buttons use the same `/account` page; test on the **live storefront**, not the Theme Editor preview. For a **VIP-tier benefits table** not showing on the loyalty page, ensure the embed app is enabled. Important: on a **team-built custom loyalty page**, the tier table and redeem-program descriptions are integrated through the theme and **don't always auto-update from the app** — if the page shows the wrong points/perks after you edit them in Joy (a store saw points show 10× the configured value at launch), flag it and we re-sync the block. Keep template variables (e.g. `{{earning_point_raw}}`, `{{loyalty_point}}`, `{{earning_discount}}`) in any custom description text so it renders correctly.

### Q48: Does the widget slow my store (INP / render-blocking / load time)? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
The Joy script loads on all pages even when the widget's **page restriction** limits where it's *displayed* (the restriction controls display, not script loading). The Unified widget lazy-loads with a smaller initial bundle, and setting the widget font to **Inherit** removes the extra Google Fonts request. For a specific INP/render-blocking concern, share your PageSpeed report and we'll review whether Joy is a material factor — in the cases this week the impact was minimal, but we investigate and advise per store.

## 16. Birthday Rewards

### Q50: A customer's birthday reward didn't fire, or fired late / wrong month. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Set **Earning programs → Celebrate a birthday** to **Automatic** with your timing (exact day / calendar month) and turn it on. The key rule: the birthday must be on file **at least 30 days before** the reward scan for that cycle — a birthday entered too close is skipped that year (or picked up by a backup end-of-month scan, which can look "late"). We can **manually trigger** the reward for a specific customer — share the email. The **30-day anti-fraud window is reducible to 7 days** on request (safe because customers can't self-edit their birthday in the widget/loyalty page). Birthday codes also have an expiry window, so check the customer isn't applying an expired code, and you can set **different birthday gifts per tier** via "Member by tier."

### Q51: Where do customers enter their birthday? 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Customers enter it in their **Joy profile in the widget** (Profile / Birthday Rewards block) after logging in; on **Advanced+** it can also be entered via the **Loyalty Hub** on the account page. Optionally you can add a **birthday field to your registration form** (Settings → set the field name, then edit `customers/register.liquid`) or **sync existing birthdays from a Shopify metafield** (Settings → General → Sync Shopify metafields to Joy). POS can't capture birthdays. You don't need the separate "Complete birthday info" reward enabled for the monthly birthday reward to work — it's just an optional incentive.

## 18. Bug reports, errors & escalations (misc)

### Q53: The app is stuck / search doesn't work / coupons or blank sections aren't showing. 🔁 recurring
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Many display glitches clear with a **cache clear / different browser / hard refresh** (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+R). Specific fixes this week: a blank **Rewards / My Coupons** tab was resolved by re-enabling the Redeeming section and reloading (used coupons sit under "Unavailable"); a blank **VIP-tier table** usually means the embed is off; **customer search lag** can follow a mass import (the list catches up in a couple of hours). For a persistent app-stuck, unresponsive menu, chatbox minimizing, or a **permission/pixel re-auth error page**, share the store URL, exact page, and a screenshot, and grant collaborator/theme access so the tech team can patch it. Note the referral pixel (**Settings → Customer Events → Joy**) must be connected for referral tracking, and the new Thank-you/Order-status page pixel isn't fully supported yet.

### Q54: On the Thank-you page, guests see two "join the loyalty program" asks — can I remove one? 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions
**Standard answer:**
Both the **Reward Celebration** block and the **Sign-up** block can appear on the Thank-you page, which can feel redundant to guests. The Sign-up block is **guests-only** (it disappears once the customer is signed in as a member); the Reward Celebration block shows for **both** guests and members but with different content (members see actual points; guests see sign-up encouragement). Joy can only detect **logged-in vs not** (not "guest vs member" beyond that). If it's too much, we can hide the block you don't want for the not-logged-in view — tell us which one and we'll adjust it.

### Q55: Marketing emails from my own store are landing in Joy's support inbox. 🆕 new
**Frequency:** ~1 session (several messages)
**Standard answer:**
These are your store's own promotional campaigns (e.g. via Soundest/Omnisend/Klaviyo) that got routed to the Joy support channel rather than to your customers — no action is needed on Joy's side, and there's nothing wrong with your loyalty program. To stop them, remove the support address from that campaign's mailing list at your end. If anything Joy-related comes up, we're happy to help.

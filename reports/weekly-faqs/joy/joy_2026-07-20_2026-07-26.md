## 1. Getting Started, Setup & Launch

### Q1: How do I set up Joy from scratch?
**Frequency:** ~12 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Start at **Joy Admin → Reward programs**: create your **Place order** earning rule (points per $ spent), then a **Redeeming program** (discount, free gift or free shipping), then decide whether you want **VIP Tiers**, **Referral**, and **Birthday rewards** (Essential+ for these).

Once your programs are configured:
1. Enable the widget embed — **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → Joy Loyalty - Widget** → Save.
2. Optionally add the **Joy Loyalty** loyalty page template (Essential+) with the blocks you want (Ways to earn, Ways to redeem, VIP Tier benefits, Referral, FAQs).
3. Use **Sandbox mode** with a test-account list to try the whole flow before going live (see Q4).

If you're not sure where to start or want a review before launch, our team can walk through a setup checklist with you, or (for larger programs) send a template sheet for you to fill in rules/features and configure it on your behalf.

### Q2: Can your team set up or style my widget / loyalty page for free?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — this is a free setup service. Share a reference design or describe your branding (colours, fonts, layout) and, if needed, grant **collaborator access** via your 4-digit code (**Shopify Admin → Settings → Users → Security → Collaborator request code**). Our team styles the widget and/or loyalty page and sends a preview link before anything goes live — nothing changes on your storefront until you approve it.

This also covers migrating you from the Classic to the Unified widget (see Q28) with a fresh restyle included.

### Q3: What's included on the free Starter plan vs paid plans?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Starter (free)** covers the core loop: earning, holding and redeeming points, the loyalty widget, referrals (capped, raised on paid plans), and a basic loyalty block on the Shopify customer profile.

Starting at **Essential**: points expiration, the dedicated loyalty page, birthday rewards, free gift/free shipping redemption, analytics, multi-language, fraud prevention. **Advanced** adds VIP tiers, Rule Engine, milestones, POS, the full Loyalty Hub, custom opt-in enrollment. **Ultimate** adds the API/webhooks and checkout-page redemption (Shopify Plus only).

Compare and upgrade at **Settings → Subscription → All plans**.

### Q4: How do I test my program before launching?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Turn on **Sandbox mode** at **Reward programs → Sandbox Mode**, then add your test emails to the **Test list** button that appears. Only those accounts earn points while sandbox is on — real customers are unaffected. Enable the Joy app embed on a duplicate/draft theme if you want to try the widget without touching your live store, then log in with a test account and run through signing up, placing an order, and redeeming.

Two things that catch people out: an email not on the test list earns nothing (looks like a bug, isn't), and the sign-up reward won't fire for an account that already existed before the program started — use a genuinely new test email. Ask us for a pre-launch review before going live and we'll check your earning, redeeming, VIP tier and widget setup.

### Q5: Can I migrate or import data from another loyalty app?
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Migration is available on **Essential, Advanced and Ultimate**, and can bring over customer name, email, phone, VIP tier name, birth date, member status and point balance. There are dedicated guides for Smile.io, Rivo, Yotpo Loyalty, LoyaltyLion, Stamped Loyalty, Bon Loyalty and Appstle; anything else (Square, Growave, custom platforms) uses the generic CSV route.

Two paths:
- **Import** (self-serve, no enabling needed) — **Customers → Import**, covers point balances and birthdays.
- **Migrate** (assisted, full data set) — the tool at **Settings → Migration** is locked until our team enables it. Tell us which app you're moving from and what data you need, and we'll turn it on.

Two things that don't migrate: previously-issued discount codes from your old platform (they aren't imported as Joy coupons), and detailed activity history (order-level earn/redeem timestamps). After any import, remember to re-**Launch** the relevant program to trigger recalculation of points and Amount Spent.

## 2. Points — Earning

### Q6: A customer placed an order but got no points — why?
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Work through these in order:
1. **Order hasn't reached the reward status yet** — points fire on the trigger set in Settings → Order (Paid or Fulfilled).
2. **Customer is a Guest, not a Member** — if your rule rewards members only, a guest checkout earns nothing.
3. **Opt-in enrollment is on and the customer hasn't joined** — activity log shows "order recorded" but no points.
4. **Program date range, product/collection conditions, or order-level exclusions** (tag, source, payment gateway, B2B) don't match.
5. Two contradictory sales-channel conditions on the same rule (e.g. "equals Online store" AND "equals POS") — no order can ever match both.

If none of these explain it, send us the order number and customer email and we'll check the activity log directly.

### Q7: Why didn't a customer get the sign-up bonus?
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The sign-up reward isn't granted the moment an account is created — it fires when the customer **logs in** with the Joy widget embed active, joins via the **widget popup**, or is identified as a member at **POS**. Simply leaving an email at checkout or through a newsletter form doesn't qualify, even though Shopify may mark them "Enabled".

The other frequent cause: the account was created **before** the sign-up program's start date — by default only accounts created on/after that date qualify. If you want existing customers included too, this needs a manual pass from our team (or their points added individually at Customers → [customer] → adjust points).

### Q8: Can I set different point values for specific products or collections, including excluding sale items?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes. Create multiple **Place order** rules, each with its own rate and its own product/collection condition, at **Reward programs → Earning programs → Place order → Add rule**. To exclude discounted items from earning, keep sale items in a dedicated collection and set the rule's product condition to **exclude** that collection — mirror the same exclusion on your redeeming program's Applicable categories so redemption doesn't stack on already-discounted items too.

Running more than one Place-order rule requires **Rule Engine** (Advanced, Ultimate). On Essential you have a single rule, so a temporary campaign means manually changing the rate and changing it back.

### Q9: What is "Customer Opt-in" (Join Program), and how does it change earning?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
With opt-in enrollment on, a customer must actively **join** (click "Join Program" while logged in) to become a Member. Until they do, they're a Guest — and if your Place-order rule rewards Members only, a Guest's orders earn nothing even though the order shows up in the activity log.

This most often catches merchants out when opt-in is turned on **after** launch — customers who signed up in the gap earned as Members, then the rules changed underneath them. If you're cleaning this up: points already awarded don't disappear on their own (tell us if you want a reset), and entry rewards won't retroactively fire for customers who joined before a tier program went live.

### Q10: Why aren't social / Instagram / review points being awarded?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The customer must be **logged in** at the moment they perform the action — Joy identifies by account session, not IP or device.

- **Social follow/share** is **trust-based** — there's no API to verify a follow, so points are awarded on click-through.
- **Instagram-specific programs** need an Instagram Business/Creator account connected (**Integrations → Instagram**) and the customer's username linked in the widget.
- **Google/product reviews** sit in an approval queue by default unless the review app's "Default status" setting is set to Approved, and the review app connection itself can silently drop — check it shows Connected.

### Q11: Birthday reward / "Complete birthday info" reward wasn't given.
**Frequency:** ~7 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
These are two separate rewards. **Complete birthday info** grants points once, the moment a customer first submits their birthday — it doesn't relate to their actual birthday.

**The birthday reward itself** needs all of these true: the birthday was saved **at least 30 days before** the birthday (anti-abuse rule — the single most common reason it's "missing"), the customer is a registered member, the date falls in your configured window (exact date / month / custom range), and the delivery method (Automatic vs Manual claim) matches what happened. Customers can't edit their own birthday after first entry — you can update it from their customer profile, and there's a **Trigger birthday reward** button there for manual one-offs.

### Q12: Do points I add manually count toward a customer's VIP tier?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not automatically. Manually-adjusted points are, by default, **excluded** from VIP tier accumulation — adjusting a balance only changes what the customer can spend, not their tier progress. If you want a manual adjustment to also count toward the tier threshold, check the **"include in tier calculation"** option when you make the adjustment at **Customers → [customer] → adjust points**.

Remember VIP tier points and redeemable point balance are two separate metrics — adjusting one doesn't automatically resync the other.

## 3. Redemption & Discount Codes

### Q13: How do customers redeem, and does the code apply automatically?
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The main path is the widget or loyalty page: log in, open **Redeem points**, pick a reward, click **Redeem**. Joy generates a one-time code — the customer either clicks **Apply** to push it to the cart, or copies it to paste at checkout.

**The code doesn't apply itself.** This is the single most common "my discount disappeared" report — the points were spent and the code is valid, but nobody entered it. Unused codes are always retrievable from the widget's **My coupons** section. Other surfaces: cart drawer, checkout page (Plus + Ultimate only), and POS.

### Q14: A redeemed code isn't working / combining at checkout.
**Frequency:** ~8 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Check in this order:
1. **Discount combination isn't enabled** on the redeeming program — by default Joy coupons don't stack with other discounts. Configure which classes (Product/Order/Shipping) may combine at **Redeeming programs → [program] → Combinations**. This only affects **newly issued** coupons, not codes already in customers' hands.
2. **Shopify's own combination rules** apply regardless of your setting — two Product-class discounts can't both apply to the *same* line item (non-Plus), and Order+Order or Product+Order combos only work for merchants without checkout.liquid customisations. Shipping+Shipping never combines.
3. **The code's own conditions** — minimum order value, eligible products, sales channel.
4. **The discount is bigger than the cart** — a fixed-amount reward larger than the cart total can zero it out and, on some third-party checkout apps, throw an error. Set a minimum cart value on the redeem program to prevent this.

### Q15: Can I edit a coupon after it's issued, or deactivate one without refunding the points?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
No — once a coupon is generated, its expiry and conditions can't be edited. If a customer's code is expiring, issue a replacement (revoke and let them re-redeem, or create a code directly in Shopify Admin → Discounts).

To **deactivate a code but keep the points spent**, don't use Joy's Revoke (that always refunds the points) — deactivate the code directly in **Shopify Admin → Discounts** instead. Note the status inside Joy may not auto-update to Expired afterwards. There's no bulk-deactivate — send us the store URL and the list/criteria for a batch job.

### Q16: Does a redeemed discount scale with the quantity of items in the order?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
No — a redeemed reward is an **order-level discount**, applied once per order regardless of how many units are in the cart. This is expected behaviour, not a bug: there's no per-unit capping or per-item multiplication of the redeemed reward's value.

### Q17: Why doesn't my product show up when picking a redeem reward?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Only products **published to the Online Store sales channel** sync to Joy's reward picker — Draft products or ones removed from the Online Store channel won't be selectable.

Separately, if you're picking products/variants for a **discount** program and hit a "too many products/variants" validation error even though you selected fewer than 100 products: the limit actually counts **variants**, not products — this is a hard cap from Shopify's Discount API and can't be raised by Joy. Group the products into a Shopify Collection and select the collection instead to work around it.

### Q18: A coupon imported from another loyalty app (e.g. Smile.io) can't be revoked.
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This is a known limitation with coupons brought over from a prior platform — Joy's normal Revoke button doesn't work on them the way it does for natively-issued coupons. If you need one deactivated, send us the customer and coupon details; our team can hide it from the customer's view and manually credit the equivalent points if needed. There's no self-serve fix for this today — it's logged with our product team.

## 4. VIP Tiers & Milestones

### Q19: A tier entry reward wasn't given, or I want to re-grant it.
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Entry rewards fire **once**, the first time a customer reaches a tier, and are **not retroactive** — customers already in the tier when you create or edit the reward don't get it, and customers who joined before the VIP tier program launched are assigned a tier retroactively but don't trigger the entry reward.

Set them at **Membership → VIP Tiers → [tier] → Entry reward → Add reward** (not under Tier privileges — that's a different, recurring mechanism). To re-grant on re-reaching a tier after a downgrade, enable **"Allow giving entry reward again after being downgraded"** at **Tiers tab → Tier configuration settings → Customer management**.

### Q20: My customers' tiers or points look wrong after editing VIP tiers.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
This is almost always a re-assessment, not lost data — changing a threshold, the calculation rule, or re-launching VIP Tier makes Joy re-evaluate every customer. The most common specific cause: the tier program's **start date** — if it's set to today, everyone starts at zero; set it back to the date you actually want to count from and re-launch.

Remember **VIP tier points** (cumulative, drives tier) and **point balance** (what's redeemable) are separate metrics — redeeming doesn't lower tier points, and adjusting a point balance doesn't resync tier points.

### Q21: Can an Exclusive (manually-assigned) VIP tier require annual renewal, like Tier Assessment does for regular tiers?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Not currently. **Tier Assessment** (the rolling-window re-evaluation that keeps or drops a customer based on spend/orders/points over a lookback period) applies to the standard, automatically-progressing VIP tiers — it isn't built for **Exclusive tiers**, which are manually assigned only and don't auto-progress or auto-demote.

If you use an Exclusive tier for ambassadors or wholesale accounts and want a "must requalify every year" rule, there's no built-in mechanism — you'd need to manually review and reassign annually. This has been noted as a gap for our product team.

### Q22: Can different VIP tiers earn points at different rates, and what's the setup order?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes — set it up either as a **Bonus Points** privilege perk (whole multipliers only: 2×, 3×, 4×) on each tier, or via **Rule Engine** for fractional rates like 1.5×. Either way, requires VIP Tiers already set up and **enabled**.

If you're configuring several programs together, the sequence matters: **launch VIP Tiers first**, then set up per-tier earning rates, then redeem programs, then milestones last. Per-tier rate fields only become available once the VIP Tier program is activated — not simply saved as a draft. Activating (without publishing/notifying customers) has no customer-facing impact, so it's safe to activate early while you finish configuring.

## 5. Referral

### Q23: Referral is enabled but I don't see it anywhere in the widget.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Referral sharing lives inside the loyalty widget (and, on Advanced+, the Referral Management block on the account page) — it isn't a separate floating element by default. Add the **Referral block** to your loyalty page or thank-you page for extra visibility, or use the `#joy-referral-program` deeplink in a menu item or button.

If you specifically don't see the section inside the widget at all, check it's toggled on under **On-site content → Widget → Referrals**, and that the underlying **Referral program** itself is turned on at **Reward programs → Referrals** — those are two separate switches.

### Q24: When exactly does the referrer get rewarded?
**Frequency:** ~5 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
The referrer shares their link → the referee clicks it, lands on the referral page and enters their email → a **7-day cookie** is stored → the referee places an order **with the same email** → both sides are rewarded.

The referee's reward issues at the claim step. The referrer's reward stays pending until the referee's order reaches your shop's earn-status setting (Paid by default — Fulfilled shows "Awaiting fulfilment" in the meantime, which is normal, not a failure). Common blockers: different emails at claim vs checkout, an expired cookie, a $0-subtotal order, anti-cheat (same browser/email/IP), a fully-refunded order with auto-revoke on, or the referral program still being in Sandbox.

### Q25: I'm getting "Failed to adjust store credit" when setting up a referral reward.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
This means Shopify's Store Credit permission needs to be (re-)granted to Joy. Go to the reward setup for the program using Store Credit and click through the **Grant Access / Confirm** prompt — it's a one-time authorization that then covers all programs using store credit.

### Q26: How can I see how many people have referred my brand, and what do the export statuses mean?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Referral performance shows in **Analytics → Referred revenue** (sales generated through the referral program) and in **Referral Management**, which shows per-customer referral counts.

For referral **export** status fields: "Pending"/"Awaiting fulfilment" mean the referee has claimed but the qualifying order hasn't reached the reward trigger yet (see Q24); "Completed" means the referrer's reward has been granted. If you see a "Completed At" timestamp on a row that's still showing status Pending, that's a display inconsistency in the export rather than the referral actually being complete — send us the export and we'll check with the team, since we've seen this reported as a real bug.

## 6. Widget & On-site Display

### Q27: The launcher button overlaps my cart / chat / navigation. Can I move it precisely?
**Frequency:** ~15 sessions 🔁 recurring (still the largest topic)

**Standard answer:**
Rough clearance is self-serve: **On-site content → Widget → Widget design → Setup → Settings → Launcher** → Alignment (bottom-left/bottom-right), size, border radius, plus **Advanced → Display → Page restriction** to hide it on conflicting pages.

**Exact placement** — a specific pixel offset, matching another button's size, sitting behind another app's floating button, or repositioning after a click — needs custom CSS from our team. Send a screenshot/mockup of the target position, the page URL, and the device (with OS if mobile-specific), plus your collaborator code, and we'll apply it and send a result to verify.

If a conflicting floating button on the page **isn't Joy's**, we can't move it — we check the storefront first, and several cases each week turn out to belong to another app (chat widgets in particular).

### Q28: Should I be on the Classic or Unified widget?
**Frequency:** ~6 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Unified** is the current version and where all development goes; **Classic** is deprecated. Check which you're on at **On-site content → Widget → Widget design** (the "live" badge marks the active one) and switch with **Switch to Unified** — all your data (points, programs, customers, translations) carries over untouched.

We're happy to migrate and restyle it for you with a preview link before it goes live (see Q2). Note: switching back to Classic discards your current Unified design, and a few features (milestone UI, interactive popup, wallet pass/QR) still render in Classic style even on Unified stores.

### Q29: My cart drawer shows a redeem button and "0 points" even to logged-out guests.
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
This was confusing guests seeing a clickable "Redeem" with a zero balance before logging in. There's now an option in the theme's cart-drawer block settings to **hide the redeem-inline block for guest/unauthenticated visitors**, showing only a login prompt instead. If you don't see this option yet, send us your store URL and we'll confirm it's enabled on your theme.

### Q30: My widget or loyalty page content isn't translating correctly.
**Frequency:** ~10 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
First check your default language at **Settings → Translations** isn't still sitting on English. Most remaining cases are a **third-party translation app** (Translate & Adapt in particular) overriding Joy's own translated fields — check for overridden rows for the affected language and remove them.

**Two things that genuinely can't be translated through Joy:** product names shown in redeem rewards are pulled directly from the Shopify product record, so they follow whatever the product is named in Shopify (edit the product itself, or use Shopify's own product-title translation, not Joy's); and some auto-generated VIP tier description strings inside Customer Accounts can still show untranslated — tell us the surface and we'll log it. Plan gating: editing English content is on all plans; changing the default language is Essential+; auto Detect Language is Advanced+.

## 7. Integrations & POS

### Q31: Where do I turn on syncing my points/tier to Shopify (metafields)?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
**Settings → General → Show metafields on Shopify admin → Turn on**, then optionally **Allow third-party app access** so tools like Klaviyo can read it via the Storefront API, and **Sync metafields data** to backfill existing customers. This pushes Point Balance and VIP Tier name to Shopify Customer Metafields under the `$app:joy_loyal` namespace, visible at Shopify Admin → Customers → [customer] → Metafields.

### Q32: Does Joy sync to Omnisend the same way it does to Klaviyo?
**Frequency:** ~1 session 🆕 new

**Standard answer:**
Yes — Omnisend receives the same profile properties as Klaviyo: point balance, VIP tier, member status, referral link and birthday, kept up to date automatically. If a threshold change hasn't reflected yet, there's a manual **Sync Again** button you can use rather than waiting for the next automatic pass.

### Q33: Joy's POS tile shows an error or points aren't syncing at the register.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
POS redemption requires **Advanced or Ultimate** — Essential POS supports earn-only points display. If the tile errors out (e.g. a session-token or loading failure), first have staff log out and back into the POS device to clear the cached session, confirm the device's POS app and OS are current, and remove/re-add the tile. Running two versions of the Joy POS extension side by side (old + new) can also conflict — remove the old one first. If it persists, send the device model, OS version and POS app version.

Remember a customer **must be attached to the sale before payment completes** for points to be awarded — Shopify doesn't pass customer info to Joy retroactively if it's added only when emailing the receipt afterwards.

## 8. Billing & Pricing

### Q34: My bill went up — can specific sales channels be excluded from the order quota?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🔁 recurring

**Standard answer:**
Yes, with a trade-off: excluding a channel removes those orders from the loyalty program entirely (no points, no rewards) as well as from usage counting. To do it, apply a consistent order tag to every order from that channel, confirm you accept the loyalty trade-off, and send us the store URL, tag and reason — we'll configure the exclusion.

This has come up specifically for **marketplace/multichannel orders** (e.g. Shopify Target+, Marketplace Connect) that were being counted toward the usage-fee quota even though the merchant considers them a separate sales channel — one case got these excluded from the bill going forward after escalation. If you think orders from a channel you don't consider "real store traffic" are inflating your bill, flag it and we'll review with the billing team.

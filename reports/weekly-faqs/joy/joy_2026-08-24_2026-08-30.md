## 1. Getting Started / Launch

### Q1: How do I launch from Sandbox to Live and retroactively sync historical points/VIP tiers for existing customers?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Toggle **Sandbox Mode** off under Reward programs to go live. To backfill history: **Settings → Pre-launch → Sync Past Orders** re-scans historical Shopify orders and awards points per the *current* earning rules (can go back multiple years); VIP tiers need a separate recalculation the team can trigger. Two gotchas worth setting expectations on: (1) orders that were **already processed** under an earlier version of an earning rule keep the point total from *that time*, not the current rate — a sync doesn't retroactively re-rate them; (2) **customers who existed in Shopify before Joy was installed do not get the Sign-up bonus** retroactively just from the sync — that needs **Settings → Pre-launch → Sign up account → Sync**, a separate action.

### Q2: How do I stop every logged-in customer from auto-becoming a "Member" — I want an explicit opt-in?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🆕
**Standard answer:** With Shopify's **New Customer Accounts**, any recognition (login, checkout email, newsletter) makes someone a Member automatically by default — there's no separate registration step. To require an explicit join action: switch **Settings → General → Membership enrollment** to **Opt-in** (or **Manual**) — under Opt-in, a logged-in customer stays a Guest until they click **Join** at a loyalty touchpoint (widget, account page, or loyalty page); a guest who hasn't joined doesn't earn Place-order points. There's no self-serve bulk tool to revert already-enrolled Members back to Guest by a cutoff date — the team can do this as a one-off backend action (points balances stay intact, only membership status reverts).

### Q3: Can the team bulk-convert a list of customer emails into Members before launch?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Yes — collect the store URL and the email list, and the team can bulk-convert them. If VIP Tiers is part of the launch, activate the VIP Tier program **about a day before** go-live rather than day-of: recalculating tier status across a large, multi-year customer history can take hours. Keep Notifications off during this window — no retroactive send happens once turned on later.

### Q4: What data transfers when migrating from another loyalty app (Smile.io, Rise.ai, Awtomic, etc.), and what plan do I need?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Assisted migration requires **Essential plan or above** and is unlocked by the team on request (Settings → Migration is locked until enabled). Migrated fields: Customer Name, Email, Phone, VIP Tier name, Birth date, Customer status, Point balance. For points-balance + birthday only, a merchant can self-serve immediately via **Customers → Import**, no enabling needed. **Store credit from another platform is not migrated as a Joy concept** — Shopify-native store credit stays with the customer regardless of app — but a merchant can manually convert an equivalent number into Points via CSV import if they want it inside the points system.

### Q5: Our program has very low participation / redemption rate — what should we change?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Same guidance as prior weeks: raise visibility before raising the offer — add a Rewards link to menus, add the Point Calculator to product pages, enable the Reward reminder popup, and add a Sign-up block to the Thank You page. If the earning rate itself is thin (e.g. under 2%), consider raising it toward the 2–5% "sweet spot," and add more relevant reward items (product-specific rather than generic gift cards) plus a VIP tier layer to give repeat customers a reason to keep engaging.

## 2. Points — Earning

### Q6: A customer's order didn't earn points — why?
**Frequency:** ~9 sessions (merged across several distinct causes) 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Check in this order: (1) order hasn't reached the reward trigger status (Paid/Fulfilled, Settings → Order); (2) customer is a Guest, not a Member; (3) Sandbox Mode/Test list is on and the email isn't whitelisted; (4) draft order created in Shopify Admin — by default only Online Store orders count unless the program's Sales channel is changed to include "Online store and Admin draft orders"; (5) a conflicting rule condition; (6) order-level exclusions (tag, source, gateway, B2B). **New this week:** a real, unresolved-by-Joy cause surfaced repeatedly — orders placed through a **third-party cart/upsell app** (e.g. a slide-cart or checkout-bridge app) that then *pushes* the order into Shopify are not recognized as native online-store orders by Joy's Place-order rule, so they silently earn nothing. There's no built-in fix; the team credits points manually for affected orders. Collect the order number, customer email, and which third-party cart/checkout app is in use before escalating.

### Q7: The sign-up/welcome bonus wasn't granted to a customer who already had a Shopify account.
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Accounts created **before** the Sign-up program's start date don't qualify by default — this applies even after the program later goes live. Fix: **Settings → Pre-launch → Sign up account → Sync** grants it retroactively to pre-existing accounts (a one-time backend/self-serve action, distinct from the general points-sync above), or add points manually via Customers → adjust points for a single customer.

### Q8: Can I set a progressive/tiered earning rate based on order value brackets in one order (e.g. more points per dollar the bigger the order)?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not as a single sliding-scale rule. The closest approximation is **multiple Place Order rules under Rule Engine**, each with its own order-value condition (e.g. "$0–$99 → 1pt/$1", "$100+ → 2pt/$1") — Joy evaluates and stacks whichever rule(s) match. Requires Advanced/Ultimate for Rule Engine.

### Q9: Is there a shared/company-level point balance for B2B accounts with multiple logins?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No — every Shopify customer account has its own separate point balance; there's no pooled/company-level balance across multiple logins today. Logged as a feature request.

### Q10: Can I run a limited-time bonus (e.g. "double points today only")?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Yes, via **Rule Engine** — a Place order rule with a date/time condition plus its own rate. Requires Advanced/Ultimate (Rule Engine isn't available on lower plans).

### Q11: Does the points/VIP-spend calculation double-count tax or shipping if our storefront prices are already tax-inclusive?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No — by default, points and VIP-tier spend both calculate from the **order subtotal after discounts**, already excluding shipping and tax, so a VAT-inclusive storefront doesn't double-count. On Advanced, a merchant can optionally opt a program in to also reward on tax/shipping via "Reward for additional fee," but that's off by default.

### Q12: Our review-earning program awarded points twice for the same review.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** In the reported case, the merchant approved the same review twice — once via mobile (which didn't visibly register at first) and again on desktop — causing a double award. If this happens, manually deduct the duplicate points for that customer; if a merchant reports this pattern recurring without a double-approval, escalate as a possible Activities-log display bug with the customer email and review record.

### Q13: Can VIP tiers be bulk-updated (upgraded or downgraded) via CSV import for existing customers?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕 — **conflicting guidance seen this week, flagged for review**
**Standard answer:** **Yes** — Customers → Import supports a tier-only update file (include the new tier name + the new VIP-tier amount per customer) to bulk-update tiers without touching point balances. One caveat: if the VIP Tier program's settings are later edited and re-launched, Joy recalculates every customer's tier from the program's start date, which can override a manually-imported tier assignment. *(Note: two different agent replies in the same chat this week gave contradictory answers — one said tiers can only be changed one-by-one, the other confirmed the CSV path above. The CSV path matches the cached KB's Customer Import notes, so it is the answer to use; flagging so a KB clarity pass can prevent the mixed signal from repeating.)*

## 3. Points — Redemption

### Q14: Can I cap redemption at a percentage of the order total, or require a minimum spend, specifically in the Quick Redeem cart-drawer widget?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not in Quick Redeem (cart drawer) specifically — it shows currently-affordable rewards without a % cap or minimum-spend gate. Standard Redeeming programs (Amount Discount) do support min/max points per redemption and a **Dynamic Discount** mode that can restrict redeemable value relative to cart total — use a regular redeem program instead of Quick Redeem if this control is needed.

### Q15: If a customer redeems points for a coupon but doesn't complete checkout, do the points come back automatically?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No — points are not auto-refunded just because checkout wasn't completed. The generated coupon stays valid (retrievable from My Coupons) until it's used or expires; the customer can still use it later, or the merchant/customer can manually revoke it (if unused) to return the points immediately.

### Q16: A previously-working discount/redeem code stopped working — turned out the underlying product had changed.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring (broader pattern of last week's Q53)
**Standard answer:** Same root cause as the free-gift "out of stock" issue from last week, but it also affects discount codes generally: if the linked product's **variant ID changes** after a Shopify-side product edit (or the product is deleted and recreated), an already-issued code can reference a stale, no-longer-valid variant. Fix: revoke the invalid coupon (refunds the points) and let the customer re-redeem fresh — this generates a code against the current product reference.

### Q17: A redeem block / discount stopped showing after items were removed from the cart — is this a bug?
**Frequency:** ~2 sessions 🆕
**Standard answer:** This is expected, not a bug — a redeemed discount is tied to the cart value it was originally generated against. If the cart total later drops below that original value (e.g. the customer removes an item), Joy hides the redeem block/discount rather than showing an invalid amount. The coupon itself is still valid; the customer just needs to bring the cart back up to the qualifying value, or generate a fresh redemption sized to the current cart.

### Q18: I manually extended an expired coupon's date in Shopify Admin → Discounts — it works at checkout, but Joy's coupon history still shows it as "Expired."
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** This is a cosmetic-only mismatch. Extending a code directly in Shopify makes it valid and usable at checkout immediately (Shopify validates natively), but Joy's own coupon-history display doesn't automatically re-sync to reflect a Shopify-side edit — it can keep showing "Expired." No functional impact; if the merchant wants the Joy-side status corrected for record-keeping, escalate with the coupon code and store URL.

### Q19: Is there a way to auto-convert an unused/expired coupon back into points, or trigger a revoke via API/Shopify Flow?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not currently — there's no time-based auto-conversion and no Shopify Flow action to revoke a coupon programmatically. The only paths are a manual **Revoke** by the merchant (Customers → [customer] → coupon → Revoke, only works on unused coupons) or the customer's own **Self-Revoke** if that setting is enabled. Logged as a feature request.

### Q20: When a customer redeems points and earns more points on the same order, is the new earning calculated before or after the redeemed discount?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** **After** — new points on an order are calculated on the subtotal *following* any loyalty discount already applied to that order, not the pre-discount value.

### Q21: Can the Joy discount be made optional so the customer can choose not to apply it (rather than it auto-applying)?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not for an **Automatic Discount**-type reward — Shopify auto-applies the best eligible automatic discount at checkout and the customer can't selectively decline just Joy's piece of it, short of the merchant's own Combinations settings blocking it from stacking with something else. There's no per-customer opt-out toggle.

## 4. Widget

### Q22: The widget isn't reflecting changes I made in the editor.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Usually caused by editing the wrong widget version — a store can be *live* on Classic while the merchant edits settings in the Unified editor (or vice versa). Check **On-site content → Widget → Widget design**, which shows both cards with a "Live" badge on whichever one is actually active, and switch to editing that one (or switch the store over to Unified if that's the intent).

### Q23: Can I reposition or resize the widget launcher beyond the built-in Alignment/Size options (e.g. an exact offset to avoid overlapping a page element)?
**Frequency:** ~4 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Rough clearance is self-serve via **Settings → Launcher → Alignment**. For an exact pixel offset (e.g. clearing a WhatsApp button, cart icon, or "Ver Cesta" button on a specific breakpoint), this needs custom CSS from the team — collect a screenshot, device, and the target offset and escalate; don't try to talk the merchant through DIY CSS.

### Q24: The Sign-up reward has no button/action in the widget — how does a customer actually claim it?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** This is a known gap, not a bug to troubleshoot — the Sign-up program is one of the few reward types with no dedicated in-widget action button (it fires automatically via the login+widget-active path, so there's nothing to "click"). If customers are confused about how to sign up at all, use the program's description/detail text in the widget to point them to the account/login button rather than expecting a claim CTA. Logged as a UX improvement request.

### Q25: A social "Follow us" or Instagram program is awarding points on click without verifying the actual follow.
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Expected — Joy cannot verify most social-platform actions via API (platform restrictions), so generic social programs are trust-based: points fire on clicking "Take Action," not on a confirmed follow. Instagram-specific programs (comment, story reply/mention, live comment) are more verifiable since they connect a Business/Creator account, but generic "Follow us" is click-based across every platform, including WhatsApp Channel/Group.

### Q26: A customer's name shows wrong in the widget after logging in.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Joy displays the name synced directly from the Shopify customer record — if it's wrong in the widget, the Shopify Customer record itself has the wrong name for that email. Fix it in **Shopify Admin → Customers**, not in Joy.

## 5. VIP Tiers

### Q27: If I change a VIP tier's threshold or calculation rule, what actually happens — and can it surprise customers?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕 (high operational impact — flagging even at low frequency)
**Standard answer:** Any threshold or calculation-rule change triggers a **full recalculation for every customer**, not just the tier being edited, because the whole tier list has to be re-scanned against the new rule. This can also re-fire **"Tier Achieved" emails to everyone** who now (re-)qualifies unless notifications are temporarily turned off before the change. On large customer bases (e.g. 300k+), the recalculation can take hours — schedule it off-peak. There's no built-in preview of how many customers will move before running it; export the current tier-member list first so you have a before/after comparison. A "tier change history" export isn't self-serve — it's a one-off backend pull on request.

### Q28: Do customers who were already in a VIP tier before a relaunch automatically get that tier's entry reward again?
**Frequency:** ~3 sessions 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** No — the entry reward only fires automatically for customers who newly reach the tier after the relaunch. Existing tier members from before the change don't get it retroactively unless **"Allow giving entry reward again after being downgraded"** applies to their specific case (a downgrade-then-re-reach scenario) — a straightforward "already there, rule changed" case still needs the team to manually trigger distribution for the affected list.

### Q29: Can a Free Shipping VIP-tier perk have a different order-value threshold per tier (e.g. Silver free-ship at $499, default at $950)?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No — the Free Shipping tier perk is unconditional (it waives the fee entirely whenever it applies); it doesn't support a spend-threshold variant that differs by tier. Logged as a feature request.

### Q30: We want a B2B "Trade Account" tier and a separate retail VIP tier, with product access gated to Trade only — how do these systems relate?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** B2B **Partner Tiers** and consumer **VIP Tiers** are two separate systems — Partner Tiers track a Shopify B2B Company's lifetime spend and don't auto-assign on B2B approval by default (needs a $0 threshold or a Shopify Flow trigger), and **Exclusive Products** (the product-restriction mechanism) only works within the standard VIP Tier system, not Partner Tiers. To gate retail products behind a "Trade Account Required" state, the practical path is a DTC **Exclusive tier** assigned via Shopify Flow when a customer is approved as a B2B company member — not the B2B Partner Tier itself.

## 6. Referral

### Q31: How does the referral flow actually work end-to-end, and when is each side rewarded?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Referrer shares a unique link → referee clicks, lands on the referral page, enters their email to claim (7-day cookie starts) → the **referee's** reward is issued immediately at that claim step. The **referrer's** reward stays pending until the referee places a qualifying order (same email as claim), following the shop's Paid/Fulfilled trigger setting. A referral code can also be entered directly in Shopify's standard discount box at checkout on any plan — the Shopify-Plus-only piece is only the *dedicated inline block* for entering it, not the ability to redeem a referral code at all.

### Q32: A referee placed a $0 (fully discounted) order — why didn't the referrer get credited?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** By design — anti-cheat requires the referee's order **subtotal to be greater than $0**; a fully-discounted order never qualifies the referrer for a reward, regardless of other settings.

## 7. Notifications

### Q33: The email logo looks blurry in loyalty notification emails.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** If the email is set to reuse the Shopify store logo directly, it's constrained to a small size (used elsewhere as a 32×32px favicon-style asset), which looks blurry when stretched. Fix: upload a dedicated, higher-resolution logo separately at **Settings → Email → Logo** instead of relying on "use shop logo."

### Q34: I want to apply one email template's styling (colors/fonts) to all my other notification templates at once.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not supported — each notification email is styled individually in the block editor; there's no bulk "apply this style everywhere" action today. Logged as a feature request.

### Q35: A customer is getting a points-expiration reminder email even though their points aren't actually close to expiring.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** Same pattern as prior weeks — check Joy's own send logs first. If Joy shows no record of sending that email (or the customer's Klaviyo profile shows no matching "Point Expiration" trigger event logged), the email is coming from the merchant's own Klaviyo flow/segment built on top of Joy's synced data, not from Joy itself. Ask the merchant to check their Klaviyo flows before assuming a Joy bug.

## 8. Integrations

### Q36: Klaviyo billing spiked unexpectedly — the merchant traced it to deleted contacts coming back as new billable profiles.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕 (high value — prevents a costly, confusing merchant complaint)
**Standard answer:** This is a Klaviyo-side behavior, not a Joy defect: Klaviyo doesn't retain any record of a profile once it's fully **deleted**. If Joy later sends any legitimate data update for that same email (e.g. a points/tier sync webhook), Klaviyo has no way to know it was ever deleted and creates a **brand-new, billable profile**. Klaviyo's own guidance is to **suppress** contacts instead of deleting them — a suppressed profile stays excluded from reactivation and won't come back this way. If a merchant asks how to permanently remove someone from Klaviyo without this risk, point them to Klaviyo's suppression feature rather than deletion.

### Q37: A Shopify Flow action ("Add point by variable") is returning a 404 error.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** In the resolved case, the cause was a **decimal presentment-amount** value being passed into the point-amount variable, which the action couldn't accept. Fix: add a `| round` filter to the Liquid variable feeding the action so it passes a whole number. If a merchant hits this error on a Flow using Joy's point-adjustment action, check whether the variable feeding it can carry decimals first before escalating as a platform bug.

### Q38: Does Joy support sending loyalty notifications over WhatsApp?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Not as a native automated channel — Joy can **capture SMS/WhatsApp marketing consent** from the customer (via the widget's Profile → Settings page, written to the Shopify customer record), but actually **sending** WhatsApp messages requires the merchant's own WhatsApp Business API/provider connected separately; Joy's role stops at capturing consent. (This is distinct from the LINE integration, which does push notifications directly.) Logged as a feature request for native WhatsApp sending.

## 9. Pricing / Billing

**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕 — **verify wording before repeating to a merchant**

If a chat agent said "SDK available from Advanced, full API is Enterprise-exclusive," that phrasing is inconsistent with the cached KB (which gates both together on Ultimate) — treat the KB as source of truth and flag this specific chat answer for a correction pass rather than repeating it.

### Q40: My monthly order count for the usage fee looks wrong compared to what I actually see in Shopify.

If there's a genuine mismatch (seen this week: a merchant with 62 real orders being counted against thousands), escalate for a manual recount and correction — don't assume it's explained by channel/order-type nuances without checking, since real discrepancies do occur.

## 11. Onsite Content / Loyalty Page

### Q42: Is there an alternate layout/template option for the loyalty page besides the default?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No — only the one default Joy Loyalty page template is offered; a merchant can rearrange sections (drag-drop) and edit text/content freely within it, but there's no alternate pre-built layout to switch to.

### Q43: A promotional block (e.g. Hero Banner) meant only for the loyalty page is showing up on every page, including the homepage.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** This usually means the block was actually added to the theme's shared **Header** section rather than the loyalty-page-only template — a Header-section block renders site-wide by design. Fix: remove it from the Header and add it as its own section/block scoped to the loyalty page template instead.

### Q44: The "Write a review" popup content, image, and CTA link need editing to match our branding.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Text content is editable at **Settings → Translations → Storefront blocks** (search "Write"). The popup **image** has no native upload field — changing it requires custom code from the team. The CTA button's **destination URL** likewise isn't self-serve — collect the desired link and escalate for the team to apply it.

## 12. Edge Cases / Bugs

### Q45: Store credit expiring on a set date expired at an unexpected time of day (e.g. 8 AM instead of end-of-day).
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Store credit expiration is always computed as **11:59 PM GMT+0**, which converts to a different local clock time depending on the merchant's own timezone — not a bug, a UTC-vs-local-time mismatch. If a merchant is confused by an early-morning expiry, explain the GMT+0 basis; there's no merchant-facing setting to change the expiration timezone today.

### Q46: Can a point-expiration date be retroactively changed for points customers have already earned?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🔁 recurring
**Standard answer:** No — a change to expiration rules only applies to points earned **after** the change; it can't be applied backward to already-earned points. If a merchant needs a blanket adjustment anyway (e.g. wants to preserve balances past a looming expiry), the practical workaround is exporting the affected customers' point balances before the expiry date and re-importing them after — the team can run this manually on request for a specific date.

### Q47: A staff account can't see/access Joy's Customers or Loyalty page inside Shopify Admin.
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** This is a standard **Shopify Admin permissions** issue, not a Joy setting — the staff account needs app access and customer-viewing permission granted under **Shopify Admin → Settings → Users and permissions**, the same as any other app.

### Q48: Can I safely build/test a new version of a live program without affecting the currently-running one?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Yes — **duplicate the program** first; the duplicate stays in draft and can be freely edited without touching the live original, then publish when ready. One thing to flag to the merchant: even while a program isn't "launched," loyalty content on the **Account Page (Loyalty Hub)** can still be visible live regardless of the storefront widget/page state — if the goal is a fully hidden unlaunched program, the account-page content needs to be handled too, not just the widget.

## 13. Reporting / Analytics

### Q49: What exact fields are available in Joy's self-serve vs. team-assisted customer/points exports, and is there an automated recurring report?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** Self-serve basic export (Customers grid) includes: email/name, phone, member status, point balance, current VIP tier + amount, referral link, birthday, last-updated timestamp. A team-assisted backend export can add: points earned/redeemed/expired/refunded/adjusted within a date range, all-time balance, all-time expiry date. Separately, some stores receive an **automatic monthly summary email** (Customer ID/Name, Email/Phone, Points Earned & Redeemed that month, Current Balance, Point Expiry Dates) — confirm with the team whether this is enabled for a given store rather than assuming it's universal, since this wasn't previously documented.

### Q50: Is there a metric for "when was our rewards program last used"?
**Frequency:** 1 session 🆕
**Standard answer:** No single "last used" timestamp exists at the program level. Point the merchant to the **Analytics** dashboard (redemption trends filtered by date range) for aggregate activity, or an individual customer's profile activity log for per-customer last-activity detail.
